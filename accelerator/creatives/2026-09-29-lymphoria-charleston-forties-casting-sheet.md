# Casting sheet: Lymphoria Charleston wedding (forties)

Filed 2026-09-29. For [[2026-09-29-lymphoria-charleston-wedding-forties-scene-tags]]. Replaces the operator's earlier sheet, which was written for the 63-year-old version. Ages follow the forties script; wardrobe follows the scene tags (every frame carries one bright color).

- **Nicole, narrator.** 44. Divorced eleven months after an eighteen-year marriage. Two kids in high school. Lives in Portland. At the start she is thirty pounds heavier than three years ago: a hard, tight shelf of a belly, a puffy face, a jawline gone soft. Keep her the same, recognizable woman from the mirror to the dance floor. Warm, dry, not bitter. Wardrobe: a coral jacket on the river walk, a loose T-shirt at the mirror, a pink blouse in the montage, a bright coral top for the close. The navy dress at the rehearsal dinner, the cream dress at the reception. A tangerine dress is the third in the suitcase.
- **Richard, her ex-husband.** Late forties. The script never says his age, so cast him a few years older than Nicole. Remarried six months after the divorce. Polished and a little smug at the rehearsal dinner, holding court. At the reception he stands six feet away with a martini and watching, then his face goes dead. One line: "I'm sorry."
- **Amber, Richard's new wife.** 30, fourteen years younger than Nicole. She used to work for Richard. She is pretty and trying too hard: at the rehearsal dinner she sucks in every time she stands and tugs her dress down over her hips. An emerald dress in the phone photo. Put her in a bright, tight dress at the rehearsal and a saturated color at the reception. Her one line, hissed: "You told me she was a trainwreck. You made a fool out of me." She walks out to the valet.
- **Maya, Nicole's sister.** Late forties, a little older than Nicole. A registered nurse for twenty years. Plainspoken, warm, blunt ("you're going to want to throw me out"). Teal scrubs and a canvas tote in the kitchen, and teal scrubs again in the doorway at week three. She carries the mechanism, so she needs to deliver a long speech naturally, like she's said it to patients before.
- **Jessica, Nicole's friend.** Forties. At the coffee shop she can barely look Nicole in the eye. At the rehearsal dinner she wears a lemon-yellow dress and hugs Nicole: "You look completely at peace."
- **Mark, Richard's brother.** Late forties to early fifties. Big, warm, the kind of man who hugs everyone. He delivers the public payoff at the reception: "She's doing a lot better than you are, Richard." Needs presence and good timing. Bright shirt or tie.
- **Tom and Sarah, the couple getting married.** Forties, both on a second marriage. Mostly background: glimpses at the rehearsal table and on the dance floor. No dialogue.
- **The kids, photo only.** Two teenagers in bright soccer jerseys, seen in the Portland montage photos. No dialogue.
- **Wedding guests, background.** Friends of twenty years at the rehearsal dinner, the terrace and the dance floor. Bright summer colors. They go quiet when Mark speaks. No dialogue.

Changed from the previous sheet: Nicole is 44, not 63. The marriage lasted eighteen years, not 35. The kids are in high school, not grown. Amber is 30, not 45, and used to work for Richard. Maya has nursed for twenty years, not thirty, and the geriatric ward is out. Tom and Sarah are on second marriages. Richard, Jessica, Mark, the kids and the guests have a casting age where the old sheet had none.
