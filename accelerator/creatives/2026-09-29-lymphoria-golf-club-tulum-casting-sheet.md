# Casting sheet: Lymphoria golf club v3 (Tulum, first-person song)

Filed 2026-09-29. For [[2026-09-29-lymphoria-golf-club-tulum-first-person-song-base]]. Replaces the operator's earlier sheet, which was written for the sister version (Patrice). Operator note: Joyce is a gray-haired silver fox, real sexy.

- **Becca, lead and narrator.** 63, Mark's wife. At the start she is 22 pounds heavier than at sixty: swollen stomach, puffy eyes, a jawline that has gone soft. Keep her the same, recognizable woman from the club and the clubhouse mirror, through Tulum and the six weeks at home, to the spring dinner. Wardrobe: her coat in the clubhouse bathroom, and a cover-up at the Tulum pool that she holds closed over her middle. At the dinner she wears the dress from Mark's retirement party, the same one shown on the bedroom door.
- **Joyce, the woman by the pool.** Mid-sixties, and she has to read as mid-sixties, because Becca's line is "you look so good for your age." A silver fox. Real gray or silver hair, worn loose or swept back, not dyed and not a wig. Sexy the way a confident woman is sexy: a visible waist, toned arms, a clean jawline, bare skin in a black one-piece. Not surgically tight, not filled; the look is natural. Big sunglasses, a book, a straw bag. She is amused and a little patronizing ("Honey"), unhurried, with nothing to prove. She should look like who Becca becomes. Appears only at the Tulum pool. She introduces the bottle there and gives it to Becca.
- **Mark, husband.** 66. Sold his software company two years ago, retired, golfs five days a week. Show the new money: a new watch, fitted golf shirts, whitened teeth, his phone face down on the counter. He appears in the bedroom doorway, behind the cart barn with the cart girl, passing the dress with his golf bag, and at the spring dinner, where he turns around surprised. There is no apology or reconciliation scene.
- **The cart girl.** 22. Drives the beverage cart. She appears with Mark behind the cart barn in a wide shot with no faces, laughing, his hand on her thigh. Later she works the bar at the spring dinner, holding glasses and looking over. Cast the same woman for both. She has no spoken name and no dialogue.
- **Dave, Steve and Tom, Mark's foursome.** Men in their sixties. They are named in the narration but have no dialogue. They appear teeing off in the setup b-roll and at the window table at the dinner, with Mark's back to the door. Dave is the first to see Becca walk in.
- **The friends' partners, setup b-roll only.** Dave's date at the Christmas party is noticeably younger, the girl from the pro shop. Steve's girlfriend is 26 and on his arm. They have no dialogue and appear nowhere else.
- **Club guests and resort guests, background.** Guests at the spring dinner turn when Becca enters. Guests on the loungers at the Tulum pool fill every chair but one. No dialogue.
