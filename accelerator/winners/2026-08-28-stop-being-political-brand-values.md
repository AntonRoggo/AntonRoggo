## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "we don't tone it down" - Values-first polarization: the brand accepts losing customers to remain outspoken about freedom, natural health, Christian traditional values, and anti-censorship beliefs.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "You have a good product, it's amazing, but like, I'm leaving because of your messaging." - Most-aware. The audience already knows the product and brand messaging; the creative filters alignment rather than educating or broadly persuading.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "we want to be the brand that's not afraid to say that stuff" - Stage 5. Differentiation is worldview, founder character, mission, and tribal alignment rather than a product mechanism or new promise.
4.   Tone of Voice: "if you don't like it, that's cool" - Founder as outspoken movement leader / insider; the register is defiant, polarizing, raw, combative, tribal, and unapologetic.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "being a healthy, independent individual" - Values-aligned consumers who prioritize freedom, natural health, independent thought, and brands willing to speak publicly.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "Christian traditional values" - The ad directly serves multiple aligned groups:
7.   "freedom fighters that inspired us" - Anti-lockdown and freedom-movement supporters.
8.   "not censoring information" - Free-speech and anti-censorship consumers.
9.   "not injecting chemicals into your body" - Natural-health and chemical-avoidant shoppers.
10.                Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
12.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
13.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "don't bring anything else into it" - The audience makes purchase decisions based on company messaging and values, and may leave or join brands for ideological reasons.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
14.                Pain Points - Physical: Not addressed.
15.                Pain Points - Emotional: "you weren't allowed to speak freely" - The emotional pains are censorship, pressure to self-silence, judgment, mandates, lockdowns, and frustration with chemical exposure.
16.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: "I'm leaving because of your messaging" - The brand faces customer rejection and social pressure to separate business from beliefs.
17.                Pain Points - Identity: "we want to stand up for what's right" - The identity conflict is whether to mute convictions for broader approval or remain an outspoken freedom-oriented brand.
18.                False Beliefs: "a lot of this stuff is like, not even political" - The creative reframes its messaging from unnecessary politics to common-sense health, freedom, speech, and moral values.
19.                Felt Solutions Tried: "just keep politics out of what you do" - The acknowledged alternative is toning down the message and selling only the product; the founder explicitly rejects it.
20.                New Mechanism Introduced: "one of the reasons I started this company was because of all the BS that went down during COVID" - The brand-origin mechanism is resistance to mandates, lockdowns, censorship, and chemical-heavy living.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
21.                Core Promise: "promoting a brand that has values that stand up for what's right" - Customers can buy premium natural skincare while joining and supporting a freedom-oriented mission.
22.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
23.                Proof Elements: "I started this company" - Proof is founder testimony, an explicit origin story, willingness to lose customers, named product Freedom Balm, and unfiltered value statements.
24.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "Buy some Freedom Balm." - Freedom Balm and premium F-Balm are offered; price, discount, bundle, subscription, guarantee, bonus, scarcity, and urgency are Not addressed.
25.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "Hit the link below, read some reviews, get some premium F-Balm. Join the mission" - The closing CTA combines product purchase and review-reading with explicit movement membership.
