## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "it's not a scam we're the real deal" - Skepticism-to-trust conversion through a real-world parking-lot encounter, transparent operations, product quality, and risk reversal.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "I keep seeing the ads" - Product-aware. The prospect recognizes the founder and ads but has not purchased because she rarely buys online.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "we have a special triple purification process" - Stage 4. The ad stacks founder access, real facilities, fast shipping, review volume, process differentiation, and a guarantee to overcome trust friction.
4.   Tone of Voice: "we are a real company I'm a real person" - Founder as candid peer and reassuring insider; the register is spontaneous, transparent, story-led, repetitive, and confidence-building.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "I just don't buy a lot of stuff online" - Skeptical ad viewers who are interested in F-Balm but hesitate to purchase from an online brand.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "and you're skeptical" - The ad also serves older prospects and people with dry, irritated, scarred, or visibly tired skin.
7.   "she's getting to actually try it out now" - Trial-oriented prospects who want proof before purchase.
8.   "it's dry or it's even like white" - People with visibly dry skin.
9.   "I wanna just rejuvenate my face" - Facial rejuvenation seekers.
10.                Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Gender (explicit + implicit): "this woman's walking the other way" - The encounter subject is explicitly female; the broader audience is otherwise addressed neutrally.
12.                Daily Occurrences: "apply it to moist skin for best results" - The named routine is facial application on moist skin; dry-hand use is also referenced.
13.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "I just don't buy a lot of stuff online" - The avatar sees ads, hesitates to shop online, and is encouraged to read reviews before trying.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
14.                Pain Points - Physical: "something that's irritating you with your skin" - The physical concerns include irritation, dryness, whitening, scars, stretch marks, and a face that needs rejuvenation.
15.                "on scars on stretch marks" - Scars and stretch marks.
16.                "it's dry or it's even like white" - Severe visible dryness.
17.                "bring some like life back into my face" - Dull or depleted facial appearance.
18.                Pain Points - Emotional: "and you're skeptical" - Distrust, fear of an online scam, and uncertainty about whether the brand is real are the emotional obstacles.
19.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.
20.                Pain Points - Identity: Not addressed.
21.                False Beliefs: "not a scam it's the real deal" - The ad corrects the belief that the online brand may be fake and that lower-quality bleached or deodorized tallow is equivalent.
22.                Felt Solutions Tried: Not addressed.
23.                New Mechanism Introduced: "special triple purification process" - Triple purification and premium, non-bleached, non-deodorized tallow explain the claimed quality difference.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
24.                Core Promise: "feel better look younger" - Visible and sensory skin improvement across rejuvenation, dryness, irritation, scars, and stretch marks.
25.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
26.                Proof Elements: "over 10,000 five star reviews now" - Proof includes the parking-lot encounter, 10,000 reviews, the founder's real identity, in-house facilities, purification, real shipping operations, and review-reading.
27.                "we really do make it um in our own facility" - Manufacturing proof.
28.                "honestly like three to five day shipping" - Operational fulfillment proof, not a results timeline.
29.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "you can uh try it risk free" - Risk-free trial backed by repeated money-back promises. Price, discount, bundle, subscription, bonus, scarcity, and urgency are Not addressed.
30.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "F-Balm at the link below" - Direct product-link CTA at the close, framed for skeptics as a risk-free trial after reviews and legitimacy proof.
