Source: "Top Ads All Time" client doc, filed into the vault 2026-08-28. Proven top performer for The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm; messaging extraction was already done in the source document and is preserved verbatim below.

So, check out the review of what this lady said and then click the link below to try it out for yourself.

## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "this guy is just unashamed of who he is, what his values are" - Third-party founder-character endorsement: radical honesty, Christian expression, natural health, and freedom make the company worth supporting.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "click the link below to try it out for yourself" - Product-aware. The product, review count, ingredients, benefits, founder values, and guarantee are all presented before trial.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "usually you're trying to tell the founder not to lie to the customers" - Stage 5. The ad differentiates through founder morality, worldview, third-party insider testimony, a memorable packaging act, and mission alignment alongside product proof.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I just do a little bit of marketing for this company" - Marketing insider as amazed third-party witness; the register is candid, admiring, values-forward, conversational, and anti-corporate.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "support a company like that" - Values-driven natural-skincare shoppers who want to support an openly Christian, freedom-oriented, honest founder.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "people to be healthy and free" - The narrower groups are Christian-aligned buyers, freedom-oriented consumers, ingredient-conscious skincare shoppers, and people with dryness or visible aging concerns.
7.   "gets rid of the bags under your eyes" - Under-eye shoppers.
8.   "reduces fine lines" - Anti-aging shoppers.
9.   Age (explicit + implicit): "reduces fine lines" - Explicit age: Not addressed. Fine lines and under-eye bags imply adults concerned with visible aging.
10.                Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
12.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "Five ingredients that you could make from home." - The avatar prefers simple, natural, recognizable ingredients and supports businesses based on beliefs.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
13.                Pain Points - Physical: "bags under your eyes" - The physical pains are under-eye bags, fine lines, and skin needing hydration.
14.                "reduces fine lines" - Fine lines.
15.                "hydrates your skin" - Lack of hydration, implied by the promised relief.
16.                Pain Points - Emotional: "usually you're trying to tell the founder not to lie to the customers" - Distrust of dishonest founders and companies is the emotional problem.
17.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.
18.                Pain Points - Identity: "not afraid to say what his beliefs are" - The identity tension is whether a company hides its beliefs or openly lives them.
19.                False Beliefs: "not to lie to the customers to sell their product" - The ad challenges the belief that founders must exaggerate, conceal beliefs, or act corporately to sell successfully.
20.                Felt Solutions Tried: Not addressed.
21.                New Mechanism Introduced: "It absorbs easily for long-lasting hydration." - The product mechanism combines simple edible ingredients and tallow vitamins with easy absorption for sustained moisture.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
22.                Core Promise: "hydrates your skin, gets rid of the bags under your eyes, reduces fine lines" - Hydrated, smoother-looking skin from a simple natural product while supporting a values-aligned company.
23.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
24.                Proof Elements: "Over 10,000 five-star reviews on this product" - Proof includes a marketer-insider endorsement, a referenced review screenshot, the Jesus-figurine story, ingredient specifics, vitamin callouts, review scale, and founder honesty.
25.                "I'll leave a screenshot of it uh at the end" - Displayed customer review proof is promised.
26.                "vitamins A, D, E, K, and B12" - Nutrient callout.
27.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "30-day money-back guarantee on everything" - A 30-day guarantee creates a risk-free trial. Price, discount, bundle, subscription, bonus, scarcity, and urgency are Not addressed.
28.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "click the link below to try it out for yourself" - A direct trial CTA framed as both product evaluation and support for an unashamed values-based company, after the review screenshot.
