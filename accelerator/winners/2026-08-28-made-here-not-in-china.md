## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "We don't make our stuff in China. We make it here." - Local legitimacy and transparent manufacturing: the founder visually proves the brand is a real Canadian operation rather than an imported reseller.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "Hit the link below and give it a try if you have not used it yet." - Product-aware. The brand, product, headquarters, and reviews are central, while the ask targets people who know of it but have not yet purchased.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "This is where we do it." - Stage 4. Differentiation comes from behind-the-scenes proof, local manufacturing, family ownership, sourcing, and brand values.
4.   Tone of Voice: "Hey, I'm Ben, I'm the founder of The Tallow Truth." - Founder-insider speaker in a transparent, informal, patriotic, reassuring, warehouse-tour register.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "People are seeing products shipped from China." - Tallow shoppers who care whether a product is genuinely made and shipped by a real Canadian company.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "We are the Pro-Freedom Tallow Brand" - The creative also serves values-aligned freedom supporters and shoppers seeking a multi-use balm for face, body, or dry skin.
7.   "for your face, for your body, for dry skin" - Multi-use skincare buyers.
8.   "follow us on Instagram if you wanna stay caught up" - Brand followers interested in the company and its values.
9.   Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
10.                Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Daily Occurrences: "use for your face, for your body, for dry skin" - General face and body application is named; no specific daily time is stated.
12.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "follow us on Instagram if you wanna stay caught up" - The audience is invited to follow brand activity and values, browse the product range, and support a family-owned Canadian maker.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
13.                Pain Points - Physical: "dry skin" - Dry skin is the only specific physical issue named; the phrase all kinds of issues remains nonspecific.
14.                Pain Points - Emotional: "I want to clear up some confusion." - Confusion and uncertainty about product origin and legitimacy are the emotional frictions.
15.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.
16.                Pain Points - Identity: Not addressed.
17.                False Beliefs: "this is real, this is legit" - The ad corrects the suspicion that the brand may be an imported, opaque, or unreal online operation.
18.                Felt Solutions Tried: Not addressed.
19.                New Mechanism Introduced: "Grass-fed tallow balm" - The closest product mechanism is grass-fed tallow plus local in-house production; no biological causal explanation is supplied.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
20.                Core Promise: "use for your face, for your body, for dry skin, for all kinds of issues" - A legitimate, locally made, multi-use tallow balm for face, body, and dry-skin needs.
21.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
22.                Proof Elements: "Over a thousand five-star reviews now." - Proof includes review volume, founder identity, visible products and boxes, headquarters, manufacturing, packing, and shipping operations.
23.                "we got boxes, we got product" - Physical behind-the-scenes evidence.
24.                "We make the stuff here and we ship the stuff from here." - Operational origin proof.
25.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): Not addressed.
26.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "please hit the link below" - Direct website CTA repeated after product proof, with Instagram offered as a lower-commitment brand-following path.
