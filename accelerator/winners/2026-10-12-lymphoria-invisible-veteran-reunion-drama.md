# Lymphoria winner: "Invisible," the veteran at his unit reunion (drama)

Filed 2026-10-12. The operator marked it a performance winner and wrote [[drama-ad-guidelines]] next to it. This note is the why. The full script is in [[story-010-invisible-veteran-reunion]].

## Why it won

- **The wound scars.** He is invisible in a room full of his own people. His platoon and his wife look right past him, and his identity (infantry, two tours) makes it worse.
- **Three hooks, one room, one night.** Version 1 is the scene (Rachel jumps on Jake, "I'm right here, Brennan"). Version 2 is someone else's eyes (Sutter can't find him, "what the hell are you feeding him?"). Version 3 is the pure ego hit ("What'd you do, supply?" / "You don't look it"). Each one ends on Rachel's reaction, and all three lead into the parking lot.
- **The guide is tied to the wound.** The helper is the man his wife was looking at. Cole tells him to fuck off first, so the help lands harder.
- **The mechanism is said while moving.** It is delivered in a garage gym between kettlebell swings, with a body test the viewer can try (the thumb dent stays), plus the dismissive authority ("The VA told me the same shit").
- **A real clock with the same people.** Veterans Day is six weeks out, and the whole platoon will be there.
- **One witness per week.** The ring slides loose. The 26-year-old manager asks, "You lose weight, Cole?" Rachel stares. The young guys step aside. The platoon shirt fits.
- **Every hook flips in the same hall.** Sutter spots him first. The young vet says "Yeah. I can tell." Rachel looks at Cole, not Jake.
- **Blunt dialogue, no explained feelings.** "Yeah. I saw you looking." "Let 'em look." Nobody gives a speech.
- **The close names the viewer's wound.** "They trained you to be the most dangerous man in any room... nobody sees you. Not even your wife." Then the offer.

## Reusable moves

- The thumb-dent test as the mechanism's proof on screen.
- A title-card countdown to a named event.
- The bottle moves from hidden behind the shaving cream to out on the counter next to the dog tags. That shows the arc without a word.
