# Winner: $57 face cream is mostly water

Source: "Top Ads All Time" client doc, filed into the vault 2026-08-28. Proven top performer for The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm; messaging extraction was already done in the source document and is preserved verbatim below.

## Script (verbatim)

I just bought a popular face cream at the Shoppers Drug Mart and I checked to see what the first ingredient is. And it's water, which is actually pretty harmless. But this thing cost over 57 dollars. And when I asked ChatGPT what percentage of water was in this cream, it said 70 to 80 percent. So just know that when you buy these things and maybe they do work, I don't actually use this stuff so I don't know, but you're paying for 70 to 80 percent water. And you're paying a lot more than you do for the ingredients that are in the F-Balm tallow. So sometimes people say why is it expensive and it's like well for starters it's not 70 to 80 percent water. The first ingredient is beef tallow, there's organic olive oil, there's raw honey, there's things that deliver benefits to your skin. And that's what you pay for. We make it in house, we use premium ingredients. It's not 70 to 80 percent water. So if you want to try this stuff that's fine. But if you're worried about spending 40 to 50 dollars on the F-Balm just know that it's real ingredients that deliver real results. We now have over 5,000 reviews. If you want to try this stuff instead of something that's 70 to 80 percent water, hit the link below and give it a try. Read the reviews. I don't think you'll be disappointed. If you don't like it we'll give you a money back guarantee.

## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "this thing cost over 57 dollars" - Ingredient-value exposé: expensive conventional face cream is mostly water, while F-Balm charges for active, recognizable ingredients.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "if you're worried about spending 40 to 50 dollars on the F-Balm" - Product-aware. The creative directly handles F-Balm price resistance by comparing it with a known face-cream alternative.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "The first ingredient is beef tallow" - Stage 4. Differentiation comes from a comparative ingredient audit, price defense, in-house manufacturing, reviews, and guarantee.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I just bought a popular face cream at the Shoppers Drug Mart" - Founder as consumer investigator / skeptical insider in a plainspoken, comparative, demonstrative, and mildly contrarian register.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "worried about spending 40 to 50 dollars on the F-Balm" - Price-conscious skincare shoppers evaluating whether F-Balm justifies its cost versus conventional face cream.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "buy these things" - The ad also targets conventional cream buyers, ingredient-label readers, and shoppers who equate a higher-priced formula with better value.
7.   "I checked to see what the first ingredient is" - Ingredient-conscious shoppers.
8.   "maybe they do work" - Open-minded conventional-product users.
9.   Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
10.                Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
12.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "I checked to see what the first ingredient is" - The avatar buys retail face cream, compares price, inspects ingredient order, and may use ChatGPT to evaluate formulations.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
13.                Pain Points - Physical: Not addressed.
14.                Pain Points - Emotional: "why is it expensive" - Price anxiety and the feeling of paying too much for diluted skincare are the emotional frictions.
15.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.
16.                Pain Points - Identity: Not addressed.
17.                False Beliefs: "you're paying for 70 to 80 percent water" - The ad challenges the belief that expensive conventional cream contains proportionally valuable ingredients, and that F-Balm is overpriced by comparison.
18.                Felt Solutions Tried: "a popular face cream at the Shoppers Drug Mart" - A conventional retail face cream is the only alternative solution directly addressed; the speaker explicitly says he does not use it.
19.                New Mechanism Introduced: "things that deliver benefits to your skin" - The mechanism is concentration of recognizable ingredients - beef tallow, olive oil, and honey - rather than 70-80% water.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
20.                Core Promise: "real ingredients that deliver real results" - More ingredient value and claimed skin results for the spend than a water-heavy face cream.
21.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
22.                Proof Elements: "We now have over 5,000 reviews." - Proof includes the purchased comparison cream, its first ingredient, a ChatGPT water estimate, F-Balm ingredient callouts, in-house production, and review volume.
23.                "it's water" - Ingredient-label comparison.
24.                "we use premium ingredients" - Premium-input claim.
25.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "spending 40 to 50 dollars on the F-Balm" - F-Balm is positioned in the $40-$50 range and backed by a money-back guarantee. Discount, bundle, subscription, bonus, scarcity, urgency, and guarantee window are Not addressed.
26.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "hit the link below and give it a try. Read the reviews." - Direct trial CTA framed as an informed alternative to water-heavy cream, followed by review-reading and a guarantee.
