## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "there's three of us in the house" - Unscripted household-wide loyalty: one family uses multiple products, repurchases, shares them, anticipates launches, and aligns with the brand's values.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "You're one of our best customers." - Most-aware. The customer already buys repeatedly, knows multiple products, plans future purchases, and advocates to family.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "everything we've tried so far we really like a lot" - Stage 4. Differentiation comes from authentic repeat-customer proof, product-family adoption, household penetration, gifting, launch anticipation, and value alignment.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I'm just calling you uh to say thanks." - Customer and founder in a warm peer-to-peer phone call; the register is spontaneous, grateful, informal, friendly, and unpolished.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "between the three of us, we, we use it a lot" - Natural-product households seeking a trusted range that different family members can use repeatedly.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "my uh 20-year-old son uses it like stink" - The creative surfaces several household segments:
7.   "he loves going all natural" - Young natural-lifestyle men.
8.   "I give some to my sister-in-law" - Family sharers and word-of-mouth advocates.
9.   "we're gonna order the, that tick balm" - Early adopters watching product launches.
10.                Age (explicit + implicit): "20-year-old son" - A 20-year-old male is explicit; the mother's and other household members' ages are Not addressed.
11.                Gender (explicit + implicit): "my uh 20-year-old son" - The testimonial speaker is a woman and her son is male; three household users are referenced but the third gender is not stated.
12.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
13.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "he loves going all natural and stuff" - The household prioritizes natural products, uses tallow frequently, repurchases, tests variants, gifts products, and follows new launches.
14.                "this Friday, I think we're gonna order" - Planned near-term purchase.
15.                "I give some to my sister-in-law" - Product sharing.

18.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.

20.                False Beliefs: Not addressed.
21.                Felt Solutions Tried: "I started out using just the, the F-Balm" - The customer has tried F-Balm, Face Whip, unscented and summer-scent variants, the sun product, and plans to try tick balm.
22.                New Mechanism Introduced: Not addressed.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
23.                Core Promise: "It's, it's a really great product. I, I love everything." - A trusted, natural product family that can satisfy multiple household users across variants and use cases.
24.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
25.                Proof Elements: "everything we've tried so far we really like a lot" - Proof is a repeat customer's live phone feedback, three-person household use, a 20-year-old user's enthusiasm, cross-product adoption, gifting, and future-purchase intent.
26.                "we love it" - Direct household endorsement.
27.                "You're one of our best customers." - Repeat-purchase credibility.
28.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "I'll send you some um some S-Balm and some more of the face whip for free." - The caller offers free products as a thank-you. The customer plans to order tick balm on Friday; no public price, discount, bundle, subscription, guarantee, scarcity, or urgency is presented.
