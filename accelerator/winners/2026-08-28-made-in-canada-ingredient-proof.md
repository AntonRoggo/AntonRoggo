# Winner: Made in Canada ingredient proof

Source: "Top Ads All Time" client doc, filed into the vault 2026-08-28. Proven top performer for The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm; messaging extraction was already done in the source document and is preserved verbatim below.

## Script (verbatim)

F-Balm is made in Canada. Not in China, not in the US, Canada. F-Balm is made from grass-fed, grass-finished, all-natural ingredients. Grass-fed tallow, organic olive oil, Canadian honey, Canadian beeswax, organic frankincense, and freedom. It's a very high-quality, incredible product. So smooth. You only need a tiny little pea-sized amount. It melts like butter and it's the best consistency. I'm obsessed with it because it makes for a great base under your makeup, giving you the glowiest looking skin. The ingredients are sourced from Canadian farmers, which is incredible. I love shopping products that are made in Canada, and I know you do too.

## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "F-Balm is made in Canada. Not in China, not in the US, Canada." - Canadian origin and ingredient proof paired with sensorial texture and a makeup-ready glow.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "F-Balm is made in Canada." - Product-aware. The product is named immediately and the creative focuses on origin, ingredients, texture, and use rather than category education.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "grass-fed, grass-finished, all-natural ingredients" - Stage 3. Differentiation is built from ingredient specificity, Canadian sourcing, texture, small dose, and makeup compatibility.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I'm obsessed with it" - Enthusiastic customer / UGC-style speaker in a bright, patriotic, sensory, beauty-oriented register.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "a great base under your makeup" - Canadian skincare and makeup users seeking a natural balm that delivers a smooth, glowing finish.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "I love shopping products that are made in Canada" - The narrower groups are Canadian-made shoppers, ingredient-conscious natural-product buyers, and makeup wearers.
7.   "You only need a tiny little pea-sized amount." - Minimal-dose, value-conscious users.
8.   "giving you the glowiest looking skin" - Glow-focused beauty shoppers.
9.   Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
10.                Gender (explicit + implicit): "under your makeup" - Explicit gender: Not addressed. Makeup use is the only gender-coded cue and does not establish a specific gender.
11.                Daily Occurrences: "a great base under your makeup" - The product is positioned as a pre-makeup base applied in a pea-sized amount.
12.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "I love shopping products that are made in Canada" - The avatar shops local Canadian products, wears makeup, and prefers all-natural ingredients.
