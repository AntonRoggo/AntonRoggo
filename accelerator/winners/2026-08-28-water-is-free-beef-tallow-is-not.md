# Winner: Water is free; beef tallow is not

Source: "Top Ads All Time" client doc, filed into the vault 2026-08-28. Proven top performer for The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm; messaging extraction was already done in the source document and is preserved verbatim below.

Re-cut of: [[2026-08-28-57-face-cream-is-mostly-water]] (same ingredient-value-math angle, re-cut)

## Script (verbatim)

This popular face cream, I just asked ChatGPT, is 60 to 80 percent water. It was over $50. Now, this is also 48 grams of product. And so people happily pay for that and I haven't used that product. I don't know, maybe it's great. The F-Balm is less than that and it's a bigger container. It's a 2 ounce, 60 gram. And the first ingredient is not water, it's beef tallow. So we don't charge you for 60 to 80 percent water, we charge you for the ingredients that are in the tin. If you want to try this stuff and see the difference, hit the link below. We've got over 5,000 five-star reviews and give it a try. If you want to try this stuff, that's cool too. I don't know, maybe it works. Um, but water's free. Uh, beef tallow is not free. So we do have to charge for it, but I think you will experience the difference if you give this a try. Hit the link below. I'll see you there.

## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "water's free. Uh, beef tallow is not free." - Value-by-concentration comparison: F-Balm costs less per larger container and charges for tallow rather than a formula that is mostly water.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "The F-Balm is less than that and it's a bigger container." - Product-aware. The product, price relationship, size, ingredient difference, reviews, and trial ask are directly compared with a conventional alternative.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "the first ingredient is not water, it's beef tallow" - Stage 4. Differentiation is built from price-size math, ingredient concentration, a physical competitor comparison, review volume, and experiential trial.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I don't know, maybe it's great." - Founder as fair-minded consumer investigator in a casual, comparative, skeptical, humorous, and non-dogmatic register.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "people happily pay for that" - Value-conscious face-cream shoppers comparing F-Balm with a $50-plus conventional product.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "If you want to try this stuff and see the difference" - The narrower segments are conventional cream buyers, ingredient-conscious shoppers, and prospects hesitant about tallow pricing.
7.   "60 to 80 percent water" - Shoppers concerned about diluted formulas.
8.   Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
9.   Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
10.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
11.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "people happily pay for that" - The avatar already spends more than $50 on popular face cream and compares size and ingredients before switching.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
12.                Pain Points - Physical: Not addressed.
13.                Pain Points - Emotional: "we do have to charge for it" - Price resistance and concern about overpaying for filler are the emotional barriers.
14.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: Not addressed.
15.                Pain Points - Identity: Not addressed.
16.                False Beliefs: "we don't charge you for 60 to 80 percent water" - The ad challenges the belief that the more expensive conventional cream offers better value and that F-Balm's price lacks justification.
17.                Felt Solutions Tried: "This popular face cream" - A popular conventional face cream is the only alternative named; the speaker explicitly says he has not used it.
18.                New Mechanism Introduced: "the first ingredient is not water, it's beef tallow" - The mechanism is concentration and ingredient substance rather than water-heavy formulation; no biological mechanism is stated.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
19.                Core Promise: "I think you will experience the difference" - A noticeable experiential difference from a larger, less-expensive, tallow-first product.
20.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
21.                Proof Elements: "over 5,000 five-star reviews" - Proof includes a competitor's price, reported water percentage, exact sizes, first-ingredient comparison, lower-price claim, and review volume.
22.                "48 grams of product" - Competitor size evidence.
23.                "It's a 2 ounce, 60 gram." - F-Balm size evidence.
24.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "It was over $50." - The competitor is over $50; F-Balm is said to cost less and be larger. Exact F-Balm price, discount, bundle, subscription, guarantee, bonus, scarcity, and urgency are Not addressed.
25.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "If you want to try this stuff and see the difference, hit the link below." - Direct comparison-led trial CTA, repeated at the close after reviews and the value explanation.
