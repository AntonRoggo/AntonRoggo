# Winner: Canadian business / government accountability

Source: "Top Ads All Time" client doc, filed into the vault 2026-08-28. Proven top performer for The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm; messaging extraction was already done in the source document and is preserved verbatim below.

## Script (verbatim)

You might have seen that Crown Royal is moving one of their businesses to the United States, moving one of their manufacturing plants to the United States. And here's the thing, I don't blame them. Doug Ford can get up there and pour out a bottle of Crown Royal and say shame on you for leaving the states. But I'm a Canadian business owner. We make stuff in Canada, and Doug Ford and the liberal government have made it hard for businesses to succeed. We make this stuff here. We source stuff from Canadian farmers, and we hire Canadians, and we want to have a business here. But the truth is, our liberal government and Doug Ford have made it hard for businesses to stay here. They charge tax when we buy the stuff for the products. They charge tax when we sell the products. They charge tax when we pay our employees. They charge tax on the business the prof... on the profits the business make. And it goes on and on and on. So I don't blame Crown Royal for leaving. I've thought of leaving. The reality is we want to make these products in Canada. These are premium beef tallow skincare products, but the liberal government has made it hard for businesses to succeed. Doug Ford, he's basically a liberal now too. He's made it hard for businesses to succeed. We want to stay here, but we need to hold our government accountable, and our brand, The Tallowed Truth, is not afraid to call them out on this. We make products here. If you want to support us, hit the link below and check out our products. But most of all, hold our government accountable so that more brands like us can stay here, can grow, can make premium products, and can hire Canadian people and can build Canadian businesses. If you want to support The Tallowed Truth, hit the link below, and I'll see you there. We got soap, we got the F-Balm. Great skincare stuff. Hit the link below and I'll see you there.

## Messaging extraction (verbatim)

**STRATEGIC LAYER**
1.   Angle: "hold our government accountable so that more brands like us can stay here" - Buying from The Tallowed Truth is framed as economic and political support for Canadian manufacturing, jobs, and business survival.
2.   Market Awareness Stage: "If you want to support us, hit the link below and check out our products." - Product-aware. The persuasion assumes interest in the brand and asks for support rather than explaining tallow or introducing a new category solution.
3.   Sophistication Stage: "our brand, The Tallowed Truth, is not afraid to call them out on this" - Stage 5. Differentiation is primarily identity, worldview, and public advocacy rather than product mechanism or a stronger skincare promise.
4.   Tone of Voice: "I'm a Canadian business owner." - Founder-activist / insider speaker in a confrontational, patriotic, frustrated, politically explicit register.

**AVATAR LAYER**
5.   Primary Avatar: "support The Tallowed Truth" - Politically aligned Canadians who want domestic companies to manufacture locally, hire Canadians, and challenge government policy.
6.   Sub-Avatar: "Canadian business owner" - The ad speaks to business owners, taxpayers, Canadian-made shoppers, and voters concerned with jobs and corporate relocation.
7.   "hire Canadian people" - Employment-focused supporters.
8.   "hold our government accountable" - Politically engaged voters.
9.   Age (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
10.                Gender (explicit + implicit): Not addressed.
11.                Daily Occurrences: Not addressed.
12.                Habits / Lifestyle Callouts: "If you want to support us" - The avatar buys Canadian-made goods, follows public affairs, and treats purchasing as support for local jobs and values.

**PSYCHOLOGY LAYER**
13.                Pain Points - Physical: Not addressed.
14.                Pain Points - Emotional: "made it hard for businesses to succeed" - Frustration, anger, and discouragement about taxes and government policy are the emotional pains.
15.                Pain Points - Social / Relational: "more brands like us can stay here, can grow" - The community-level pain is businesses leaving Canada and the loss of local growth and Canadian jobs.
16.                Pain Points - Identity: "We make products here." - The identity tension is wanting to remain a Canadian manufacturer while feeling pushed toward leaving.
17.                False Beliefs: "I don't blame Crown Royal for leaving." - The creative challenges the idea that companies leave only from disloyalty; taxes and policy are presented as the real pressure.
18.                Felt Solutions Tried: Not addressed.
19.                New Mechanism Introduced: "They charge tax when we buy the stuff for the products." - Repeated taxation across inputs, sales, payroll, and profits is presented as the causal mechanism driving businesses away.

**PROMISE & OFFER LAYER**
20.                Core Promise: "can stay here, can grow, can make premium products, and can hire Canadian people" - Support for the brand helps sustain Canadian products, jobs, growth, and business presence.
21.                Results Timeline: Not addressed.
22.                Proof Elements: "We source stuff from Canadian farmers, and we hire Canadians" - Proof comes from the founder's first-person business experience, Canadian sourcing, employment, and local manufacturing claims.
23.                Offer Structure (price, discount, bundle, guarantee, urgency): "We got soap, we got the F-Balm." - The product range is named, but price, discount, bundle, subscription, guarantee, bonuses, scarcity, and urgency are Not addressed.
24.                CTA Copy + Positioning: "If you want to support The Tallowed Truth, hit the link below" - A direct purchase CTA framed primarily as political and economic support, repeated at the close with product examples.
