## The guidelines (operator's text, verbatim)

Paste into a new chat with the winning example, the story idea and the ICP.

**THE GOAL**

- A short film that hits the viewer's ego or deepest fear in the first three seconds, then pays it off.
- The product is the fix, not the topic.

**THE EMOTIONAL TRIGGER**

- Find what this person fears becoming. Put them in that moment, in front of the person they'd least want watching.
- Men: can't protect her, outdone by someone weaker, invisible to his own people, fired, his kid sees it, his wife looks at another man.
- Women: the beauty she was known for is gone and people say it, her husband stops wanting her, her friends laugh about her.
- It has to scar, not embarrass. If it wouldn't replay at 3 AM for years, it's too small.
- Their identity makes it worse: the soldier his platoon doesn't recognize, the security guard who can't stop a fight.

**HOOKS**

- Open on the hit, never the setup.
- One short line says it out loud. The line people repeat in the comments.
- Three angles on the same moment: the scene, the pure ego hit, someone else's eyes.
- Each hook works alone and leads into the same first scene.

**THE STORY**

- Real places from real life and real viral stories. No stock settings.
- Logic first: why it happens, why they meet again, why he can win now.
- A clock: an event in six weeks with the same people.
- Plan the flips first. Every hit in the hooks gets reversed in the payoff.
- Change the pace every scene. Loud, quiet, fast, slow.

**CHARACTERS**

- Build every character from the wound.
- The lead: name, age, job, one flaw that drives him. Same body after, never skinny.
- The person who matters: her own voice, says the thing that hurts, someone the viewer is scared to lose.
- The one who outdoes him: weaker, younger or lower status. Or the man his wife looks at.
- The helper: someone he'd really meet, used to be bigger than him, ideally tied to the wound.
- The witnesses: whoever laughed comes back for the payoff.
- Cast everyone with ethnicity, age and look. Keep the antagonist in the lead's community.

**HOW THEY TALK**

- Like real people in that community. Short, blunt, swearing where it fits.
- They say the cruel thing plainly and never explain feelings.
- Fights hit money, the body and the marriage in one breath.
- No speeches about who he used to be, no AI voice, no jokes, no slurs, never "sex."
- The register:
  - "I'm right here, Brennan."
  - "Yeah. I saw you looking."
  - "He had his hands on me. And you said please."
  - "You SECURITY, Terrell. That's the whole damn job!"

**THE HOLD**

- Fallout with the person who matters.
- The helper explains the mechanism while they're moving: gym, mats, truck.
- Doubt at home.
- The weeks: one visible win and one person who notices, every week.
- The payoff: same place, same people, the flips land.
- The close: a short VO that names the viewer's wound, or the next guy asking what he's doing. Then the offer.

**DON'T CHANGE**

- The mechanism: the body's drain has no pump, waste backs up and soaks into fat like a sponge, so the gym makes you stronger but never smaller. Only the cause line changes per ICP.
- Half a dropper morning and night. Sweet like honey, light golden.
- 70% off, 30-day guarantee, "button below."

**DELIVERY**

- ClickUp page like the winning example. No em dashes, no emojis.
- Within 10 percent of the winner's length.
- The hold's first scene works after all three hooks.
- Under the page: a short explanation, then suggestions with reasons.
