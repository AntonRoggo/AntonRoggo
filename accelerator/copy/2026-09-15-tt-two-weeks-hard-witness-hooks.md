# Two weeks without telling anyone: 20 hooks with a specific witness

Base: "I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling anyone, to see if anybody would notice." Structure per [[2026-09-15-experiment-on-a-hard-witness-hook]]. Each hook swaps "anyone" for a specific witness whose trait makes noticing hard.

Witness types are drawn from who actually notices in the reviews: husband ("your skin looks amazing, what are you doing?"), brother (Botox), other women, family, daughters and friends, physiotherapist, someone who saw a photo on social media.

---

## The oblivious witness (if they notice, it's big)

1. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my husband, who hasn't noticed a haircut in twenty years, to see if he'd notice.

2. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my husband of 31 years, to see if he'd notice anything at all.

3. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my brother, who has never once commented on my appearance, to see if he'd say anything.

4. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my mom, who has never complimented my skin in my life, to see if she would.

5. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my three sons, to see if a single one of them would notice.

## The expert witness (if they can't tell, it's real)

6. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my daughter, who's a makeup artist, to see if she could tell what changed.

7. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my esthetician, who's been doing my facials for ten years, to see if she'd notice.

8. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my dermatologist, to see what she'd say at my next appointment.

9. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my hairdresser, who sees my face in a mirror under bright lights every six weeks.

10. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my sister, who notices everything, to see how long it would take her.

11. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my best friend, who's had Botox for years, to see if she'd ask what I'm doing.

## The crowd (many eyes, no warning)

12. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my coworkers, who see my face on Zoom every single day, to see if anyone would say something.

13. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my book club, six women who notice a new lipstick, to see if anyone would ask.

14. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling the women at church, to see if anyone would ask what I'm using.

15. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks, then posted a photo with no filter and no caption, to see if anyone would comment.

## The occasion (one room, one night)

16. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks before my 40-year high school reunion, without telling anyone, to see if anybody would ask.

17. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks before a wedding I knew my ex would be at, to see if he'd notice.

18. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks before my daughter's wedding, without telling her, to see if she'd notice in the photos.

## The payoff in the hook (pattern interrupt)

19. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my brother. He asked if I'd had Botox.

20. I put beef fat on my face every night for two weeks without telling my husband. On day fourteen he said, "Your skin looks amazing. What are you doing?"
