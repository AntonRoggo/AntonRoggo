## SCRIPT

Which one actually works on fine lines? Beef tallow, or the moisturizer you already own?

They both promise to soften your lines, but NOT in the same way.

Your moisturizer follows the drugstore formula. First ingredient, water. 70 to 80 percent of the jar. It sits on top of your skin and evaporates.

But here's the part most people miss. After a certain age, your skin stops making its own oil. And water doesn't replace oil.

Beef tallow, on the other hand, takes a very different approach. It's almost bio-identical to the oil your skin stopped making, so your skin absorbs it instead of wearing it. The Tallowed Truth's F-Balm is five ingredients: tallow, olive oil, honey, beeswax, frankincense. Made in-house in Ontario.

One reviewer with deep smoker lines above her lip wrote, "they are diminishing." Another, three days in: "Lines look softer."

That just shows how far the tallow gets. Under the lines, not on top of them.

So which one are you using?
