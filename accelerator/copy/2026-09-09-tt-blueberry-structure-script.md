## Cold open option B: reply to a Botox comment

Format: comment bubble on screen, 54-year-old woman on camera replies. The reply replaces sentence 1 and hands off to sentence 2. Keep the reply to three short sentences max, the reference's cold open is one. Comment text stays under 10 words so it reads in two seconds.

## Deviations from the reference, on purpose

- Beat 17 and 18 swap the bonus and deadline for the guarantee and review count. The client file is explicit: guarantee is the only offer mechanic this account has ever run, no scarcity, no urgency. Matching the reference here would break the brand's proven close.
- Beat 16 carries the day 1 / week 1 results from the existing water ad script (HOLD 8), since the reference has no results beat and TT always has one.
