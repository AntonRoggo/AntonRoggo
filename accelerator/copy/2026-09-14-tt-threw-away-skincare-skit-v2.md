# "Why are you throwing away all your skincare?" skit, v2

Rebuilt on the script-only breakdown of the Tao Clean two-person ad. Engine: each answer creates the next question. Chain: why throw it away → this is all I use now → that's it? → what's in it → why does your skin look like that. Second reveal (people think she had Botox) is more surprising than the first (one tin). Pain beats target visible aging per operator, 2026-09-14.

Every symptom and benefit word checked against the raw review export on 2026-09-13. Sources in the beat table.

Supersedes [[2026-09-12-tt-threw-away-skincare-skit-adaptation]].

## SCRIPT

A: Why are you throwing away all your skincare?

B: This. This is all I use now.

A: That's it? That's the only thing you have left?

B: That's it.

A: It looks kind of basic. Do I need it?

B: Are you still doing anything for the fine lines?

A: A little bit. Okay.

B: Yeah. And the bags under your eyes?

A: Wow. Okay.

B: How many anti-aging creams are on your shelf right now?

A: Mmm.

B: Twelve? And every single one is mostly water.

A: Yeah. The water shelf. Okay girl, I get it. So what's in it?

B: Five things. Beef tallow first. Then olive oil, honey, beeswax, frankincense. That's the whole list.

A: Beef tallow. Like, fat.

B: Like fat. After a certain age your skin stops making its own oil. That's the lines. That's the dryness. Tallow is almost the same stuff, so your skin drinks it instead of it sitting on top of your face like frosting on a cake.

A: Okay.

B: And it's my moisturizer, my night cream, my under-eye, my hand cream. All at the exact same time.

A: Hold on. Is that why your skin looks like that?

B: My brother asked if I'd had Botox.

A: Wait, so... PEOPLE THINK YOU HAD BOTOX?

B: Mhmm.

A: Yes!

B: NOPE. JUST THE TALLOW.

A: Okay, yeah. I'm impressed.

B: AND IT GOES UNDER MAKEUP.

A: WAIT, REALLY?!

B: Yeah, not greasy, soaks right in. They make it in-house in Ontario, it's got over 15,000 five-star reviews, and I mean, it just so happens that I was on the website, and there's a money-back guarantee.

A: Okay wait, yeah, never mind. I don't want this. I need it. Imma go get it right now.

B: Exactly!

## Text hierarchy
Largest: WHY ARE YOU THROWING AWAY ALL YOUR SKINCARE? and PEOPLE THINK YOU HAD BOTOX?
Problem labels, near the face or the prop: FINE LINES / UNDER-EYE BAGS / HOW MANY CREAMS? / TWELVE? / THE WATER SHELF
Mechanism, one claim per screen: the five ingredients, YOUR SKIN STOPS MAKING ITS OWN OIL, the four uses, ALL AT THE SAME TIME, JUST THE TALLOW, UNDER MAKEUP, NOT GREASY
Offer, held long enough to read: MADE IN ONTARIO / 15,000+ FIVE-STAR REVIEWS / MONEY-BACK GUARANTEE / GO GET YOURS NOW

## Loop points
Strongest: "Exactly!" back into "Why are you throwing away all your skincare?" Match the framing so the tin is in the same spot.
Short cutdown (20 to 30s): end on "PEOPLE THINK YOU HAD BOTOX?" and restart on the hook. Question plus surprise is a complete mini-loop without the offer.

## Deviations from the source, on purpose
- No sale, no free gifts. Guarantee is the only offer mechanic in the account's history. The sale/price/scarcity bucket in the current test is 6.30x, but the mechanic is unconfirmed, so nothing was invented here.
- The diagnosis beat keeps the source's direction (B diagnoses A: fine lines, under-eye bags) and then A notices B's skin at the turn, because that's the scene customers describe in their own reviews.
- One second reveal, not two. "It replaces all of them" is already implied by "this is all I use now," so the shout is spent on the Botox question. Makeup moved to the desirability beat.
- "After a certain age" instead of 30 or 40. That number is still undecided.
