## What didn't add up in the working draft
1. The old bottom half was still attached: a second demo, a second order, "the difference after one day was absolutely insane," the husband's Botox line, the flat "wow / thank you" facial, "10,000" reviews, "thousands of years," and the "hit" typo. All removed; its ingredients, price-worry, link and twice-a-day beats were moved up into order.
2. The mechanism stopped at "those lines get deeper." The draft never said why tallow helps (same fats, replaces what you lost) or why the Olay doesn't (mostly water). With "oh, that's why" now in Sarah's mouth, that explanation is owed: she names a cause and the script has to close it. Restored as one paragraph.
3. "Oh, that's why" reverses the operator's earlier instruction (don't say the moisturizer is doing it, only that it doesn't replace the oil). The restored line keeps it on the safe side: the Olay isn't harming her, it just can't put the oil back. If the blame reads too strong, the earlier "does it replace the oil you're losing?" does the same job without it.
4. The hook's promise ("what a regular dermatologist won't tell you") had no payoff again once the dermatologist line was dropped. Restored.
5. She ordered before she'd seen the ingredients, the price, or the reviews ("So I'm like fine and I order it" came straight after the mechanism). Order is now mechanism, ingredients, price worry, link and proof, order, demo.
6. The cottage and "hadn't seen her since June" are gone. The new logic, "for the first time I notice," holds on its own: the esthetician has just made her look at lines, so she finally sees her neighbor's. No hole. "Winter was coming" is also gone; the naturopath's "Then one winter, you do" still carries the season.
7. "After 40" (naturopath) against "somewhere after 50" (narrator) is not a contradiction: "For years you don't notice" bridges them.
8. Typos fixed: "hears" to "here's," "hit" to "him."
9. Still open, as before: "y'all" to one person in an otherwise "you guys" voice; V3 says over 60 while "four years on me" makes the narrator 58 (make Sarah 66 if V3 wins); "40 to 50 dollars" isn't in the client file.
