## Script 1: "I tested seven brands"

### Why the swaps
- Point 1 ("stop buying whipped tallow") attacks TT's own Face Whip. It has to go.
- Point 4 ("be careful with TikTok Shop") attacks the channel TT sells on. It has to go.
- Point 2 (factory farmed) stays.

### Ideas for point 1: a bigger problem than whipped air
1. **Made in China, white-labeled (recommended).** "Flip the tin over. If it doesn't say where it's made, it's probably bulk tallow from China with somebody's sticker on it." This pays off "made in Canada, made in-house" and sits in TT's best lane (manufacturing trust, $104 CPA; "Made here, not in China" is ad #3).
2. **Bleached and deodorized.** "If it's snow white and smells like nothing, it's been bleached and deodorized. Real tallow is creamy." TT's allowed proof: not bleached, not deodorized.
3. **The brand never touches the tallow.** "Half these brands never render a thing. They buy the same bulk tallow and put their logo on it." Pays off "made in-house".
4. **Tallow isn't the main ingredient.** "Read the label. If tallow isn't first, you're paying for coconut oil and shea." This needs the operator to confirm that tallow is listed first on F-Balm.

### Ideas for point 4: replacing the TikTok Shop warning
1. **No money-back guarantee (recommended).** "If a brand won't give you your money back, don't buy it. If they don't believe in it, why should you?" The guarantee is TT's only offer mechanic, and it doesn't touch any sales channel.
2. **They can't tell you where the tallow came from.** "Ask them who the rancher is. If they can't tell you, walk away." Pays off "local ranchers", but it overlaps with point 2.
3. **It smells like ground beef.** "If it smells like a burger, it wasn't purified properly." This is customer language ("made me smell like ground beef," said about other brands) and it pays off "triple purified". Risk: scent is 39% of TT's own one- and two-star reviews. Don't use it until the operator rules on this.
4. **Brands with 200 reviews.** "If it's got 200 reviews, all from the same month, keep scrolling." This pays off TT's review volume, but the live count must be confirmed first.

## Script 2: "80% of tallow companies don't melt their own tallow"

80% of tallow skincare companies don't render their own tallow.
They just buy it from a supplier.
This usually means one of two things.
One: it's highly processed commercial tallow sourced from who knows where.
They'll tell you it's grass-fed because their supplier told them.
But is it?
Two: it's mixed with who knows what in the process.
Commercial rendering bleaches it to control the color and deodorizes it to control the scent.
To get the consistency big producers want.
So instead, ask your tallow company to show you their raw fat. If they can't, don't buy their product!
The tallow industry is so popular that most tallow products on the market contain barely any tallow.
That's why we show our whole process on social media.
We source raw beef fat from local ranchers here in Canada and make it in-house, from raw fat to finished balm. We triple purify it. We never bleach it. We never deodorize it.
It's a tedious process.
But it's the only way to make sure you actually have pure, quality-controlled, grass-fed tallow.
Then we mix in honey, olive oil, beeswax and frankincense, making a product that's mostly tallow.
That is why it works!
That is why our F-Balm has over [10,000] reviews.
And that's why it can help people with dry skin, acne, eczema and rosacea.
So people actually cancel their Botox appointments after trying it.
Try it yourself today, and experience what real tallow feels like.

### What changed and why
- Irish farms became local ranchers here in Canada. "Melt in-house" became "make it in-house, triple purify". The vague "chemicals to control color and scent" became TT's own proof: never bleached, never deodorized.
- "Raw Irish honey, olive oil and beeswax" became the real F-Balm list: honey, olive oil, beeswax, frankincense. No origin is claimed, because the olive oil comes from Tunisia and the frankincense from India.
- "Takes several days" was cut, because TT's process time isn't on file. "Tedious" stays.
- "100,000 customers in 60 countries on 6 continents" was cut, because TT has no figure on file. TT is 72% Canada and 27% US. The line is one beat shorter now.
- "2,000 5-star reviews" became "over [10,000] reviews", the brand's own historical claim. Confirm the live number before it ships.
- Dry skin, acne, eczema and rosacea all trace to TT reviews (eczema, psoriasis and rosacea: 975; acne: 46). The Botox line is kept as instructed.
