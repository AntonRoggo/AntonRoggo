## Story logic pass, short

Chain: the company sold, the big shot, the foursome, wives to girls, he notices, the doorway → the glasses, the cart barn → no scream, the clubhouse mirror, "I almost believed him" → a year of effort, the doctor → his Amex, Tulum, no note → the one chair, "can I sit here" → "you look so good for your age, what are you doing" / "honey, I was where you are" / "alone in Tulum on somebody else's card" → forty pounds, the husband who stopped reaching, the massages, "the drain has to open every day" → 001's mechanism → the bottle, the herbs, "I always bring two," "watch" → home, he didn't ask, he didn't check the card → the weeks, the dress on the door → the dinner, Dave first, the stare, Kaylee at the bar, "what did you do," no smile, walks past → coward, the label, the close.

1. **The setup is his slope, not a list.** Six things the viewer sees (the watch, the shirts, the teeth, the phone, Dave's pro-shop girl, Steve's "babe"), then the doorway. The catch lands as the bottom of it.
2. **Why she's in Tulum.** Rich, just caught him, his card. "Somewhere he wasn't." No friend, no plan, which is what makes Joyce's "alone in Tulum on somebody else's card" land: Joyce reads her in one look because she's been there.
3. **The guide can know (checklist 4).** Joyce had it, forty pounds, a husband who stopped reaching, and learned it from a drainage therapist. Same credential chain as the pool-chair v2.
4. **Who asks.** Becca asks, the bank's "I have to ask. How?" in the operator's words. Joyce's "honey" keeps the pool-chair's tone without the refusal beat, which the runtime can't carry.
5. **The bottle isn't sold.** A stranger gives her a spare. The name waits until the close, and "I didn't read the label until the flight home" says why.
6. **He doesn't know she knows, and doesn't notice she left.** "Didn't ask where I'd been. Didn't check the card." That's his absence in two lines, and it sets up the dinner stare.
7. **Arithmetic.** 63, 66, 22. Twenty-two since sixty. Fourteen at four, nineteen at six. Six weeks from the pool to the dinner.

Runtime: about 750 spoken words in the blocks, roughly 5:10 at 145 wpm, plus the hook. With Hook V1 (Block 2 skipped) about 4:55.
