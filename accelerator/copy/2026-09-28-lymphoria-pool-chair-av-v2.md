## Logic pass on v2

1. **The refusal and the pleading are back in the source's order:** refuse (No, No?, Not after that comment), guess list, "please tell me," "you don't need it," "no, look," the confession, "can you please tell me now," "before I tell you." Lorraine earns it. That is the source's engine and it was the operator's note.
2. **The tone is back:** "Oh honey" four times, "you really don't know anything, do you," "you're adorable," "if the shots are the route you want, take it," "Damn." / "You." Camille toys with her and Lorraine takes it because she wants the answer. Everett's shoulder kiss and "Nothing, baby" keep him affectionate, which is what Lorraine is envying.
3. **The chain no longer needs a coincidence.** Camille is at the resort because she comes for the drainage; the woman who did it told her the table is once a month and the drain has to open every day (Ana's line), and gave her the bottle. It also answers why a wealthy Black couple is in Trancoso, which the setting was asking.
4. **Checklist 4, the guide.** Camille is living proof (forty pounds, four years) and relays a practitioner. Better than "I had it" alone.
5. **Water is out.** "Tight and full," "stuck waste," "you can't burn waste." The animation callout says waste.
6. **The Amazon knock is out.** The bank's warning replaces it, so the ad never names the store the brand sells on.
7. **Arithmetic:** both in their 50s, Camille four years out, forty pounds, "after fifty it slows down and stops," six weeks in the stamps and in "thank me in six weeks." Nothing else claims a number.
8. **Hooks:** V1 is the source's open with the operator's line. V2 and V3 withhold the mechanism. All three end on "Oh honey. It's not the shots." and the table opens on "You really don't know anything, do you."

Note on the narrator: a two-hander that ends on "Damn." / "You." and Everett's drinks does not need a voice after it. The one-line narrator is there if the platform needs a spoken CTA; the end card can carry it alone.

Still the account's open number: 60-day.
