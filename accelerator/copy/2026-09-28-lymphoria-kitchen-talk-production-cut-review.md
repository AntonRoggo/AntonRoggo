# Review: "The Kitchen Talk & The Gym Guide" production cut, against the Reddit source, the tailgate winner and the bank

Filed 2026-09-28. Operator's production cut of the "considering leaving my wife" story, checked against [[story-007-considering-leaving-my-wife-husband-pov]] (the source), [[story-008-tailgate-son-tells-dad]] (the structure the song used), [[2026-09-28-lymphoria-considering-leaving-my-wife-song]] (the song), [[mechanism-and-product-core]] and [[story-ad-logic]].

Verdict: too far. The cut keeps the props (cookout, kitchen, weeks, herbs, offer) and drops both engines: the Reddit's husband ("before I get called an asshole") and the tailgate's rule that the person who loves her carries the mechanism. Four sections fail logic.

## Fails story logic
1. **Her effort is twelve minutes.** The guide says "that treadmill is making your stomach bigger" on Day 1, minute twelve. Every winner puts months of effort on record before anyone says "not fat" (locker room: five days a week for three months; 001: three years; tailgate: "gym, dieting, cutting back, none of it worked"). Here the viewer, and Chris, can say "you tried for twelve minutes." The absolution has nothing to absolve.
2. **The mechanism makes Chris wrong.** Chris asks her to try. A stranger tells her trying makes it worse. The tailgate's CTA is "my son had to be the one to tell me, and he was right." This cut's logic is "my husband told me to try, and trying was the problem." Chris then vanishes until a neck kiss. In the source, the update is his: "so proud of her," the walks together.
3. **"Cardio clamps the drain" is not in the bank and contradicts the source.** No Lymphoria winner says exercise makes it worse. The stance knob is "keep both, the product unblocks them" (locker room), "didn't change a single thing" (coworker), "still drinks on weekends" (tailgate). The Reddit's payoff is exercise together, three days a week because of her knees. The song kept it. This cut tells her to stop, then never lets her walk.
4. **The hook promises what the body never delivers.** The Reddit card says "considering leaving my wife." The kitchen has no leaving, no ultimatum, no "I don't want to be that dude." And "that afternoon I saw the Reddit post my husband wrote" is unstaged: how did she see it? If she read it, the kitchen is him caught, not him brave, and "he didn't yell, he wasn't mean" is the wrong beat to open on.

## Drifted from the winning beats
- **Cortisol without a face.** Operator rule: cortisol mechanisms carry the blame transfer and the villain keeps a face. Here the stress is "sleepless nights." Nobody did this to her, so the cortisol structure is the wrong tool (the song used the birth as the cause for that reason). None of the seven pieces are present: Sarah never asks how she is, effort isn't on record, no "they did this to you," no heart-then-body, no mission.
- **The guide.** Bank guides had it themselves or a relative did, and they are in the lead's life (crew, gym peer, aunt, roommate's dad). Sarah is a stranger who slaps the emergency stop on another woman's treadmill. Checklist: characters act like real people. That is not how people act at a gym.
- **No number.** "No unrealistic math" left the montage with no pounds. Every winner has one at week four and week six (14, 19; the source itself says 35). Without it, "260 at 5'2" to "the pre-baby dress zipped right up" in six weeks reads as sixty-plus pounds, far over the ceiling. The song has the same dress problem at week five and should say "the dress I bought last summer and never wore" (spine A's device), not a pre-baby dress.
- **The mirror, the wrong authority, the circle, the truth beat are gone.** "Your doctor never told you about the cortisol spiral, did he?" asserts a doctor we never met.
- **Chris's list is gone.** The source's kitchen is "I asked you to the gym with me, a diet with me, stop the fast food, you said no to every one." That list is what makes "at least try" land and what makes her Day 1 a turn. Cut it and the talk is sanded.

## Voice and messaging
- "Trapped fluid," "hoard fluid," "cellular waste and fluid": ruled out. Stuck waste, drain the waste, never fluid.
- "Red Clover calms the tissue inflammation so cortisol resets": invented herb job. Bank: calms the swelling so it doesn't build back.
- "Pregnancy compressed your pelvic lymphatic vessels": new claim. Bank cause for the birth is "after a baby it slows and never gets back up" or the c-section drains.
- "Tastes mild and sweet" vs "tastes like honey." "Open your drain today" vs the bank's closers. "Fresh botanical batch restock" vs "small batches, herbs sourced fresh, sells out."
- "Heart disease took your mom at forty-eight" is invented. Source: "runs in my wife's family." A specific is good; this one is a death we made up.
- "Radiant," "unshakeable confidence," "healthy, radiant, curvy woman who had her life back": asserted, not shown. The bank shows the stare and the sentence ("You look like yourself again"). "You are the most beautiful woman in the world" is generic where the tailgate's wife says "my husband."
- "Rock-hard balloon," "heavy pressure on my lower back": not traceable to a quote in the evidence file.
- Em dashes throughout. Names collide: Linda (advertorial, 61), Sarah (blind date coworker).
- The Reddit's register is gone entirely: no "asshole," no "35 fucking lbs," no "I don't want to be that dude." The source's voice was the point.

## Keep
- The words-on-screen Reddit card is the right hook device if the logic is fixed: either the card is narrator-only and she never sees it (the song's version), or stage how she found it and let the kitchen be her confronting him.
- Kitchen "at least try" and "thirteen." Labor Day at the same yard. The week montage shape. Half a dropper morning and night, fifteen minutes, four herbs in order, 70 percent, Buy 2 Get 1, small batches.

## The fix in one move
Put the mechanism back in Chris's hands the way the tailgate does: Sarah (or a peer of Chris's whose wife had it) supplies it, Chris relays it in six lines, "just try it, for me," and the walks at week five are the two of them. Then her effort is the three years she refused, the twelve minutes become the tailgate's "gym won't fix it" said by someone who had it, not a stranger's emergency stop, and Chris is right.
