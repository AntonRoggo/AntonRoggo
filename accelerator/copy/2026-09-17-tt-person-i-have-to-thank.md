## THE CREATIVE (built on V1)

Okay, I'm 62. I'm a grandmother of four. And my hairstylist just told me I have no wrinkles. And the person I have to thank for that? My friend Linda. Who is also 62.

Okay, so let me set the stage for you.

So my daughter sends me a photo. From her wedding. Ten years ago. And I'm looking at it next to the one she took at dinner on Sunday, and I'm like, what the heck happened to my skin? The lines around my mouth. Around my eyes. My neck. I can't see a shred of that woman in myself.

And it's not like I wasn't trying. I have tried everything. Lotions, creams, serums, you name it. I had a drawer full of it. At some point I just, I got so tired of trying so many products at ridiculous prices that I simply gave up. I'm just old, right? That's what I told myself.

So I'm at my friend Linda's place the next weekend. We've known each other thirty years. She's my age. And I'm telling her all of this, the photo, the creams, I'm just old. And she's sitting across from me, and I stop. Because her skin. Her skin has changed. Like, changed so much for the better. And I'm like, what are you doing?

And she gets up, and she comes back with one tin. That's it. One tin. And she puts it in my hand and it says beef tallow.

And I'm like, Linda. Beef tallow. Like, fat? On your face?

And she goes, my mom used to make this stuff. Put it on.

So I did. Right there at her kitchen table. And I just kind of sat there. Because my grandmother did this too. She put tallow on her face her whole life. I remember thinking she was crazy when she told me that. And here's my friend, same age as me, with skin I'd kill for, using the thing I laughed at.

I put it on a couple more times that weekend. And she sends me the link. The Tallowed Truth. F-Balm. Made in Canada, five ingredients.

So I bring it up with my doctor. The holistic one. Fully expecting her to laugh. She didn't laugh. She said, I use it. She said, after 50 your skin stops making its own oil, and that's the dryness, that's the lines. Nothing in a jar of cream replaces oil. Tallow is the closest thing to it. Women have been putting it on their faces for two thousand years. It's not an $800 treatment.

So I'm in the car, and I'm scrolling, and I realize I've been seeing these ads for a year and never once clicked. 15,000-plus five-star reviews. And people my age, saying the exact same thing I'd been feeling. "At 55 I was so tired of trying so many products at ridiculous prices that I simply gave up." That was me. That was literally me.

So I ordered it. First night, I woke up and my skin didn't feel dry or tight. That hadn't happened in, like, ever. Two weeks in, the lines in that photo, they're starting to look softer. My skin felt soft again. All day, not just after I put it on.

About a month in, I go for the facial I'd booked before the photo, and the esthetician asks me if I'd had some laser procedures done. I said no. She just looked at me.

And then Saturday. I'm in the chair getting my hair cut, and my hairstylist stops, and she's looking at me in the mirror, and she goes, your skin. It's so nice. You don't have any wrinkles. I'm 62. I've been going to her for fifteen years. She's never said that once.

So, Linda, if you're watching this. Thank you for not letting me give up. And to my grandmother, wherever you are. You were right. I'm sorry I laughed.

The link is below. Go to the official site, The Tallowed Truth. There are knockoff tallow balms everywhere now and they're bleached and they don't work. Read the reviews, and if you don't like it, you get your money back. And listen. If you've been telling yourself you're just old, same. But I'm back. And now it's your turn.

## Notes
- Two guides, split by job. The friend introduces (53 reviews; the granddaughter, 66, was the previous version). The holistic doctor explains (the only professional in the reviews who says she uses it herself). Neither is an esthetician, who are on record against tallow.
- Same-age friend is a stronger witness than the granddaughter: a 24-year-old's skin proves nothing, a 62-year-old's does. The cost is the hook's twist. "My 24-year-old granddaughter" was a surprise; "my friend" is not, so the surprise moved to "Who is also 62."
- Hairstylist-friend variant, if wanted: "I will be 68 and my clients at my hair salon have been making comments that I look younger" [raw]. The friend who does hair introduces it and the payoff is in her chair. Collapses two witnesses into one, so not used.
- "The holistic one" is one phrase. If it reads as a claim about a credential, cut to "my doctor."
