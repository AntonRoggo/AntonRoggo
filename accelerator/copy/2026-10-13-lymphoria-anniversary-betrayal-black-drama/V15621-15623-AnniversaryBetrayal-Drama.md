**WHY IT WORKS**

*   The wound is being turned down on her own anniversary, then finding out why from the window. Her husband and her maid of honor in one shot.
*   All three hooks are the same night and the same window, from three angles: the scene, her line, and Jasmine's eyes.
*   The cruel line, "You stopped trying after the baby. Look at you", is the line people repeat in the comments, and the payoff flips it.
*   The guide is his own sister. She has every reason to protect him and picks Aaliyah anyway, so the help lands harder.
*   The clock is a real event with the same people: Jade's birthday, six weeks out, Lance at the grill.
*   The payoff flips the hooks. He was too tired for her. Now he asks to talk, she says no, and Damon asks if she's tired.
