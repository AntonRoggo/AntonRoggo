# Lost-glow hooks

20 hooks for the Karen lost-glow script. Entry: the slow fade. Nothing broke, nothing failed. She just woke up one morning and the light was gone. First-person customer voice, spoken-word delivery.

Source script: [[2026-08-29-karen-script-lost-glow]]

---

## The mirror moment

1. I woke up last Tuesday, looked in the bathroom mirror, and my first thought was, who is that. I had slept eight hours. I wasn't sick. I just looked like someone turned the brightness down on my face.

2. There's this thing that happens when you wash your face in the morning and the woman staring back at you looks ten years older than the woman you feel like on the inside. And nobody warned me that would just become every morning.

3. I caught my reflection in the bathroom window this morning, not even the mirror, just the window, and I looked exhausted. I slept fine. I feel fine. My face just doesn't agree anymore.

4. Okay so I need to know if this happens to anyone else. You wake up, you feel rested, you feel good, and then you look in the mirror and it's like your face didn't get the memo.

## The video call

5. I was on a Zoom call for work last week and I caught my own face in the little corner box and I genuinely did not recognize myself. Because the woman on screen looked like she hadn't slept in three days. I'd slept eight hours.

6. My coworker asked if I was feeling okay on a video call last Thursday. I was feeling great. My face just looked like it gave up.

## The sentence that changed

7. My husband used to say "you look beautiful." Now he says "you look fine." He means it every time. But "fine" when you used to hear "beautiful" is a completely different sentence.

8. The last time someone told me I looked rested was so long ago I actually remember where I was standing. That's how rare it became.

## The withdrawal

9. I stopped wanting to go to dinner with friends. Not because I was sad or depressed. I just got tired of getting ready, looking in the mirror, and feeling like nothing I put on top was going to fix the face underneath.

10. I used to take pictures with my daughter every time she came over. Somewhere around last fall I started saying "not today" and I couldn't have told you why.

11. My sister invited me to her birthday dinner and my first thought wasn't what to wear. My first thought was, will there be photos. That's when I knew something had shifted.

## Products failing

12. I have a moisturizer, a serum, and an eye cream on my bathroom counter that cost me over two hundred dollars combined. My skin looks exactly the same as it did the day I bought them. Actually, it looks worse.

13. I added a vitamin C serum, a peptide cream, and a retinol in the last year. My skin care routine got more complicated every month. My skin got more tired every month. Same direction, opposite speed.

14. I used to pick up a new moisturizer and feel like it was going to be the one. Now I pick one up and I already know. Because the last four didn't do anything either.

## The brightness metaphor

15. My friend looked at me over coffee last month and said, "You look tired, are you okay?" And I was okay. I just, somewhere around 50, my face stopped looking like it matched how I actually felt. Like someone dimmed a light and I can't find the switch.

16. I don't look old. I want to be clear about that. I look faded. Like a photo of myself that sat in the sun too long.

## The daughter/compliment

17. My daughter came over last Sunday and I watched her face when she looked at me and I could see it. She didn't say anything. She didn't have to. I looked tired and she noticed, and she's too sweet to say so.

18. I used to get compliments on my skin in my 40s without trying. Now I'm trying harder than I ever have and the last compliment I got was my husband saying I looked "refreshed." That was in March.

## The realization

19. I spent six months thinking I was doing something wrong with my skincare. Turns out I was doing everything right. The problem was that everything I was using was made mostly of water, and my skin stopped needing water about ten years ago. My skin needed its oil back.

20. The weirdest part is I didn't lose my skin overnight. I lost it so slowly I kept blaming the products. I blamed the moisturizer, the serum, the cleanser. Turns out none of them were broken. They were just the wrong tool for what was actually happening.
