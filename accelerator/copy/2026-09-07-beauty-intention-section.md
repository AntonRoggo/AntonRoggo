# Beauty / intention section (newsletter draft)

The bleak and boring days come from a lack of intention.
You're always thinking about the next thing or worried about what others are doing.
You're too busy to live your own life.

But things can be beautiful. Everything.

Most of life is having the energy to pay attention.

Here are things to fix in order of most to least importance:

1. How your room looks. Keep it clean. Keep artwork and plants around you. Your environment is either feeding you or draining you.

2. The food in your house. When I was living paycheque to paycheque making 2k a month, 75% of my money was spent on quality food. It's not a luxury. It's the foundation.

3. Your morning routine. Your breakfast can be the first step. Eat real food. Make a real breakfast. Get sunlight. Sit down and relax for a bit. Wake up earlier if that's what it takes.

4. How much time you spend outdoors. Get out of the city when you can. You weren't built to stare at walls and screens all day.

5. Do you have time to do nothing? And if you do, are you wasting it on your phone?

Beauty isn't something you go seek outside. It's something you integrate into your life. You should be able to look at your day and confidently say it was beautiful, at least in some way.

Don't wake up like you're late to your own life. Don't force your morning to be rushed, ugly, and overstimulated. Look up new recipes for breakfast. Experiment. Try something new.

Above all else, don't start your day inside your phone. Relegate it to the least important thing. Don't check it first thing in the morning. Everything can wait, I promise.
