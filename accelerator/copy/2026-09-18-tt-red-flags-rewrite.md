## The winners' skeleton (cholesterol script, ~230 words)
1. Hook: a condition the viewer already fears, plus three symptoms she can check in two seconds, in plain words ("your stream resembles a leaky faucet").
2. "I thought this was just a part of getting older until [trigger event]." Then "I didn't get it. I was eating clean and working out." Then the fear.
3. Rejected alternative: "I didn't want to get on any medications."
4. Discovery with a crowd: "this Facebook group where hundreds of people were raving."
5. Product: category name, count, ancient use, jobs mapped to the condition.
6. "I was a bit skeptical at first, but after seeing 98,000 five-star reviews."
7. "Here's what I noticed": the three hook symptoms resolved, by name, first person, with an honest hedge ("I haven't taken a second blood test yet").
8. "The only change in my routine."
9. Taste objection: "super delicious."
10. CTA on the condition: "If you're struggling with X... first bag risk free."

Two formats were stitched together: a red-flags confession for the first half and the 30-day timeline for the second. The winners are one voice start to finish.

## The rewrite

What they call it: dry skin 1,048 (dead lane as a lead), wrinkles 946, aging 265, "aging skin" 78, "mature skin" 66. "I have purchased $50 to $500 creams for my aging skin to no avail." "My skin suddenly looked wrinkly and old" (74). Google: "why has my face aged so fast."
