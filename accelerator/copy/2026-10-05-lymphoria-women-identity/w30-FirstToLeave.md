## Source

Source summary (our words): A UK woman's member story on a commercial slimming site. She describes years of feeling self-conscious on nights out with friends: they wore going-out clothes while she hid in loose, shapeless shirts. She wore restrictive control underwear and a heavy-duty bra, and often went home early just to get out of them. She also describes office-job routines that made healthy habits harder. Her turning point was a family wedding, where she was a bridesmaid and wanted to feel confident in the dress. She reports reaching her goal size before the wedding and no longer needing the control pants. Note: the research entry framed her as formerly slim and sporty; the source itself describes her as overweight through her twenties. The sporty former self in our script is invented, as is everything else.
