## Source

In a reported interview, a mother recalls an episode from her twenties: she went to a restaurant expecting to meet a group of mothers for dinner and learned the plan had been changed to lunch without anyone telling her. A friend later put it down to her weight. She describes the damage to her self-worth and pulling back from making friends, and later regretted the experiences she missed because of other people's opinions and her own fear of being judged. The source also covers dieting and medical events; none of that is used here. The dinner club, the names, the guide and the payoff are invented.
