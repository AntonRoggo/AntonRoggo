Source summary (our words): A 47-year-old working mother of three teenage sons, who also looked after elderly parents and was busy at church, remembers her twenties as easy: she had been a cheerleader and homecoming queen and felt good in her clothes. The weight came on slowly between pregnancies. She once lost 27 pounds, then gained it back and more over one holiday season, and years of diets left her discouraged. Climbing stairs left her short of breath, and she worried about her heart. The turning point was a friend who joined a weight-loss program, dropped about 20 pounds and looked noticeably better. Within days she joined the same program, and over six months she lost 62 pounds through portions, group support, dance-fitness classes and daily dog walks. Her husband supported her throughout.
