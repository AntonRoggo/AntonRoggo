FORMAT: SONG

## Source

Source summary (our words): A personal newsletter essay. The writer lost about 150 pounds roughly ten years earlier and kept it off long enough that it became part of who she was: people admired her, asked how she did it, and she felt welcome everywhere. She has since regained 45 to 50 pounds and describes it as failure. Her friend was hosting a clothing swap, and she dreaded being the largest woman there with nothing to try on, so she used bra shopping as her excuse not to go, then bought a costly bra as both comfort and reward. When the host sent a photo of clothes in her size, she changed her mind and went. She was welcomed, found things that fit, and ended the night on a rooftop trading body-image confessions with friends. Her own summary: shame turned "into belonging." (Opened and read 2026-10-04.)
