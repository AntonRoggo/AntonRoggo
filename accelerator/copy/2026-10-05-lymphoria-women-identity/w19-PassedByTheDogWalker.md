## Source

Source summary (our words): A woman built her early identity around competitive tennis and trained at a serious academy alongside future top players. Injuries ended that path, and with it the daily structure and movement she was used to. Decades of desk-bound corporate work followed, and the weight built up without her noticing. The turning point was an ordinary walk near home: an elderly woman with a small dog passed her easily while she struggled for breath. She was struck that someone roughly four decades older was finding the walk easier than she was, and that moment pushed her to change her life, later becoming a fitness coach for women over forty. Commercial coaching-business biography, not independent evidence.
