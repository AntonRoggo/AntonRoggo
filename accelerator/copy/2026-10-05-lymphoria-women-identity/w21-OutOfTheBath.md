## Source

Source summary (our words): A commercial testimonial from a weight-loss company. A woman of 62 had to bring her weight down before a needed abdominal operation. She had dieted on and off since her twenties and had a long medical history, including cancer treatment, surgical complications and a badly hurt ankle. Before the change she could not reach her feet, so her husband put on and tied her shoes. She could not climb out of the bath or get down to the floor without help, walking hurt, and housework and gardening had stopped. She says needing him for personal care made the marriage feel less equal. After about three stone came off over six months, she could bathe alone, reach her feet, and do housework and gardening again, and the two of them felt like equal partners. She says she feels "more like the old me." (Opened and read 2026-10-04. Only the relationship and independence beats are used; the medical history and the company's program are not.)
