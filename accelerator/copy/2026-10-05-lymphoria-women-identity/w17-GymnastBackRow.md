## Source

Source summary (our words): A woman in her mid-fifties had been a competitive gymnast and later a coach, and ran often. A knee injury ended the exercise, and over time she gained a lot of weight, skipped breakfast and leaned on takeaways and big portions. She knew about the weight but told herself it did not matter, until a photograph from a family gathering made her cry and see how far she had drifted from her fit self. She joined a commercial slimming program online because group meetings made her anxious, and over a little more than two years she lost a large amount of weight through fuller healthy meals and long walks near her coastal home, finding her competitive streak again. Commercial testimonial, not independent evidence.
