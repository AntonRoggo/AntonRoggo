# w45. LeopardDress (the woman who dresses everyone she knows steps out of a cab in a leopard dress, hears one line from the bouncer, and gets straight back in)

FORMAT: SONG

Why song: the insult is one line from a stranger and she never answers it in the moment, so there is no confrontation scene to dramatize. The power is the hidden aftermath (the dress in the back of the closet, the shop window she stops dressing) and walking back to the same door in the same dress. Spine A, with a guide who walks into her shop.

## Source

Source summary (our words): A member story on a commercial weight-loss program's UK blog. A young woman who loved fashion describes years of insecurity about her size. On a night out she wore a bold leopard-print dress, and as she got out of the taxi a stranger shouted a cruel insult about her body. She went straight home and never wore the dress again. She later moved in with a partner who accepted her as she was, joined the program, and reports losing a large amount of weight over a little more than a year, eventually wearing her dream wedding dress. She now describes herself as "comfortable in her own skin." Our script keeps only the shape of the moment (the dress, the cab, one stranger's line); the woman, the shop, the guide and the payoff are invented.
