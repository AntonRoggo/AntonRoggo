## Source

- Title: Former cheerleader who once weighed over 300 pounds reveals transformation
- Publisher: Inside Edition, interview (2015-08-10)
- Link: https://www.insideedition.com/11387-former-cheerleader-who-once-weighed-over-300-pounds-reveals-incredible-transformation (opened; summarized, nothing reproduced)

Source summary (our words): A woman who cheered for a big university went on to coach dance and cheer. While teaching, she caught herself doing something strange: in the studio mirror she watched the kids behind her and steered her eyes away from her own reflection. Her confidence was at its lowest point. Her weight had climbed past 300 pounds, and she traced it to food she leaned on after a breakup in her teens. She joined a televised weight-loss program, set herself the goal of fitting into her old cheer uniform again, and over about nine months lost around 145 pounds and reached that goal. She now teaches at a high school. Note from the research entry: eating-disorder history appears in related coverage, so nothing here imitates extreme dieting.
