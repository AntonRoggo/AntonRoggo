## Source

Source summary (our words): A woman in her late forties had loved running and once ran marathons. Years of fertility treatment and other health problems, plus doctors telling her to avoid hard exercise, changed her body and her routine. The turning point came after a Mediterranean cruise, when she saw holiday photos of herself in a sleeveless dress and did not recognize the woman in them. She wanted her confidence and a sense of control back. She joined a commercial slimming group, changed how she ate, adapted family recipes, added gentle exercise like Pilates and countryside walks, and lost a significant amount of weight. She framed the change as feeling stronger and kinder to herself, not chasing her old marathon self. Commercial testimonial, not independent evidence.

Link: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/real-life-stories/elsie-perks (opened; summarized, nothing reproduced)
