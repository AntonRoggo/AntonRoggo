## Source

Source summary (our words): A former professional dancer in her early forties had spent years touring and training every day, holding a small dress size without thinking about it. After she settled down and opened her own dance school, she moved less and ate out more, and the weight crept on. Grief made it worse: she lost both parents within a year and leaned on comfort food. What stung most was work. Meeting new pupils, she felt embarrassed, because she believed a dance teacher should look fit and healthy, and she no longer did. She avoided photos and felt her energy was gone. She later lost a large amount of weight through a commercial slimming program and got her confidence back. Commercial testimonial, not independent evidence.
