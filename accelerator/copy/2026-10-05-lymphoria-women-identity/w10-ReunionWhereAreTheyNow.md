Source summary (our words): A lawyer had been heavy since school, around 200 pounds at graduation, and by her late thirties she weighed about 260, which she tied to eating for comfort. In the fall of 2015 her 20th high school reunion invitation arrived. Scrolling the list of classmates on Facebook set off a wave of anxiety: she was afraid people would see only her size and that her career and everything she had built would go unnoticed. Instead of skipping the event, she decided the problem was how she saw herself and changed her habits: whole foods, a tracking app, learning to cook, walking, yoga and swimming. Over time she lost about 105 pounds and says her confidence and mood improved.
