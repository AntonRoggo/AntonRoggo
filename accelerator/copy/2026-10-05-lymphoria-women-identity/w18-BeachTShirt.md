## Source

Source summary (our words): A woman in her late thirties had always been slim and fairly active, then gradually gained weight until she avoided mirrors and dreaded clothes shopping. The low point was a long-awaited beach holiday abroad: in the heat she stayed covered in a loose T-shirt the whole time, unhappy with how she looked. On New Year's Day she joined a commercial slimming group, found she could still eat foods she enjoyed, and lost a meaningful amount of weight. The moment that stayed with her was trying on a favorite dress she had not worn in years and finding it fit, which brought happy tears. On a later beach trip she wore a bikini with confidence. Commercial testimonial, not independent evidence.
