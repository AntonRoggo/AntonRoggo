Source summary (our words): A woman who cheered for a pro football squad writes that this one part of her past still draws more attention than any career she had after it. She has been to several high school reunions and to a big anniversary reunion of her old squad, and for that one she dropped about 35 pounds so she could wear a dress she loved. Since then she has put roughly 40 pounds back on. Now a national reunion of former pro cheerleaders is coming up. She expected it to be easy, but as the date gets closer the old worries about how she looks, and about explaining it, have come back. Living in Los Angeles adds pressure, because she feels people expect her to look polished.

Source summary (our words): A woman who cheered for a big university went on to coach dance and cheer. While teaching, she caught herself doing something strange: in the studio mirror she watched the kids behind her and steered her eyes away from her own reflection. She joined a televised weight-loss program, set herself the goal of fitting into her old cheer uniform again, and over about nine months lost around 145 pounds and reached that goal. She now teaches at a high school. Note from the research entry: eating-disorder history appears in related coverage, so nothing here imitates extreme dieting.
