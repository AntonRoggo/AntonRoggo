## Notes
- "Smokers' lines, even if you never smoked" is the widening move. 15 reviews say smoker; 71 say "around my mouth" without it. Naming the category then widening it keeps the hook specific and the audience broad.
- The 4-star quote is deliberate. The 69 ad's "it's not a miracle" is a beat, and a real mixed review is the strongest version of it.
- "Priced out what it costs to get those filled" is the only line not from a review or the 69 ad. Filler for lip lines is the procedure this audience compares against. Cut it if it reads as a claim.
