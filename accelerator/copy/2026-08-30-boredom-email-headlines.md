# Email headlines: Learn to Be Bored Again

20 subject lines + preview texts for the boredom/phone/focus email.

---

## The confrontation

1. Your phone is an anesthetic
   *It numbs you from how you actually feel.*

2. You can't sit with yourself for 30 seconds
   *You're waiting for your order at the cafe. It's been maybe thirty seconds.*

3. Your brain is fried and you know it
   *Focusing on anything without bright colors and catchy music is impossible.*

4. You scrolled for 5 hours yesterday and got nothing from it
   *Your phone drains your energy without giving you anything in return.*

5. You picked up your phone while reading this
   *No notifications. No news. Nothing important. You picked it up anyway.*

## The reframe

6. Boredom is a withdrawal symptom
   *Think of it like an addicted mind looking for its next hit.*

7. The biggest sign of taste is restraint
   *It's tasteful to not have your phone out all the time.*

8. Everything you want is gated behind focus
   *Effort and focus are what allow you to achieve anything. Everything.*

9. Boredom stopped being uncomfortable once I did this
   *Once you can sit with yourself and just listen, something changes.*

10. You don't have an attention problem, you have a phone problem
    *7 rules I follow to stop my phone from destroying my focus.*

## The curiosity

11. Ian Fleming's daily routine while writing James Bond
    *Once you can sit with yourself, boredom lets you get creative.*

12. 7 rules I follow so my phone doesn't destroy my focus
    *Rule 3: I don't check it until lunch.*

13. Why I don't check my phone until lunch
    *Any urgent stuff I answer on my computer.*

14. I stopped listening to music when I walk. Here's what happened.
    *Listening to yourself is a skill, and skills need training.*

15. The death of the quiet moment
    *You're at the cafe. It's been thirty seconds. Somehow your phone spawned into your hand.*

## The identity

16. You have no notifications, no news, nothing important. So why are you scrolling?
    *An endless feed generated by a computer with an algorithm.*

17. Somehow your phone spawned into your hand
    *You have no notifications. There is nothing of actual importance.*

18. You're anxious because you're not where you want to be
    *So get where you want to be instead of anesthetizing yourself.*

19. The painting, the instrument, the book. You know which ones.
    *All the unfinished projects you have. Don't you want to finish them?*

20. Creativity is a muscle you haven't trained in months
    *At first you produce terrible ideas. Then slightly less terrible ones. Then a masterpiece.*
