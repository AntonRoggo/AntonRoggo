## Story logic pass (workflows/story-ad-logic.md)

Chain: the chair, the flirt read, the wife, the comment, "you really don't know anything," the refusal, Everett leaves → the guess list → "please tell me" / "you don't need it" / "look, here" → the effort and the money → "I've been exactly where you are" → the flashback and the chain → "understand something first" → surface vs. drain → the diagnostic by touch → "so you ARE on the shots" / "needles?" / "adorable" → the bottle → Brazilian women, collecting programs → "why this" / "it drains" → the mechanism, the herbs, the dose → "you need the drain open, you're not lazy, you're backed up" → where, copycats, six weeks → "start before" → "Damn." / "You."

What it caught and what I did:
1. **The source's "watch / wait / where did he go / exactly"** is a cream absorbing on camera. A bottle of drops has no demo. The locker room's diagnostic by touch is the one thing a fit stranger can do at a pool that proves her point on Lorraine's own body, and it lands on the source's own "Exactly."
2. **Checklist 4, the guide.** Camille is living proof ("three years ago that chair was mine, forty pounds heavier") and she is a link in a chain ("a woman on that chair over there said to me exactly what I'm about to say to you"), which is 005's device. She has no clinic and needs none; she had it.
3. **Checklist 11, why Camille talks to a woman who just insulted her.** The source has the same problem and solves it the same way: she refuses first ("No. Not after that comment."), and only tells after Lorraine asks three times ("please tell me," "can you please tell me now," "please"). Kept. The refusal is what makes the telling worth something.
4. **The operator's accountability rule.** Lorraine's confession ("I do an hour every morning... I swear") puts her effort on the record before the mechanism. Camille's absolution is the bank's, one word changed: "You're not lazy. You're backed up." Camille's own flashback has one concession-shaped line ("Everett stopped reaching for me at night, and I let him") which is about the marriage, not her body; it stays because it is the moment Lorraine's face changes.
5. **Arithmetic.** Both in their 50s; Camille three years out, forty pounds; "after fifty it slows down" fits both. Six weeks is the only claim, and it is in the last line of the sale ("Thank me in six weeks"), the bank's clock.
6. **The shots, for this audience.** Present as the wrong guess ("anyone can look like that on the shots") and as the source's needle joke. Camille never runs them down beyond "I didn't," which is the source's line.
7. **Checklist 3, hooks and body.** All three end on "Oh honey. It's not the shots." and the hold opens on "You really don't know anything, do you." V2 and V3 are Lorraine's V.O. over the same shot, so the scene is one scene.

Watch:
- The chain link is now "a woman on that chair over there," three years ago, at this pool. The resort is the Brazil slot, literally; Camille comes back every year.
- "Forty pounds" is Camille's number, not a Lymphoria claim, and it makes "that chair was mine" believable.
- Everett's exit and return are the only staging in the scene. He leaves so the women are alone; he comes back with three drinks so the last beat has a witness. Cut them and the scene is two chairs and a bottle, which also works.
