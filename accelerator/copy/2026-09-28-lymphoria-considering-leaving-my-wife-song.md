## 4. Story logic pass (workflows/story-ad-logic.md)

Chain: the cookout, the words on screen → that night, the post, "asshole" → I love her, more after the baby → the baby weight never left, then more → her head got fixed, her body didn't → five two, two sixty, heart disease, "she will die" → the list she turned down → the kitchen after the boy's asleep, "at least try," "I have tried," the separating line, "see him at thirteen," "...alright, I hear you" → the hall mirror → Jamal on the jog: Bri was worse, asked at church, the gym won't fix it, it's not fat → the drain, no pump, the pregnancy slowed it and it never came back, three years, stuck, hardens, her knees → four herbs, nineteen in six weeks → the relay, "drain it out how," the offer, "for me," "...alright, I'll try it" → Day 1 to Week 6, the week-three hurdle, 14, 19 → sneakers at the door, the walk, "my wife" → Labor Day, same yard, no whisper → the CTA, 35 in five months.

What it caught and what I did:
1. **Hook chooses the body (checklist 3).** All three hooks are the same cookout and the same words, so the body works under any of them, the way the tailgate's three hooks are one tailgate. H2's aunt and H3's cousins both return on Labor Day, so whichever hook runs, the payoff is theirs.
2. **The witness hears, the lead doesn't (tailgate rule).** In H3 she is cutting up a hot dog, oblivious. In H2 she hears the aunt but only says "I'm not," and the reader learns the rest from the words on screen. The post is his, not hers.
3. **The ultimatum is the source's and it stays.** The Reddit says he told her he was thinking about separating and that it "put the nail in the coffin." The song keeps it as the thing he says and hates saying ("I don't want to be that dude"), and the CTA closes it: "And I never had to leave." Whoever wants a softer Chris cuts one line, "I've been thinking about leaving," and the talk still works on "I can't watch you slowly kill yourself."
4. **Her effort and the refusal both stand.** The Reddit has her turning everything down. The song keeps the list and her refusal, and gives her one line in the kitchen, "I have tried," which the source implies with "multiple conversations." The absolution then comes from the mechanism, not from him: it's not fat, it's waste, the gym won't fix it. No line says she ate wrong.
5. **The guide (checklist 4).** Jamal's credential is the tailgate's: his wife had it worse, he learned the herbs from her. "How do you even know all this?" / "Because Bri found these drops." "Asked when she was due at church, two years after" is the tailgate's "looked 10 months pregnant," pointed at H2.
6. **The clog cause is the birth, and it absolves.** "Carrying a baby slows it down, and after the baby it never gets back up." Three years is the build. Her knees are the source's detail and here they carry the waste, which is why the walk is at her pace later.
7. **Depression beat.** "I thought it was her head. She got help for that. She's been fine up there since." Four lines, the source's own, and they are what makes "her body's a different story" land. They are not a knock; they say the mind was fixed and the weight still climbed. Cut all four if it reads wrong; nothing downstream depends on them.
8. **Arithmetic.** The boy is 3; "see him at thirteen" is the source's "teenager." Fourth of July to Labor Day is nine weeks, the drops are six, so Labor Day is after the montage. 19 at week six, 35 at five months in the CTA, both under the ceiling. Five two, two sixty is the source's number, kept because it is the stake.
9. **Week three (checklist: the honest beat turns upward).** The source's "first month was a hurdle and a half." The scale barely moves, she says "I told you," he puts the bottle next to her coffee every morning. It is the only dialogue in the montage and it is what "for me" bought.
10. **Same event, recognized.** Same yard, same aunt, same cousins, no whisper. Nobody fails to know her; the yard goes quiet, which is the tailgate's double take.
11. **The lead narrates and gives credit.** Tailgate: "my own son had to be the one to tell me, and he was right." Here the credit goes to the strangers and to her: "I asked strangers... then I had the hardest talk... I'm proud to say." The source's update closes on thanking the commenters, so does the CTA's first line.

Watch:
- "Stop the drive-through" is the source's "Not buy fast food." It is the one habit named, and it is on the list she turned down, so it reads as his ask, not her fault. If it still sounds like blame, "cook with me."
- Dose is the tailgate's "twice a day, under the tongue." Still the account's open ruling.
- Guarantee: 30 days, the tailgate's own.
- "Asked when she was due at church" makes Jamal's wife's story rhyme with H2. If H2 doesn't run, it still works as the tailgate's "10 months pregnant."
