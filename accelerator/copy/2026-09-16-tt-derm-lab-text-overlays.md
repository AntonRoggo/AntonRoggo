# Text-on-screen overlays: "dermatology research lab" hook

Audio: "A dermatology research lab just published something that explains why some women's skin ages faster, even when the skincare routine is exactly the same. If you're over 40 and your cream stopped working, this is going to piss you off. Because it pissed me off."

Overlay rules: short, open loop, complements the audio, not a transcript of it. Customer language where it exists: "aged so fast," "change overnight" (Reddit), "why has my face aged so fast" (Google autocomplete). Note the research finding that dermatologists mostly don't back tallow, so the overlay carries the finding, not a dermatologist endorsement.

---

## The loop (why some women age faster)

1. Why some women age faster
2. Same routine. Different face. Here's why.
3. Why her skin aged faster than yours
4. The reason your face aged so fast
5. Two women, same routine, ten years apart

## The finding

6. New research: why your cream stopped working
7. What the lab found about skin after 40
8. The study that explains your face
9. Published last month. Read it before you buy another cream.

## The anger

10. This pissed me off
11. This is going to make you mad
12. Nobody told us this

## The twist (same routine)

13. It was never your routine
14. Your routine isn't the problem. This is.
15. Same cream for 20 years. Here's what changed.

## Pattern interrupt

16. Skin ages faster after 40. Not why you think.
17. Why nothing works after 40

---

My picks: #2 for the loop, #14 for the twist, #10 for the anger.

Avoid: anything that says "dermatologists recommend" or "dermatologist-approved." The 2025 JCD analysis found 7% of dermatologists recommend tallow; the audio names a lab, not an endorsement, and the overlay should stay there.
