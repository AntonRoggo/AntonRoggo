- "Okay, where do I get this?" / offer line / "So I'll leave the link right here for you."

### Angle-specific modifications
- Bloated and tired → dry and itchy. Chaga → beef tallow. Mechanism: your skin stopped making its own oil.
- Foggy focus → lines and wrinkles. Lion's mane → frankincense.
- Dull skin, dry, achy joints → dull skin and breakouts. Collagen → olive oil (glow) plus raw honey and beeswax (hydration, breakouts).
- Stressed, moods → extremely sensitive, irritated easily. L-theanine → nothing synthetic, five ingredients. The prescription is an absence, which is the twist.
- Pills → greasy. "No pill here" → "Not greasy."
- Coffee → F-Balm. "Ready in less than 30 seconds" → "you only need a pea-sized amount" (Made in Canada winner).
- "Tastes really good" → "smells really good." 441 frankincense and 282 honey reviews are mostly about the scent.
- "Huge improvements" → "my brother asked if I'd had Botox." Verbatim review.
- 100,000 reviews → 15,000, operator's current claim.

### Replacements required
- Sale, free VIP starter kit, free express shipping → made in-house in Canada, money-back guarantee, 3 to 5 day shipping (client file). TT runs no bonus.
- Any mechanism claim with no source. Sebum decline is in a review; nothing else per-ingredient is, beyond the single reviews cited below.
