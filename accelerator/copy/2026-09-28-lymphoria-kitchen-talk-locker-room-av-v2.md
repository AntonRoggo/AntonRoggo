What it caught and what I did:
1. **Effort on record before the guide.** Three weeks, every morning, the app, the hill, three pounds. The source's "first month was a hurdle and a half" is now that beat. The guide never tells her to stop; she tells her why it isn't counting.
2. **Chris stays right.** He asked her to try. She tried. The drops made the trying work. Week five he is on the hill with her, the source's walks. The anniversary is his beat: "You look like you," the tailgate wife's line.
3. **No cardio blame.** The cause is said in four nouns, "Baby. No sleep. Weight. Stress," and "the two of them fed each other" is the spiral. The treadmill appears only inside the locker-room button line, "fighting a clogged drain with a treadmill," which is about where the effort went, not that it hurt her.
4. **The cortisol block is the aunt's, word for word** from "cooking your cortisol sky high" to "you have to drain it," with the slots filled: three years for ten months, "for him" for the sister, "mama" for "baby." One deviation from the blame-transfer rule to flag: the rule wants a villain with a face. There is no villain here, so "they did this to you" becomes "that's what the last three years did to you. Not your head. Your body." The absolution still lands outside her.
5. **The guide can know (checklist 4).** The locker-room credential unchanged: bigger two years ago, already in this gym, nothing moved, drained it. She sits down next to Kayla; nobody touches a stop button.
6. **Hook chooses the body.** V1 says "I decided I was leaving" and the kitchen opens on "I'm not going anywhere," which is the turn the hook promised. V2 promises the locker room in three weeks; delivered at week three. V3 promises the dress; delivered at the anniversary. No Reddit card.
7. **The POV switch is announced, not sneaked.** "He doesn't know the rest of this. I never told him about the locker room." One line, and it pays off at the anniversary: "When did this happen?" / "You were there for all of it." He was: the shoes at the door, the hill, watching her sleep.
8. **Arithmetic.** 260. Three weeks, 257. Six weeks on drops: 246 at week four, 241 at week six. Labor Day is nine weeks after the Fourth: three weeks of gym plus six on drops. The anniversary in November is five months from July: 225, the source's 35. Pre-baby weight is therefore about 225, which is what "the baby weight, then more" adds up to. Under four a week throughout. Aunt Bev asks "when are you due" of a 260-pound woman with a hard dome; on Labor Day at 241 with the dome gone she says "look at you," not "who is that."
9. **The kitchen keeps the list and the stake, and drops the ultimatum.** Operator ruling: he doesn't want to leave her. V1 has him deciding to leave and the kitchen has him choosing not to. "I'm not going anywhere" is the line that makes that a turn.
10. **Numbers are the bank's and the source's.** 14 and 19 (tailgate). 35 and five months (Reddit). 260 at 5'2 (Reddit). No invented deaths: "heart disease runs in your family" stays as the source has it.
11. **Vocabulary.** Waste, backup, drain, sludge implied by "hardened," "not fat." No fluid, no trapped, no "spiral" on screen, no "resets cortisol." Red clover calms the swelling.
