# Comparison line: F-Balm vs water-based cream

Modeled on "you'd have to eat 10 pounds of blueberries to get the antioxidant power of one month of Everyday Dose." One absurd quantity, tied to the ad's single mechanism (water). Slots in at HOLD 4, replacing or extending "So you're paying for 70 to 80 percent water."

Math: cream at 75% water means 25% ingredients. F-Balm at 0% water means 100% ingredients. Same jar size, four jars of cream equals one jar of F-Balm in real ingredients. At 70% water it's about three jars, at 80% it's five.

---

1. You'd have to buy four jars of that $57 face cream to get the amount of actual skincare ingredients in one jar of F-Balm.

2. Ounce for ounce, F-Balm has four times the ingredients of that $57 cream. Because there's no water in it.

3. Four jars. That's how many of that $57 cream you'd need to match the ingredients in one jar of F-Balm. The rest is water you could have gotten from your sink.

4. To get the same amount of real ingredients that's in one jar of F-Balm, you'd need four jars of that cream. Four times the price for the same thing, plus a lot of water.

5. Buy that $57 cream four times and you'll finally have as much actual skincare as one jar of F-Balm. Or skip the water.
