7. **Amazon out.** The bank's warning instead.
8. **Hooks.** V2 pays off (five years, three months, the refusal). V3 pays off ("a heavier woman's ID, it was mine" by "where did it go, out"). Nobody calls security and nobody whispers "impersonate." All three land on "I have been out in that line two hours."
