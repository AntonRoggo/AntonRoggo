## Flags before this runs
- **Vaseline, 1859, oil rigs in Pennsylvania:** documented. Robert Chesebrough, rod wax from the drilling rigs, patented 1872.
- **Rockefeller's Standard Oil and byproducts:** Standard Oil's near-total control of US refining by the 1880s is documented. That petroleum byproducts (mineral oil, petrolatum, paraffin) became cosmetic ingredients is documented. That Rockefeller personally put them in face cream is not. The line as written stays on the defensible side. Don't sharpen it further without a source.
- **"Most of what isn't water came out of the ground":** the strongest version of the claim, per the Script's compliance rule. Not verified against any specific product. Your team's call.
- **"It's in traditional Chinese medicine":** you asked for it. I have no source for tallow specifically in TCM. Animal fats appear in old Chinese pharmacopeias, but I can't cite tallow. Keep the line only if you have a reference; otherwise "for thousands of years" and "every pioneer household" carry the beat.
- **Guarantee:** Organised says 100 days. The client file says contact support within 30 days. I wrote "full refund, no questions asked" without a window.
- **The husband line** is a real customer's sentence used as the narrator's own experience. Same move as the Karen scripts. If this films with a real customer, it has to be her real reaction.
