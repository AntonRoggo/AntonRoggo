# Email headlines: You Need a More Beautiful Day

10 subject lines + preview texts.

---

## The confrontation

1. If I wanted to be miserable, this is what I would do
   *Wake up in filth. Check my phone. Skip breakfast. Repeat.*

2. You're too busy to live your own life
   *And the boring days are proof.*

3. Your morning is rushed, ugly, and overstimulated
   *That's not a personality trait. That's a design flaw.*

## The reframe

4. Beauty should be the default
   *Most of life is having the energy to pay attention.*

5. You don't have to be rich or unemployed to have a beautiful day
   *Some of the nicest things you can have are cheap or free.*

6. Everything can be beautiful if you stop rushing through it
   *Your breakfast. Your room. Your walk to work. All of it.*

## The challenge

7. Take a long hard look at your life and ask yourself one question
   *Am I doing everything I actually want to do?*

8. 75% of my paycheque went to food when I made 2k a month
   *It wasn't a luxury. It was the first thing I fixed.*

9. I signed up for rock climbing a month ago. This weekend I climbed outdoors for the first time.
   *Most people know what they want. They just make excuses.*

## The close

10. Don't wake up like you're late to your own life
    *Everything can wait. I promise.*
