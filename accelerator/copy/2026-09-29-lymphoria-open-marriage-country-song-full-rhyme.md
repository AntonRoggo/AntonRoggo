SCENE 1: THE MARRIAGE
(Visual: a small apartment, early 2000s. Young Diane, nineteen, in a modest dress, holding baby Brooke, watching Ray leave with a briefcase. Cut through the years: Diane at the stove, a plate covered in foil, the kitchen clock past ten. Then Ray today, forty-three, in a new suit with a belly under it, leaning on the doorframe of his office while a young secretary laughs at his desk.)
Well, I married at nineteen with Brooke already on the way
Both our mamas said the vows, and we did what they would say
My daddy set Ray up with a business down in town
And I learned to keep the house and keep my pretty head down
Twenty-two years I kept that house and did what I was told
Twenty-two years he came home late and ate his supper cold
And the money bought him fancy suits, a belly, and his pride
And a brand-new secretary sittin' pretty by his side

SCENE 6: THE REVEAL

You cannot burn waste, you have to drain it, understand?
