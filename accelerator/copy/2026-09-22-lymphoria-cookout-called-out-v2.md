## Logic pass on the new ending

- **Andre needs a reason to be there and a reason not to know.** Derrick's cousin, first time at the house, asks Derrick who she is. Holds. He may not know she is the hostess until Marcus walks up, which is the point.
- **Marcus's re-noticing (weeks 2 to 5) now reads as too late, not as reconciliation.** Each beat has her not responding: she keeps moving, she doesn't answer, she looks at him politely. The viewer is never asked to want him back.
- **Checklist 11, she is not cruel.** "I know" and turning back to Andre is the most she does to him. He did the rest to himself, drunk, in front of everyone. The drunk framing (ruling 3) is what keeps him a dickhead and not a monster, which keeps the ad from being about divorce.
- **The hook V.O. still holds:** "the only reason he looks at me the way he looks at her now" is true. He does. She just isn't looking back.
- **Arithmetic unchanged:** 43, 45, 47, 48, three years of bloat, since 45, two years since the back touch, twenty years married, the dress since 43, six weeks.
- **Herb origin claim gone.** "My auntie's used all four for forty years" is a person's practice, not a nation's history. Nothing to google.
- **Every single day** matches the dropper.
