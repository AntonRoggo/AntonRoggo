## Story logic pass (workflows/story-ad-logic.md)

Chain: the refusal, the photo, "because I drained it" → two hours, "my own face," "lower your voice," the supervisor → "it IS her" → "what did you do," the guess list, the sister on the shots → twelve programs, nine years, counting → "stamp my paper and I will tell you" → the bottle, "where did it go, out" → never fat, waste, the drain, no pump, after fifty, can't burn waste → liquid, the number, the first week → six weeks, three times → the belly at three, the stillingia → Brenda's appointment, the shots, "once you start you don't stop," "I wanted my face back" → "we have been lied to" → "what if it doesn't work," sixty days → the name, "now stamp my paper" → the offer.

What it caught and what I did:
1. **The absorbing demo.** "Give me your hand, where did it go, in" is the source's proof. For drops there is no hand demo, but the beat is the same question with the opposite answer, and "out" is the mechanism's whole argument. Kept the shape, flipped the word.
2. **Non-recognition, inside the ruling.** A clerk comparing a stranger to a four-year-old license photo is the one case where "you are not her" is realistic, and the supervisor settles it in three lines. Nothing to defend.
3. **Checklist 4, the guide.** The woman is living proof standing at the counter with the photo of her old self in the clerk's hand. No credential needed; the ID is the before picture.
4. **Checklist 1, hooks.** V1 is the source's open with one line changed. V2's "the photo is me, six weeks ago" is paid off by "six weeks" three times. V3's "a heavier woman's ID, it was mine" is paid off by "where did it go, out." All three land on "I have been out in that line two hours."
5. **The accountability rule.** She never concedes. "Twelve programs, nine years" is the effort on record; "it was never fat, it was waste" is the absolution, in her own mouth, because in this format she is the guide.
6. **Arithmetic.** 61, nine years of trying, six weeks on the drops, the belly at week three, the appointment in April. The license photo is old enough for the face to have changed and recent enough to be the same woman; the source never dates it and neither do I.
7. **The enemy.** The shots, twice: the sister who spends $1,400 a month and does not look like her, and Brenda's own April appointment. "It starves you while everything underneath stays stuck" is the mechanism's logic against the shots, one for one with the source's "it freezes the top while everything underneath keeps going."
8. **Checklist 11.** Brenda refuses, gets it wrong three times, and is typing the name into her phone at the end. That is the source's arc for the clerk and it is the conversion beat.

Watch:
- "$1,400 a month" is the shots' price band, used twice. "Seventy percent off, buy two get one free" is the operator's own offer from the male close. Sixty days is the account's current end card; still one number to pick.
- No pound number anywhere. The source had none, and "where did it go, out" plus "the belly at three" does the work. If the operator wants one, the woman's line "twenty-two pounds in six weeks" fits the ceiling.
- The register is general-market (Brenda, Marilyn, "ma'am"). For the Black ICP the names change and the counter stays.

## Suggestions, not applied

1. **The photo's age.** One line from Brenda ("This photo is four years old") would date the before and make "six weeks" hit harder. Left out because the source never dates it.
2. **The sister.** Brenda's sister on the shots is the best line in the scene and never returns. "Tell your sister" from the woman as she leaves is three words.
