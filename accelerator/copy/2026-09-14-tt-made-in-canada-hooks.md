## Which to run first
- **#22, #23, #26** from the plain set. #22 and #23 ride the buying-Canadian habit the audience already has without naming who they're avoiding. #26 is a customer's own sentence and the only hook in the file that is pure proof.
- **#1 and #4** are the safest and widest of the first set. No politics, no numbers, one visible action (checking a label) that 78% of the audience just did themselves.
- **#10** is the loudest and sits in the proven political lane. Highest ceiling, and it will cost some US customers if it leaks across the border.
- **#14** is the only one that uses the "not in China" half of the body's opener. The rest lean on the US half, which is where the news is.
- **#18** is time-bound. The mill closes September 15. After that it's "closed last week," then it's stale.
