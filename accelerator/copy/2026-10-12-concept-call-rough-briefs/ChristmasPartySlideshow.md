#### THE IDEA
On the call: holiday work parties make good stories, but stay away from the classic "mistaken for pregnant." The ICP is a woman in her 40s who has carried an office, a family and ten years of stress, and stopped seeing herself somewhere along the way. At the office Christmas party the boss plays her 10-year anniversary slideshow, her year-one photo fills the screen, a new hire asks "who's that?" and someone laughs "that's her!" Nobody is cruel, which is why it lands: the room just doesn't recognize her. The guide is the coworker at the next desk who was in the same spot last Christmas and got herself back. Payoff is the New Year kickoff work trip six weeks later, where the same new hire asks the same question and nobody laughs.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Realistic AI drama. No voiceover. Every beat is two people talking on screen. `Orange code lines` are visuals. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
*   **V1:** Version 1 + Hold Body.
*   **V2:** Version 2 + Hold Body.
*   **V3:** Version 3 + Hold Body.

**CAST**
Leads (hooks and hold)
*   **Nicole:** White, 44. Office manager at a mid-size insurance office in Columbus. Started ten years ago at the front desk, the girl who ran at lunch; promoted to run the whole office in year four. Twin boys, 9, and her dad's doctor visits on her calendar too. Before: about 30 pounds up, puffy face, jaw gone soft, belly pushing against her black wrap dress, wedding ring stuck. After: same woman, flat stomach, jaw back, the same wrap dress sitting right. Never skinny.
*   **Denise:** Black, 51. Payroll, twelve years at the office, the desk next to Nicole's. Last Christmas she was heavier than Nicole; by summer she was lean with a sharp jaw, and Nicole never asked. Her sister has run an herb shop in Tucson for twenty years.

Other characters
*   **Greg:** White, 58. The boss. Loud, big-hearted, makes the anniversary slideshow every year.
*   **Tyler:** 24, new hire in claims, three weeks in. Sweet, no filter.
*   **Mark:** White, 46. Nicole's husband. Kind, tired.
*   **Coworker:** 30s, from sales.
*   **Party guests (about 40):** the whole office, mixed, 20s to 60s.
