#### THE IDEA
This replaces HerbalistConstipation. On the call the team said constipation is about twice the size of the odor market, gets worse in winter, and skews older. Instead of the herbalist drama, this runs our WhatHappensWhen MOF format (W40) as a song: a narrator walks one woman through 30 days on Lymphoria, one herb's job at each step. The ICP is a woman over 50 who already knows the fixes (fiber, prunes, laxatives) and is sick of forcing it. Mechanism from the call: stuck lymph waste holds on to the water the gut needs, so everything dries out and stops; a laxative forces it once, draining lets the body move on its own. The emotional core: she sat in the car at her grandson's birthday, and 30 days later she sits in the bleachers the whole game.
