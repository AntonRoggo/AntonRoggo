#### THE IDEA
Halloween ideas from the call: the dad who gets tired after one block, the dad who drives the truck while the kids walk, the costume that doesn't fit. Last Halloween his kids picked matching superhero suits; his wouldn't zip, he made it one block and followed in the truck while their mom walked them, and his son told a friend "my dad can't walk far." The emotional core: a dad whose kids already think of him as the one who stays behind. Six weeks before this Halloween, the neighbor dad who sat in his car at the same corner last year shows up lean, and shows him the drain. Payoff: Halloween night, he walks every street.
