#### THE IDEA
From the call: recovering alcoholics carry a strong identity ("a year clean"). They are proud and still fighting, and weight loss is the angle: drain what the years left in your body. ICP: men over 40 in recovery, one to a few years sober. It could work because the trigger is a moment every one of them knows, the chip photo, and the fear behind his daughter's question. Tone stays respectful: sobriety is the win, the body just has to catch up.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Song ad. Dale sings every plain line, including his daughter's and his sponsor's words. `Orange code lines` are visuals for the editor, never sung. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
*   **V1:** Version 1 + Hold Body.
*   **V2:** Version 2 + Hold Body.
*   **V3:** Version 3 + Hold Body.

**CAST**
Leads (hooks and hold)

*   **The men at the meeting (about 15):** mixed, 30s to 70s, folding chairs, coffee cups.
