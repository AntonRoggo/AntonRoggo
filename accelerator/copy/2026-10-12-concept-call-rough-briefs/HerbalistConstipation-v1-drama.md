#### THE IDEA
On the call the team said song ads may not work for constipation and pitched authority instead: a practitioner saying these are the four herbs we give people when they're constipated, without ruining their gut with pills. The constipation market is about twice the size of the odor market, it gets worse in winter, and it skews older. The ICP is women over 50 with laxatives in their purse who are too embarrassed to say it out loud. Anton's new mechanism carries it: stuck lymph waste holds on to the water the gut needs, so everything dries out and stops; a laxative forces it once, draining lets the body move on its own again. The emotional core: she did everything right and still sat in the car during her grandson's birthday.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Authority drama, realistic AI. Ines, an herbalist, talks to one client in her shop. Every beat is two people talking on screen, no voiceover. `Orange code lines` are visuals. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
