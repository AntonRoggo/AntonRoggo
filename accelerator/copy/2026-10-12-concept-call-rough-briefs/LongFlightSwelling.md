**ROUGH BRIEF: idea from the Oct 12 concept call. V numbers and editor get assigned later.**

#### THE IDEA
A teammate on the call told us about his 16-hour flight: older passengers doing lymph stretches by the bathroom, and after landing his own feet swelled and his ring got stuck. The ICP is women over 50 who fly long-haul to see kids and grandkids who moved abroad. Everyone that age knows the moment: shoes that won't go back on after the flight. Here it happens in front of the son's new in-laws, the people she most wanted to impress. The emotional core: she saved a year to meet her grandchild and arrived as the old one who needs a stool. The guide is a retired flight attendant she meets stretching by the bathroom on the flight home, so the reason to know comes built in.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Song ad. Loretta sings every plain line, including other people's quotes. `Orange code lines` are visuals for the editor, never sung. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.

**CAST**
Leads (hooks and hold)
*   **Loretta:** White, 63. Retired school secretary from Ohio, widowed, raised one son. Proud she never missed one of his games. Saved a year to fly to Sydney and meet her first grandchild.
