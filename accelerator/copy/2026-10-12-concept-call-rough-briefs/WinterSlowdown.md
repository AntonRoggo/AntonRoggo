#### THE IDEA
On the call: in winter people move less, eat comfort food and stay inside, so position Lymphoria as the thing that keeps the drain moving while you've slowed down. The ICP is a woman in her 40s who puts on about 15 pounds every winter and never takes all of it off. By spring her jeans from last year don't close, and every year she tells herself she'll lose it in the spring. This year her neighbor, who does lymph drainage for a living, finally says it out loud and shows her. The emotional core is the yearly hiding: the hoodie on the beach, the husband's coat on the driveway. Payoff is the spring break beach trip in March, hoodie off.
