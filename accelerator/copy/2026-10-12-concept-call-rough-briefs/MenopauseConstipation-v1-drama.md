#### THE IDEA
Serena flagged the link between constipation and estrogen on the call. The ICP is menopausal women who started getting backed up when their periods stopped and now hide it from everyone, even their oldest friends. The story is the annual girls' weekend at a lake house: she secretly hasn't gone in five days, skips dinner, and sneaks laxatives. Her college roommate, who was the one not swimming last year, catches it and tells her what she learned. It runs on the menopause mechanism plus the water logic from HerbalistConstipation. The emotional core is the shame of hiding your body from the women who know you best, and the relief when one of them says "last year it was me."
