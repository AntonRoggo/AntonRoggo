**ROUGH BRIEF: idea from the Oct 12 concept call. V numbers and editor get assigned later.**

#### THE IDEA
From the call: dads of kids 1 to 5 put on weight and feel bad leaving the family to go to the gym. The real hit is identity, a dad whose kid stops looking up to him. ICP: dads in their 30s and 40s with little kids. It could work because the trigger is innocent: a 4-year-old just says what he sees, and every dad has felt the gym guilt. Emotional core: he gave four years of nights to his son, and his son calls another man strong.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Song ad. Kevin sings every plain line, including other people's words. `Orange code lines` are visuals for the editor, never sung. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
*   **V1:** Version 1 + Hold Body.
*   **V2:** Version 2 + Hold Body.
*   **V3:** Version 3 + Hold Body.

**CAST**
Leads (hooks and hold)

Before: belly pushing out over his belt, puffy face, wedding ring stuck, T-shirts tight across the middle, red in the face three ropes up. After: same man, belly down, jaw back, belt two notches in, climbs the ropes with his boy. Never skinny.

*   **Kevin's wife:** White, 34. Tired, kind, notices everything.
*   **The other dad:** Mixed, mid-30s. Fit, tank top, friendly.
