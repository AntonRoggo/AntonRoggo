#### THE IDEA
Stefan's idea. People who smoked for 20 years are very proud they quit, and smoking holds hunger down, so they gain weight after they quit. Our woman quits after 22 years for her grandkids, the whole family cheers, then she puts on 25 pounds and her sister jokes she should go back to the Marlboros. The emotional core: she did the hardest thing of her life and got punished for it. The friend from her quit group who lived the same thing shows her the drain, and the payoff is the family reunion photo.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Song ad. Darlene sings every plain line, including other people's quotes. `Orange code lines` are visuals for the editor, never sung. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
*   **V1:** Version 1 + Hold Body.
*   **V2:** Version 2 + Hold Body.
*   **V3:** Version 3 + Hold Body.
