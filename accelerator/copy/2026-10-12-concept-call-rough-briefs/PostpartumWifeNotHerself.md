#### THE IDEA
From the call: take the menopause angle where the husband feels his wife is not herself, and move it to postpartum, told by the man. ICP: moms one to three years after a baby, reached through the husband who sees it. It could work because she will not say it out loud, but he can, and every line he sings is love, not blame. Emotional core: the woman who would not be in a single photo for two years hands him her phone and says take my picture.
