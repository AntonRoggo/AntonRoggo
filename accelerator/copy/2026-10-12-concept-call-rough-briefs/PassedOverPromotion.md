#### THE IDEA
From the call: identity loss at work. A man who was the best at his job gets worn down and outrun by younger guys. Aimed at boomer men who would rather take herbs than pills. Dale, 58, gave 28 years to a building-supply company, and the regional manager job goes to a 31-year-old who calls him "big guy" in front of the whole room. That night his wife finds the photo from his first award: he was 31 too. The core is the man who built the place getting treated like furniture. Stress mechanism with the full blame transfer: the job did this to him, he did not do it to himself.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
Realistic AI drama. Every beat is two people talking on screen. No voiceover. `Orange code lines` are visuals. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.

**CAST**
Leads (hooks and hold)
*   **Dale Brandt:** White, 58. 28 years at Hollister Supply, a building-supply company in Ohio. Top salesman eleven years running, ran the region in everything but title, sat across from the men he hired and laid them off himself in 2009. Pressed khakis, the tie bar from his first award. Before: belly pushing over his belt, sport coat that won't button, puffy face, jaw gone soft, wedding ring stuck. After: same man, flat stomach, sharp jaw, navy suit buttoned. Never skinny.
*   **Hank Morrow:** White, 64. Dale's old sales partner for twenty years, retired two years ago. At his retirement party he was heavier than Dale. Now lean, sharp jaw, fishes every morning. His wife has been an herbalist for thirty years, and he ignored her for most of them.
*   **Joanne Brandt:** White, 56. Dale's wife of 32 years. Sharp, funny, always on his side.
*   **Tyler Voss:** White, 31. The new regional manager. Fitted vest, AirPods, calls everyone "big guy." Not evil, just young.
*   **Kessler:** White, 60s. The VP. Golf tan, avoids eye contact.
*   **The sales team (about 12):** mixed, 25 to 40.
*   **The conference crowd (about 300):** company sales staff in a hotel ballroom.
