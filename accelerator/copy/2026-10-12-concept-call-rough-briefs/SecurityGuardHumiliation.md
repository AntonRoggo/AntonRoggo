#### THE IDEA
Anton on the call: we tested husbands getting beat up and veterans, but never a man whose job is to protect getting humiliated in public. Reggie, a 52-year-old Black mall guard, chases a teen shoplifter and gasps out after 30 feet, right in front of his wife and daughter at pickup, while kids film it. A 25-year-old guard makes the catch and the boss turns the video into a joke. The ICP is Black men 45 to 60 whose pride is being the one who handles it. The emotional core: the protector who couldn't, seen by the two people he protects most. Mechanism is "not moving": fourteen years in a chair on the camera desk.

Realistic AI drama. Every beat is two people talking on screen. No voiceover. `Orange code lines` are visuals. Each version is one hook + the full Hold Body.
