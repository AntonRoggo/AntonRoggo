**ROUGH BRIEF: idea from the Oct 12 concept call. V numbers and editor get assigned later.**

#### THE IDEA
On the call: we need more identity-loss stories for women, not only respect stories, built on women who were known for something (cheerleaders, dancers, lifeguards), without reusing the cheerleader reunion. The ICP is a woman over 50 whose whole identity was being the fit one, and menopause took it. She taught the 6 a.m. aerobics class at the rec center for fifteen years; at 54 she walks back in and the 30-year-old who teaches it now kindly tells her to "go at your own pace in the back." The new instructor means well, which is what makes it hurt. Her guide is a former front-row student who now runs an herb shop, so the student becomes the teacher. Payoff: she leads a guest class and the room follows her count again.

#### EDITING GUIDELINES
