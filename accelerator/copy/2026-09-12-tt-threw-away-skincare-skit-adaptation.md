# Surgical adaptation: "Why does your toothbrush look so expensive?" (Tao Clean skit) → "Why did you throw away your skincare?" (F-Balm)

Source: two-person bathroom skit. A notices B's fancy toothbrush, B turns it around and diagnoses A's problems, explains the product, A flips from "I don't want this" to "I need it." Target: A notices B threw out all her skincare, B has one tin.

Speaker labels (A = the friend, B = the owner) added for readability. The source transcript had none.

---

## Phase 1: Surgical analysis

### Preserve exactly
- The inversion structure: A asks about B's product, B answers by interrogating A's symptoms. B never pitches until A asks "so what does this one do?"
- "This, yeah, this is my [product]."
- "It looks kind of [adjective], do I need it?" followed by two yes-or-no symptom questions with "okay" and "wow okay" as A's beats.
- "Yeah, I only came in here to borrow [item] and..."
- "Do you keep your [thing] in one of those..." → "Mmm, yeah" → "The super gross ones that collect [X]" → A names it with a nickname.
- "Okay girl I get it, so what does this one do?"
- Mechanism line with the "without [harsh action] like you're trying to [absurd comparison]" simile.
- "And then when you're done with it... it does all of that at the exact same time."
- "Wait so... IT [DOES X] TOO?" "Mhmm." "Yes!" "YOU GET [BENEFIT] EVERY SINGLE TIME."
- "Okay yeah, I'm impressed."
- "AND THEY [surprise fact]" "WAIT REALLY?!"
- "Yeah, and I mean it just so happens that I was on the website and..."
- "Okay wait yeah never mind. I don't want this. I need it. Imma go get it right now." "Exactly!"

### Angle-specific modifications
- Opening object: expensive toothbrush → the missing skincare (empty shelf or a trash can of bottles) and one tin.
- "Looks kind of fancy" → "looks kind of basic." The tallow tin is plain; the joke inverts.
- Symptoms: gums receding, teeth yellowing → skin tight and dry [R28071], no compliments lately [R30]. Customer-language pass 2026-09-12: "after washing" and "duller" cut, not in the reviews.
- Borrowed item: toothpaste → moisturizer.
- The gross habit: toothbrush in a bathroom cup collecting water and bacteria → a shelf of twelve bottles that are mostly water and fragrance. Nickname: "Bacteria Soup Bowl" → "the water shelf."
- Mechanism: 40,000 strokes, deep clean without sanding your gums → tallow is almost bio-identical to your skin's own oil, absorbs without sitting on your face like frosting a cake.
- The base charges, dries, and UV-cleans at the same time → one tin is your moisturizer, night cream, under-eye, and hand cream at the same time. Supported by the 69 ad's "face, body, chafing under the eyes, WD-40 of skincare."
- "So it cleans the toothbrush too?" → "So it replaces all of them?"
- "Clean brush every single time" → "One tin, every single night."
- "10 different colors" → "five ingredients."
- Official site not Amazon is not in this source; not added.

### Replacements required
- "Massive sale right now with free gifts included." TT has never run a sale or bonus. Replaced with price and the money-back guarantee, the only close the account uses. Kept the "it just so happens I was on the website" setup verbatim.
- 40,000 brush strokes and the UV light. No skin numbers invented. Numbers used: five ingredients, "like 40 bucks" (TT's one stated price range is 40 to 50 dollars).
- No mechanism age is used. The skit never states an age, so none was added.

## Phase 3: Execution

**HOOKS**

V1: Why did you throw away all your skincare?

V2: Wait. Where did all your skincare go? Your whole shelf is empty.

V3: Did you seriously just throw out like $400 of skincare?

**HOLD**

B: This. Yeah, this is my F-Balm.

A: It looks kind of basic. Do I need it?

B: Has your skin started feeling tight and dry?

A: A little bit. Okay.

B: Yeah. When's the last time someone complimented your skin?

A: Wow. Okay. Yeah, I only came in here to borrow moisturizer and...

B: Do you keep your skincare on one of those little bathroom shelves?

A: Mmm.

B: Yeah. The twelve-bottle ones. Where every bottle is mostly water and fragrance.

A: Yeah, the water shelf. Okay girl, I get it. So what does this one do?

B: It's beef tallow. So it's almost bio-identical to the oil your skin already makes. So you get this like really, really deep moisture without sitting on top of your face like you're trying to frost a cake. And then when you're done with it, that's it. That's your moisturizer, your night cream, your under-eye, and your hand cream. It does all of that at the exact same time.

A: Wait, so... SO IT REPLACES ALL OF THEM?

B: Mhmm.

A: Yes!

B: ONE TIN. EVERY SINGLE NIGHT.

A: Okay, yeah. I'm impressed.

B: AND IT'S LIKE FIVE INGREDIENTS. TOTAL.

A: WAIT, REALLY?!

B: Yeah. Tallow, olive oil, honey, beeswax, frankincense. And I mean, it just so happens that I was on the website, and it's like 40 bucks with a money-back guarantee.

A: Okay wait, yeah, never mind. I don't want this. I need it. Imma go get it right now.

B: Exactly!
