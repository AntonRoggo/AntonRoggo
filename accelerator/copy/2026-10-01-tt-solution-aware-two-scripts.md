One: it's rendered fast and hot, so bits of meat and tissue stay in the fat. That's the beef smell. They'll tell you it's grass-fed because their supplier told them. But is it?
Two: it's mixed with who knows what in the process. Commercial rendering bleaches it to control the color and deodorizes it to control the scent. Then it gets cut with seed oils and fillers to get the consistency big producers want.
So instead, ask your tallow company to show you their raw fat. If they can't, don't buy their product!

Then we mix in honey, olive oil, beeswax and frankincense. Five ingredients, mostly tallow. No water, no fillers, no seed oils. That is why it goes on smooth and sinks in instead of sitting on your face.
Use a pea-sized amount on damp skin. If it feels greasy, you're using too much. And if scent bothers you at all, we make an unscented one.
