# 12-beat skin oil script: hooks

20 hooks for the decade-progression script. Four already exist in the script:
1. This is how your skin changes the longer you go past 40 and change nothing.
2. Here's why your skin starts to change after 40.
3. Here's what happens to your skin after 40.
4. A dermatology research lab just published something that explains why some women's skin ages faster, even when the skincare routine is exactly the same. If you're over 40 and your cream stopped working, this is going to piss you off. Because it pissed me off.

---

## The cream stopped working

1. My moisturizer worked for fifteen years. Then I turned 42 and it just stopped. Same jar, same routine, completely different skin.

2. I have spent over a thousand dollars on skincare in the last year and my face looks exactly the same as the day I started. Actually worse.

3. I switched moisturizers four times last year because I thought each one was the problem. None of them were the problem.

4. The cream that kept my skin soft for twenty years is the same cream I'm using now. My skin just stopped responding to it and nobody told me why.

## The mirror

5. I washed my face this morning and the woman in the mirror looked ten years older than the woman I feel like on the inside. I slept eight hours.

6. I caught myself on a Zoom call last week and my first thought was, who is that. Because the face on screen did not match the person behind it.

7. My skin used to bounce back in the morning. Now it just stays where the pillow left it.

## The lines

8. My fine lines got deeper in the last two years even though I'm doing more for my skin than I've ever done. More products, more steps, worse results.

9. My lines aren't deeper because I'm aging faster. They're deeper because the skin around them lost its plumpness. And no moisturizer I own can fix that.

10. I looked at a photo from three years ago and my lines were the same. The difference is the skin around them used to look full. Now the lines just sit in dry skin.

## The anger

11. I found out that the moisturizer I've been buying for eight years is sixty to eighty percent water. I've been paying forty dollars to rub water on my face.

12. A skincare chemist just explained what's actually in your moisturizer and it made me angry. If you're over 40 and your cream stopped working, you should probably hear this too.

13. Nobody told me that after 40 my skin would need a completely different thing than what I'd been buying. I had to find out from a stranger on the internet after wasting hundreds of dollars.

## The comparison

14. My sister is four years older than me and her skin looks ten years younger. We use completely different products. Hers has five ingredients. Mine has thirty-seven.

15. Two women, same age, same routine, completely different skin. The difference is what their moisturizer is actually made of.

## The fact you didn't know

16. Your skin makes an oil that keeps it soft and plump. After 40, it starts making less. After 50, it really drops off. And nothing in your bathroom is designed to replace it.

17. There's a reason your face feels tight after you wash it in the morning. And it's not your cleanser.

18. Every decade after 40, your skin loses about thirty percent of the oil that's been keeping it healthy your whole life. Your moisturizer is sixty to eighty percent water. Do the math.

## The story

19. My friend grabbed my face, pulled it under the kitchen light, and said, "Your skin isn't aging badly. It dried out." I spent the next twenty minutes learning something nobody had ever told me.

20. I almost booked a Botox appointment last month. Then someone explained what was actually happening to my skin after 50. Cancelled the appointment and spent forty dollars instead of six hundred.
