A former heavyweight wrestling champion, used to power, strength and punishing training, gained weight after he retired and found his body no longer recovered the way it used to. He describes feeling lost and insecure. He would drive to the gym alone, and if he saw another car in the lot he would decide it was too crowded and go home. He talks about inventing reasons not to walk in, a way of protecting the strong image he still had of himself. The interview goes on to cover the shift in mindset, training and diet that led to his weight loss.
