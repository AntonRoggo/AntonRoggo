# 04. OfficeScramble (A former assistant pro and long hitter gets humiliated on the first tee of his company's golf outing)

## Source
- Title: The former long hitter loses his drive in front of coworkers
- Publisher: Get Golf Fit / IGD Publishing, Christian Henning first-person commercial fitness manual, pp. 6 to 8 (undated; file hosted in a 2013 directory)
- Link: https://getgolffit.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/Endurance-Builders.pdf

Source summary (our words):
The author had been an assistant golf pro and a scratch player who could outdrive almost anyone. After his mother died, with a child on the way, he left the course for a corporate job. Over about five years of office life and fast food his weight climbed from roughly 200 to 245 pounds, his game slid until he could not break 90, and he kept turning down friends' invitations to play because he was embarrassed by how he looked. When he finally joined coworkers for a round, his distance and his game were gone, and he went home feeling embarrassed and ashamed. That day became his turning point. He spent about five years rebuilding his health, got his distance back and then some, became a certified trainer, and left the job to run his own businesses.
