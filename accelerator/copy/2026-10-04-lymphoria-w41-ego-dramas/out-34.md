Gut over his belt, sport coat that won't button, puffy face, sweats in any room. After: same man, same frame, still big. Flat stomach, broad shoulders, sharp jaw. Still cocky. Never skinny.
* Donna: White, 50, his wife. Pretty, social, the one who planned every family party for thirty years.
* Mike Kessler: White, 53, Frank's old patrol partner and a fellow Army vet, retired too, teaches defensive tactics at the police academy now. Same frame as Frank, in great shape now, fitted button-down, easy smile. The kind of guy wives notice. Still a normal guy, not a fitness model. He has to look like he used to be heavier.
* Chief Dolan: White, 64, their old police chief, retired. Lean, silver crew cut, loud, loves a toast.
* Tyler: White, 27, Frank and Donna's son, the groom.

VERSION 1
`Tyler's engagement party. The banquet hall they booked for the wedding. String lights, a cake, a banner with the couple's names. Frank stands by the bar with Donna. Gut over his belt. Sport coat hanging open because it won't button. Sweat shining on his forehead under the string lights.`
`The door opens. Mike Kessler walks in. Fitted shirt. Looks ten years younger than the last time they rode together.`

`She turns her back on Frank and asks Donna to step in closer to Tyler.`
`Donna hesitates. Then she steps in next to Tyler and stares down at the floor. Frank shuffles to the back row alone, his head cut off at the top of the frame.`
