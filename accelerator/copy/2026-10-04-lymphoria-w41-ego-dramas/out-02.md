## Source
- Title: The chief does not recognize his own front-page photograph
- Publisher: EMS1 / FireRescue1, Marc Bashoor first-person essay (2024-03-25)
- Link: https://www.ems1.com/wellness-week/my-journey-back-to-health-following-a-painful-a-ha-moment

Source summary (our words):
A fire service leader with four decades in emergency services writes that his fitness slipped as he moved up into command. Bigger responsibilities, raising kids and limited gym access crowded out the training he used to do alongside his crews, and his diet leaned on carbs. In 2007, while his wife was reading the morning paper, he pointed at the heavy man in the front-page photo and asked who it was. She told him it was him. He had not recognized himself, and he calls it his painful a-ha moment. It shocked him into action: he cut sugar and refined carbs, tried intermittent fasting, started with short treadmill sessions, added functional weight training, and over the years lost about 95 pounds and kept it off.
