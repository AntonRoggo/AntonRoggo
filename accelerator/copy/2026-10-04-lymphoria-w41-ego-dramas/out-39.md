# 39. CoachCantRun (A former pro center back who coaches a youth soccer club gets laughed off his own field in front of the players, the parents and his wife)

## Source
- Title: The coach hears what a young player thinks of his running (Aaron's story)
- Publisher: Slimming World, commercial first-person testimonial
- Link: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/real-life-stories/aaron-snares

Source summary (our words): At 20, the subject was coaching football at a professional club's youth setup and already felt self-conscious about how he moved on the pitch. Then he overheard a young player say he was fat and couldn't run. It flattened him. Instead of facing it, he walked away from coaching and stopped exercising entirely. Without the game, his weekends became takeaways, drinking and late-night food, and his confidence slipped badly enough to hurt him in job interviews. The turning point came at his sister's wedding, where as best man he needed a custom suit while everyone else wore standard ones, and his grandmother, newly diagnosed with cancer, told him to do it for himself. He joined a slimming group, cooked at home, lost more than 16 stone over time, and went back to playing sport.
