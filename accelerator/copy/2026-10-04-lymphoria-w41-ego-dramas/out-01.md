Source summary (our words): (article could not be opened; summary from the research entry)
A retired Navy officer was flying to visit his granddaughter when he had to ask for a seat-belt extender. He called it the most embarrassing moment of his life. The request exposed how far his body had drifted from the man who once wore the uniform, and he tied that uniform to a pride he said he had not felt in close to twenty years. He treated the flight as the turning point and started losing weight (the article's link describes a 100-pound loss). Later, at a church ceremony, he was able to put his uniform on again and feel like himself in it.
