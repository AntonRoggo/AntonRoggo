# 27. BestManSpeech (the big brother, an old high school linebacker, gets named best man at his little brother's engagement party and becomes the joke of the room)

## Source
- Title: A best-man invitation becomes a fear of being seen
- Publisher: Slimming World, commercial first-person testimonial (undated; invitation dated 2020)
- Link: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/real-life-stories/charlie-morgan

Source summary (our words):
He played football regularly until he was about 19 and was outgoing and active. In his twenties his life drifted into pub nights and takeaways with his girlfriend and their friends, the weight came on, and he started wearing baggy clothes to hide it. Then his brother asked him to be best man. He said yes, and then it hit him that he would have to stand up and give a speech in front of every guest, a thought he says made him feel physically sick. That was his wake-up call. Following his family's lead he joined a weight loss plan, changed how he cooked his favorite meals and started short morning walks. By the wedding he was down around three and a half stone, wore his suit with confidence, gave the speech without trouble and was first on the dance floor.
