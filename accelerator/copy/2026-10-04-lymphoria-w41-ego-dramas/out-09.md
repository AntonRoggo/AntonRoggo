# 09. ObstacleRace (A former college tennis captain watches his old team sign up for a mud run without him)

## Source
- Title: Friends plan an obstacle race without inviting him (ABC News article; headline per link: Ivan Lendl motivated Mississippi man to lose 150 pounds)
- Publisher: ABC News, same interview; separate event, same person
- Link: https://abcnews.com/Health/ivan-lendl-motivated-mississippi-man-lose-150-pounds/story?id=29732379

Source summary (our words): Matt Howell played tennis in college, then drifted away from sport and gained weight for close to twenty years, reaching about 320 pounds by 37. In 2014 a birthday outing to play tennis with two retired pros ended with a stinging remark about his size, a physical showed serious health risks, and he had weight loss surgery that April. The moment in this entry came about a month later. At dinner, he listened to his friends plan an obstacle course race and realized nobody had thought to include him. He was crushed, and said it was what finally drove the point home. He rebuilt how he ate and trained, lost roughly 155 pounds in under a year, and now runs obstacle races himself.
