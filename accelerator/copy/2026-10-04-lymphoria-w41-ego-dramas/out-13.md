Leads (hooks and hold)
* Walt Brandt: White, 61, 6'1". Retired Air Force master sergeant, flight engineer, twenty years in. His flying partner, Danny Voss, was killed in a crash in 1991. Aggro, egotistical, tells flight stories nobody asked for, feels like the world forgot him and forgot Danny. Before: around 260, big frame gone soft. Gut over his belt, squadron polo two sizes too tight, puffy face, sweats in any room. Hasn't been able to button his uniform since he retired. After: same man, same frame, still big. Flat stomach, broad shoulders, sharp jaw. Still cocky. Never skinny.
* Diane: White, 59, his wife. Pretty, warm, the one who remembers every birthday in the squadron.
* Ray Castillo: Latino, 61, flew on the same crew as Walt and Danny. Same frame as Walt, in great shape now, uniform fits, easy smile. The kind of guy wives notice. Still a normal guy, not a fitness model. He has to look like he used to be heavier.
* Colonel Pike: White, 76, their old squadron commander, retired. Lean, white crew cut, loud, loves a toast.
* Young airman: White, 22, base honor guard. Crisp service blues, white gloves tucked in his belt.
* Danny Voss (photo only): White, 26, flight suit, grin, framed photo dated 1991.

VERSION 1
`Squadron reunion. A veterans hall. A framed photo on an easel: a young airman in a flight suit, DANNY VOSS 1991. Walt stands by the coffee urn with Diane. Gut over his belt. Sweating through a squadron polo two sizes too small.`
`The door opens. Ray Castillo walks in wearing his old Air Force blues. Every button closed.`
`Diane sees him and lights up.`
Diane: "RAY! Look at you in that uniform!"
`She crosses the room and throws her arms around him. Then she straightens his ribbons with both hands, smiling up at him.`
Ray: "Diane! Still fits, can you believe it?"
Diane: "Danny would be so proud of you."
`Her hand stays on his chest.`
`Walt stands by the urn holding two coffees. One was for her. Sweat on his lip. His polo riding up over his gut.`
Walt: "I flew with him too, Ray."
`Nobody hears him.`

VERSION 2

Colonel Pike: "Veterans Day, we march for Voss. Everybody in blues. Where's his flight engineer? BRANDT!"
`Walt raises his glass. Polo stretched tight, sweat through the collar. Diane is right next to him.`
`Pike looks right at him. Then past him. Keeps searching the room.`
Colonel Pike: "Anybody seen Brandt?"
Vet next to Walt: "Sir. He's right here."
Colonel Pike: "Good God. Didn't even recognize you, Sergeant! Diane, we're gonna need a bigger uniform!"
`SOUND: The whole room laughs.`
`Every head turns to Diane. She laughs along, red in the face. She won't look at Walt.`
`Behind Pike, Danny's photo on the easel.`

VERSION 3

`He nods at the photo. The young airman looks him up and down. His eyes stop on the gut.`
Young airman: "No kidding? You don't look it."
`He turns his back on Walt and asks Diane if she'd like a chair.`
`Diane stares at Danny's photo.`
