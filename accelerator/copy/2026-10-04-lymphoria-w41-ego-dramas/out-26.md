# 26. LakeRide (the biggest man from an old college rugby crew rolls into their annual ride to the lake forty minutes behind everyone, in front of all the wives)

## Source
- Title: The childhood cyclist gets dropped on the way to the lake
- Publisher: Slimming World, commercial first-person timeline (undated; event dated November 2010)
- Link: https://www.slimmingworld.co.uk/real-life-stories/alex-rowlands

Source summary (our words):
As a young teenager he played football, cricket and rugby and rode his bike constantly. Years later, studying abroad in Germany in November 2010, he joined some friends for an easy ride out to a lake nearby. They pulled away from him while he fell behind, red, soaked and short of breath. He knew by then that he had put on weight, but he chose not to step on a scale and find out how much. The ride stuck with him. The following summer his grandparents came to visit and told him plainly they were worried about his health, which hit hard given his grandmother's own medical history. Back home he started on a weight loss plan, and over roughly four years he lost a large amount of weight.
