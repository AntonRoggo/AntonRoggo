# 08. LegendsForehand (A former college tennis star gets roasted about his weight by a retired legend, on court, in front of his wife and the whole club)

## Source
- Title: A tennis hero comments on the player's lost potential
- Publisher: ABC News, Katie Kindelan interview (2015-03-18)
- Link: https://abcnews.com/Health/ivan-lendl-motivated-mississippi-man-lose-150-pounds/story?id=29732379

Source summary (our words):
A Mississippi high school teacher and tennis director had dreamed of playing professionally in college, then drifted away from competitive tennis and climbed to about 320 pounds over the next fifteen to twenty years. In 2014 his wife gave him a birthday gift: a chance to play with two retired tennis greats at an event in Nashville. During the hit, one of the legends told him he would have been a very good player if he weren't so big. He was angry at first, then came to hear it as recognition of the talent still in him. That remark, along with a physical that flagged serious health risks, pushed him to act. With surgery, a strict diet, running and lifting, he went from 320 to 164 pounds in about 11 months.
