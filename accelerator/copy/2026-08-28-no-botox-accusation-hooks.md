# No-Botox accusation hooks

Seed: "I've never had any Botox. I don't know if that's the ultimate compliment or what but this is the only thing I'm using on my face right here."

Client: The Tallowed Truth / F-Balm. Distinct from the existing Botox-lane winners ([[2026-08-28-botox-in-the-caribbean]], [[2026-08-28-natural-aging-versus-obvious-botox]], [[2026-08-28-plastic-surgeon-compliment]]), which are all Ben narrating about other people. This seed is first-person: the speaker IS the proof. The mechanism is that being accused of having work done is the product testimonial. 15 hooks across 10 mechanisms.

## Accusation as proof
1. My coworker asked me point blank what doctor I go to. I told her it's beef fat in a jar. She thought I was lying.
2. Three women at my high school reunion asked who my surgeon was. I graduated in '96. I rub beef fat on my face. That's it.
3. A woman at church grabbed my arm and whispered "who does your Botox" like she was asking for a dealer. Ma'am, I rub beef fat on my face. Beef fat is the whole story.

## Confession and the awkward truth
4. I've been lying to people about my skincare for six months. Not because it's expensive. Because telling a 45-year-old woman you rub beef tallow on your face sounds insane.
5. When people ask my skincare routine, I lie and say retinol. Because the truth, that I rub rendered beef fat on my face every night, makes me sound like I've lost my mind.

## Cancelled appointment
6. I had a Botox consultation booked for March. I cancelled it after two weeks of rubbing beef fat on my face. My husband thinks I went.

## Value math and side-by-side
7. My best friend spends $400 every three months on Botox. Last week she asked me what I'm doing differently. I didn't have the heart to tell her it costs forty bucks.
8. Botox: $600 every four months, can't move your forehead. This jar: forty bucks, five ingredients, and my coworker asked me if I "had something done" last Tuesday.
9. My sister gets Botox every twelve weeks. She can prove it. I rub beef fat on my face every night. Her coworker told us we look the same age. We are not.

## Age guess
10. A cashier carded me last month. I'm 47. I almost kissed her on the mouth. All I do is rub beef tallow on my face before bed.

## Partner noticing
11. My husband hasn't complimented my appearance in eleven years. Two weeks into rubbing beef tallow on my face, he looked at me across the kitchen table and said, "What's different about you?"

## Authority reaction
12. My dermatologist told me whatever I'm doing, keep doing it. I did not tell her it's beef tallow and raw honey. Some conversations are not worth having.

## Behavioral shift
13. Women started being nicer to me at pickup. Holding conversations longer. Making more eye contact. I changed one thing: beef tallow on my face every night.

## Repeat compliment pattern
14. I've been asked three times this month what I'm "doing." Same answer every time. Five ingredients. One jar. Beef tallow on my face before bed. No needle, no appointment, no filler.

## Foundation quit
15. I stopped wearing foundation three months ago. Not out of confidence. Because whatever this beef tallow is doing to my skin made foundation look worse, not better. Two people have asked me what "work" I had done since.
