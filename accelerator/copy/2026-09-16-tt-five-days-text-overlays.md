Script is Ben's, verbatim in [[2026-08-28-look-younger-in-five-days]]. Overlays sit over the first three spoken hooks. Rules: short, open loop, not a transcript of what he says, readable in two seconds. Style modeled on the three overlays that are working now ("Attractive 50-year-old neighbor," "Weird Botox hack," "Feedback from a plastic surgeon"): noun phrase, names the subject, implies a story.

## Notes
- #6, #24 put the guarantee in the overlay. Ben says it twice in the script, so the overlay can carry it up front without repeating a spoken line.
- #13 borrows the $57 winner's water beat. Ben doesn't say "water" in this script, which is why it works as an overlay: it adds a fact the audio doesn't have.
- #19 and #23 pull the "not Chinese tallow" beat from the back half of the script up to the top. Bucket #1 (manufacturing trust) is the best CPA in the account, so the overlay can front-load it while the audio stays on the appearance promise.
- "$40 tin" in #12 states the price. The script doesn't. Cut it if the price stays off camera.
- Avoid: "This balm..." or "If you want..." as overlays. Those are his sentences.
