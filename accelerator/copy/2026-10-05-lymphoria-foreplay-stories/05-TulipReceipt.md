FORMAT: SONG

## Source

foreplay_url: https://app.foreplay.co/discovery?ad=1FdnjFgTq6BZNWdCqBgh

Source premise (our words): A wife in her sixties, married four decades, finds a florist receipt in her husband's coat for flowers sent across town on their anniversary. Instead of confronting him, she stays silent for weeks while her older sister comes every morning and helps her get her face and her footing back. At the end she puts the receipt next to his coffee, delivers one cold line, and he cannot finish his sentence because she looks better than she has in years.
