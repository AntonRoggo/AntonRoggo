Source premise (our words): After a decades-long marriage ends, the ex-husband remarries a much younger woman and spends the better part of a year telling their shared friends that his ex-wife fell apart and aged overnight. A wedding invitation puts the two of them, and his new wife, in front of every one of those friends for a weekend. She goes, and the friends, the ex, and the new wife all see the opposite of the story he told.

Built as its own story, not a rewrite of the Charleston scripts (copy/2026-09-28 and 2026-09-29): different city, different wedding (her goddaughter's, so she cannot send regrets), a different guide (her brother's wife, a lymphedema therapist, on the phone from Tucson), a menopause mechanism instead of cortisol, no brother who calls him out, and the new wife's turn happens at the sink.
