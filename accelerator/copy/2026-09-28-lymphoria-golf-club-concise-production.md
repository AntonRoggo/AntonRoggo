## Story logic pass, short

Chain: glasses, cart barn, hand, giggle, 22 and 66 → no scream, the clubhouse mirror, 63, 22 since 60, the concession, almost → a year of effort, the doctor, the car → the call, Rio, "next flight," hands on shoulders → day three, the unlabeled bottle, objection, "menopause sludge" → 001's mechanism → the herbs, the promise → day four, week two, four, six, the dress on the door he passes → the dinner, every head, his table, the cart girl at the bar, "what did you do," no smile, walks past → the truth in one line, the label, the close.

1. **The catch and the mirror in one move.** The clubhouse bathroom puts the mirror inside the wound, which the operator asked for, and it costs one line: "I didn't scream. I walked into the clubhouse bathroom."
2. **The unlabeled bottle holds until the last cell.** Nothing in the story sells. The mechanism is the sister's, the herbs are the sister's, the name arrives when she flies home. The objection ("I don't need whatever that is") is written for a bottle with no name.
3. **He doesn't know she knows** until the dinner. "He'd told me I didn't have to come" and "Mark didn't notice, he was at the club by seven" keep that alive. The dress on the door is the visual, not a spoken beat, so the runtime holds.
4. **Restraint.** No smile, no answer, walks past. Your cut had "I just smiled, took my seat"; the bank's leads don't smile at him.
5. **No ordering beats, no Ozempic.** The sink and the circle are out. The close is the guarantee and "that's all it took for me."
6. **Arithmetic.** 63, weight since 60, 66, 22. Fourteen at four, nineteen at six. Six weeks from the call to the spring dinner.

## What your cut would have failed (fixed here)
- "Your body's natural lymphatic pump slows down." It has no pump. That's the mechanism's whole point.
- "Trapped fluid," "inflammatory fluids," "-14 lbs trapped fluid & bloat" (three places). Waste, stuck, sits.
- "I just smiled, took my seat." She doesn't smile.
- "Don't rush into harsh injections." Out, per your note; and the bank's truth beat replaced "I didn't care what he thought anymore" with "He was a coward," which is the winner's verdict.
- "Reactivate circulation," "soothe the chronic puffiness," "raw botanical extracts": the herb lines are 001's now.
