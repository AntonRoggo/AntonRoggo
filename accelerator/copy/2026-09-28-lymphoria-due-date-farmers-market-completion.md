A good friend of mine and I went to a farmers market on Saturday. Jess is due in two weeks and is very clearly pregnant. I, however, am not pregnant.

We're waiting for our coffee at this cute little shop and this middle-aged woman looked at us and said "aww how sweet, you guys are due so close together."

I was immediately in shock and just said "oop welp I'm not pregnant" and she goes "oh I'm so sorry, it must be your dress!"

I just grabbed my little girl who was toddling around and walked away so fast. I was honestly reeling in humiliation. Surrounded by dozens of people, all of a sudden I felt like the most hideous troll in the place and everyone was staring at me. I'm sure they weren't. But the immediate mental assault was staggering.

I've had a really hard time losing weight postpartum. I had a c-section that left me with an apron belly. And I was feeling really cute in this dress. Which just made it so much worse.

I have actually lost weight recently. Fitting into a cute summer dress. Feeling good about myself. Just to be told by a stranger I looked nine months pregnant.

It shattered my self-confidence. I put on a brave face and laughed it off and walked through the rest of the market, but deep down I was dying. I don't typically struggle with too much insecurity, because I know comparison is the thief of happiness and I genuinely try to be present and positive for my little girl. But man.

I ugly cried and sobbed the whole way home in the car.

Jess was furious. She apologized over and over for not saying something back to the lady. But I think we were both just in shock. How can you expect to be told something like that? Of course it wasn't her fault.

That night, after I put my daughter down, I stood in the bathroom in the dress and looked at myself for the first time in two years.

Really looked.

The apron. The shelf of belly that hangs over the scar and never went down after her. Two years old, same as she is. My face, puffy every morning like I'd been crying, even when I hadn't.
