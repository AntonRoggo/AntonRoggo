# Review: "The Master Animated Song Ad Script" (open marriage), against Spine A, the source and the bank

Filed 2026-09-28. Operator's animated song cut of [[story-009-open-marriage-daughter-reddit]], checked against [[spine-a-relative-from-brazil]], [[aunt-phone-call-cortisol-blame-transfer]], [[mechanism-and-product-core]], [[2026-09-28-lymphoria-open-marriage-comeback-song]] and [[story-ad-logic]].

Verdict: the skeleton and the order are right. Effort sits before the ask, Val comes to the door, the stare is at week six with the car outside, she walks past at the courthouse. What is wrong is inside the lines: the rhymes bent the mechanism until it says the wrong thing, the dose contradicts itself, ruled-out vocabulary is back, and six of the spine's beats are missing.

## Fails logic or a rule (fix before anything else)
1. **"You just need to digest."** The product drains. Digest is a different organ and a different verb. The whole mechanism is "you have to drain it." The rhyme with "chest" killed the one word the ad exists to say.
2. **Dose contradicts itself.** Part 3: "half a dropper when the morning wakes, half a dropper before twilight breaks" (one dropper a day). Part 6: "two droppers a day." Pick one.
3. **Water and fluid are back.** "Fourteen pounds of water washed away," and the Part 3 animation note "flushing trapped fluids." Ruled out. It is waste, stuck waste, drain the waste.
4. **"Your lymphatic rivers run stagnant and dry."** The mechanism is that nothing leaves; she is full, swollen, backed up. Dry is the opposite of the symptom two lines earlier ("belly swollen and tight"). Bank: the drain shuts down, it has no pump, nothing leaves.
5. **Theo appears from nowhere.** "On the courthouse steps, Theo took my hand." He has never been named. The black car at week six is the place to give him a line: "The third one was Theo."
6. **Arithmetic.** Married at nineteen, "twenty years," Brooke twenty-two, "thirty years ago." Three numbers that cannot all be true. If Brooke is 22 and was carried at the wedding, it is twenty-two years, and the business was set up twenty-two years ago, not thirty.
7. **"I told him every name, just like he required."** He didn't require it; she (or Brooke) demanded it and he agreed. "Just like we agreed" or "like the deal required." The point is that the reports are his own deal.
8. **The gym stall is set up and never paid off.** She's five days a week on salads and the mirror doesn't move. Val never says the line that explains it: "You can starve all you want, you can run all you want, you cannot burn waste, you have to drain it." Without the escape hatches, the viewer is left thinking the gym should have worked.
9. **Ray never says a line about her body.** Spine beat 15 is the betrayer saying her name like a question and "you look incredible, what did you do." The source gives you the inverse ("snide remarks about my clothing," "almost called me a whore and stopped himself"). Here he only screams "let's close up the door." Four source beats (the scream over nothing, she doesn't back down, weeks of remarks, the near-word, then the ask to close) are merged into one line, and the best of them is gone.
10. **"Not anymore" isn't an answer to "let's close the door."** It's a rhyme. The source's answer is "she immediately shot it down." The word is "No."
11. **The business is staged as the climax, with exclamation marks.** Part 5 is titled "The Climax" and gives it four gloating lines ("Ray didn't own a single dime of that show!"). Your ruling: a kicker, not the spine, and she doesn't take it over. The bank's comeback voice is restraint: Renee doesn't smile, doesn't speak. Two lines, flat, after the courthouse.
12. **The blame transfer is missing five of its seven pieces.** Val "didn't ask about my workout, just looked at my face": she never asks how Diane is (piece 1). "He did this to you" and "cortisol" arrive in the same line instead of the blame landing first (piece 3). No heart-then-body (4). No endurance as the clog, "every night you waited up, every day you held it together" (6). No mission restatement, "he put this weight on you, then asked to leave you for it, we're taking it back" (7). The villain has a face, which is right.

## Structure drift from Spine A
- Beat 10, objection one and the reframe ("I don't need drainage, I need to lose weight"): missing.
- Beat 11, the pointing: "in your belly, in your face, under your jaw, that's not fat, that's waste": replaced by "poison built up in your spine" and "locked in your chest." Neither is where the ad's symptoms are.
- Beat 13, objection two and the promise ("there's no way drops fix twenty years" / "six weeks, by week one your face, by week three your jawline, watch"): missing.
- Beat 16, the circle: missing. Optional, but Brooke trying it is one text.
- Beat 17, the truth: missing. "He was a coward, not right. He couldn't handle a wife his own age. The second I drained it I got me back, not nineteen-year-old me, the woman I was supposed to be at thirty-nine." "When you drain out a dead weight, you get a second chance" is a pun where the spine puts its strongest paragraph.
- Brooke never says "divorce him" first (source). Her first instinct in the source is protective, which is what makes "say yes" a considered second move. Ray also never "keeps pushing"; he asks once and she yeses the same day.
- Hooks: two, not three. Hook 1 tells the whole plot and asserts "the rest of his life regretting it," which the body can't show. Hook 2 is the one that withholds and it's good.
- Grandparents and church women are conflated on one porch, with the screenshots shown to the church ladies. Source: screenshots to the grandparents, the church women kicked out. Stage it as two short beats or pick one.

## Rhymes where the meaning broke (operator rule: rhyme only where meaning holds)
"don't ever look down" (means nothing) / "cold dinners he never could feel" / "cleared his throat on a lie" (what lie?) / "poison built up in your spine" / "stagnant and dry" / "just need to digest" / "calms the storm so you can breathe free" (red clover's job is "so it doesn't build back up") / "not a hint of the gray" (drops don't fix gray hair) / "looking bloated and tired" (the secretary did what?) / "Not anymore" / "the only drops that approve" / "before the bottles hit the rack."

Herb lines: order holds. Stillingia and cleavers hold. Prickly ash should name the cause ("restarts the circulation he shut down"). Red clover must keep the rebuild: "calms the swelling so it don't build back up."

## Voice
- Em dashes in nearly every stanza. Rule.
- Ray calling his wife "girl" and Val's "honey" are fine. Diane's "dirty little game!" and "single dime of that show!" are not her; the bank's comeback leads win by not speaking.
- Brooke drives the park, the condition, the profiles and the screenshots. Diane's own acts: saying yes, saying no, walking past. Give her the condition back (source) or at least the screenshots (source), or she is a passenger.
