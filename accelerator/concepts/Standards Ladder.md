# Standards Ladder

Five rungs. Every time the AI lets you down, do not get mad, climb one. From Mario Castelli's Day 2 field guide.

1. TASTE: the standard lives only in your head. You fix every output by hand, forever.
2. NOTE: you say it out loud once. Works today, gone by next session.
3. RULE: you write it where Claude always reads it. Said once, applies forever.
4. TOOL: you put it in code so it cannot be gotten wrong. Built once, enforced automatically.
5. SKILL: you package the whole thing so it fires itself and uses your files.

Each rung up, you do less and the system does more.

Where each rung lives in this vault:
- Taste: your head.
- Note: chat, or /remember-this in the moment.
- Rule: brand/voice.md, clients/{name}.md, or the Script's Rules that bite.
- Tool: a check in code, usually under systems/.
- Skill: .claude/skills/.

The two flywheels: banking reactions makes the system smarter, and having to articulate why something works makes you sharper. They feed each other.

The test: after you correct the AI, does the correction survive to tomorrow? If yes, you are building an apprentice. If no, you are smacking a vending machine.

The only real mistake: fixing the same output twice without banking the lesson.
