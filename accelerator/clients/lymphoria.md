## Voice and rules
- Cortisol or stress mechanisms always carry the blame transfer (operator rule, 2026-09-21). The "helper asks how she is first" piece is opt-in as of 2026-09-28: ask the operator on each cortisol script whether to include it; the default is out. Zero accountability for the lead. Full seven-piece breakdown in [[mechanism-and-product-core]], source in [[aunt-phone-call-cortisol-blame-transfer]].
- Absolution is in every mechanism, not only cortisol. It was never the lead's fault: "You're not weak brother. You're backed up."
- Reconfirmed 2026-09-22 on the Walmart ex-husband script: even when the premise is "she let herself go," the lead never agrees with it on camera. No "he wasn't wrong," no "I did let myself go." The man is the one at fault, and the script says so.
