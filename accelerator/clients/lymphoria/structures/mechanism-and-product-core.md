## 2. RULE: cortisol mechanisms carry the blame transfer (operator rule, 2026-09-21)

Any script where cortisol or stress is the clog cause must include the blame transfer from [[aunt-phone-call-cortisol-blame-transfer]]. Zero accountability for the lead. The pieces, in order:

1. (Opt-in, ruling 2026-09-28: ask the operator each script; default out.) The helper is the first person to ask how the lead is. Care before diagnosis.
2. The lead's effort is listed, so the viewer knows she did everything right.
3. "They did this to you." The blame lands before the word cortisol is spoken.
4. The injury is extended from the emotional to the physical: "Not just your heart. Your body."
5. Cortisol is the bridge, not the villain: them, stress, cortisol, drainage shuts down. The villain keeps a face.
6. The lead's endurance (no sleep, holding it together) is what sat and hardened. It reads as strength, not as failing to cope.
7. After the mechanism, the blame is restated and turned into a shared mission: "They broke your heart and put this weight on you... We're taking both back."

Never in a cortisol script: any line implying the lead ate wrong, let herself go, should have managed her stress, or needs to change her habits.

The diet stance is the one to watch. "Keep eating right" and "didn't change a single thing about my diet" are opposite promises. Never both in one script.
