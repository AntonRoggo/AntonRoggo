# Lymphoria structure: the stranger's call (odor, the kitchen sink)

Filed 2026-09-23 from the operator's script, [[story-005-the-flinch-stranger-from-the-group]]. This is the mechanism-delivery block for a shameful symptom rather than a weight: the guide is a stranger from an anonymous group who had it, the call happens in a parked car, and the mechanism is the kitchen sink. Keep FIXED lines word for word. Only touch the slots.

## Beat chain (8 beats)

1. **The post at 2am.** The symptom, how long, what it cost, the tests are negative, the doctor says it's just you, "I just want to feel [feminine] again." Replies are things she tried. Then a DM: "I had this for four years. It's not what you think it is. Can I call you?"
2. **The awkward open.** "Sorry, this is weird. I don't usually call strangers from the internet." / "Nobody does."
3. **Diagnosis by guessing (FIXED shape).** The guide describes the symptom's onset, character, and pattern before the lead does. "How do you know that." "Because I had the exact same thing. Four years. I cried in more parking lots than I can count." / "I'm in a parking lot right now." / "Of course you are."
4. **The kitchen sink (FIXED).** Scrub the sink, still stinks, the smell was never the sink, it's the gunk in the pipe. Your body has pipes, the lymphatic system, the body's drain. The biggest drains sit [in your groin]. No pump. Clogs. Old junk in a warm pipe ferments. Leaks out the nearest exit. That's why washing does nothing. That's why every test is negative: they're looking for an infection, and a clogged drain isn't one.
5. **The click.** "Two years of tests and a stranger on the phone just explained it in two minutes." / "I know. I was you. Somebody in that same group explained it to me."
6. **Herbs by job, then the product (FIXED).** "One breaks up the old gunk, one gets it moving, one flushes it out through your pee, and one calms it all down so it doesn't clog back up." / "Herbs. Come on." / "I know how it sounds. I'd have laughed at me too." Then the name, "all four herbs in one bottle, third party tested, no fillers. Half a dropper under your tongue, morning and night. Tastes like honey, weirdly."
7. **The pre-frame (FIXED).** "First week you're gonna pee constantly. Don't freak out. That's [two years] of it finally leaving the right way. Through your pee, not through your skin. Give it six weeks, then come back and tell me."
8. **The six-week call.** "It's been six weeks." / "And??" / [the absence: "there's nothing there"]. "I feel like me again." / "Go live your life."

## Slots

- The symptom and its "nearest exit" (odor through sweat and fluids; for a different symptom, the exit changes and so does where "the biggest drains" sit).
- How long she has had it; how long the guide had it (longer).
- What she tried (the replies).
- The guide's name.

## What must never change

- The guide is a stranger who had it, not a relative and not a professional. For a symptom nobody admits to, having had it is the credential.
- The guide guesses the symptom before the lead describes it.
- The parking lot line lands on both sides of the call.
- The sink comes before any anatomy.
- The herbs are named by job, not by name, on the call. The product name comes after "write this down."
- The pre-frame on peeing is said before the first dose, and said again by the lead when she converts someone.
- The lead becomes the guide in the last scene, using the same guess, the same sink, the same pre-frame.
