# Lymphoria structure: The Helper scene (fitter peer)

Filed 2026-09-21 from the operator's script. This is how the product and the mechanism get introduced when the lead meets someone his own age who is visibly fitter (gym, locker room, or similar). Built to be lifted almost whole into other scripts.

## 1. Source scene (canonical, verbatim)

Only change from the original: em dashes in the stage direction swapped for commas.

🎬 SCENE 5: THE HELPER
⏱️ ~50s · 🎵 Score drops out. Locker room ambience.
🎬 Locker room. He's on a bench. Towel around his neck. The guy he's seen around for weeks, same height, big frame but lean, no gut, opens the locker next to his.
💬 HIM: "Alright man I gotta ask. How? We're the same age."
💬 HELPER: "Ha. You want the real answer?"
💬 HIM: "I've been in this gym five days a week for three months. I eat clean. I don't drink. And this thing won't move."
💬 HELPER: "It won't. Two years ago I was bigger than you."
💬 HIM: "Come on man. No way."
💬 HELPER: "I'm serious. And I was already in the gym. Same as you're doing right now."
💬 HIM: "So what changed."
💬 HELPER: "Let me ask you something first. Your gut. Is it soft or is it hard?"
💬 HIM: "What?"
💬 HELPER: "Is it soft like fat, or is it tight and pushed out?"
💬 HIM: "It's hard. Like a rock."
💬 HELPER: "Exactly. That's what nobody tells us. Every guy our age thinks it's just fat. So we hit the cardio. Eat clean. Blame ourselves when nothing moves. But a gut that's tight and pushed out like that isn't regular fat. That's backup. Your lymphatic system is your body's drain. It carries out all the waste your body makes every day. Yours is clogged shut. The waste never leaves. It sits in your middle. Thickens. Hardens into sludge. That gut isn't just fat. It's pounds of stuck waste."
💬 HIM: "So how do you get rid of it."
💬 HELPER: "You can't out-train it. Your lymphatic system has no pump. After 30 the whole system slows down. No diet on earth can burn off waste stuck in your tissue. It has to drain. You're not weak brother. You're backed up."
💬 HIM: "That's why nothing's worked."
💬 HELPER: "You've been fighting a clogged drain with a treadmill."
💬 HIM: "So what did you do."
💬 HELPER: "I drained it."
🎬 He reaches into his gym bag and sets a small dark bottle on the bench between them.
💬 HELPER: "Lymphoria. Four herbs. One full dropper in the morning. Tastes like honey. Don't grab whatever pops up online. Most of them are weak doses and fillers. This one's third-party tested, no junk."
💬 HIM: "Come on... drops?"
💬 HELPER: "I know. I said the same thing. I'd have laughed at me too."
💬 HIM: "And that's it? That's what changed?"
💬 HELPER: "That's where it started. Look, keep coming to the gym. Keep eating right. But you've been trying to out-train a drainage problem. Nothing changes until the drain opens. Give it six weeks."

## 4. Slots (the only things that change between scripts)

- **Setting and props:** locker room, bench, gym bag. Any place two men the same age end up side by side.
- **Who the helper is:** same age, same height, big frame but lean, no gut. Seen around for weeks, so the ask feels earned, not random.
- **The opening ask (beat 1):** wording can flex, the "same age" parity cannot.
- **Effort inventory (beat 2):** the lead's real list. Frequency + duration + diet + one sacrifice, ending on "won't move."
- **Helper's backstory (beat 3):** "two years ago," "bigger than you." Must include that he was already doing the same work.
- **Button line object (beat 8):** treadmill, or the lead's main failed effort.
- **Effort words in the close (beat 12).**

## 5. What must never change

- The lead asks. The helper never volunteers. He even checks first: "You want the real answer?"
- The helper was worse off AND already doing the work. Both halves.
- The diagnostic question comes before the mechanism. The lead says the symptom in his own words ("hard, like a rock") and the helper builds on that.
- Mechanism order: belief, effort, self-blame, reframe, drain metaphor, clog, sludge, weight.
- Absolution line lands right after the why-effort-fails logic, not before.
- The solution is named with the mechanism's verb ("drained") before the product is named.
- The bottle is a physical prop placed between them. Product is shown before it is described.
- The lead voices the skepticism. The helper agrees with it.
- The close keeps the gym and the diet. The product unblocks the effort, it does not replace it.

## 6. Open questions

- This block is built for the male, hard-gut avatar ("every guy our age," "brother," soft-or-hard test). Other avatars need their own diagnostic question and symptom. Do not port Block A or B to another avatar without the operator's version.
