## Account learnings (from Anton, 2026-10-12)
Patterns across their own ads, not controlled tests.

### Every brief needs
One job, one person, the moment they started looking, their symptoms in their own words, why it works, one proof, an honest timescale.

### What wins
- One main problem per ad, told in depth. Stack symptoms and benefits. 6+ specific symptoms won 10%; 0 to 2 symptoms won 1 in 106. Never strip back.
- ALWAYS explain why it works (e.g. heme iron in liver and spleen is more bioavailable). 6% vs 2% win rate. 81 ads since June without a why: zero winners.
- One specific person (mum of a picky eater, woman with low ferritin). 6% vs 4%.
- Best format: "What would happen in 30 days." 15% win rate, cheapest sales. Aimed at one person (kids, run down, everyday man), long benefit list. The list is the point.
- Best hook: "This is what happens if..." 14% win rate, cheapest sales.
- Worst hook: opening with "If you're a mum of..." 0 of 37. Put the person inside the story, not as the first line.
- Problem, Agitate, Solution: most clicks of any structure (TOBACCO, STUCK GUT, ECZEMA, SICK PARENTS).
- Length: 90s+ wins twice as often as under 45s (8% vs 4%). Same cost per sale at any length. Let the story set the length.
- Voiceover explainers: best win rate (11%). Creator UGC: weakest (2%, 10 of 244 got big spend).
- Founder ads: most clicks and most big-spend ads. Founder origin story as the WHOLE ad costs 16% more per sale. Use founder as speaker, not origin story as the ad.
- 3s hold rate is fine (35%). Body and reason to click matter more than new hooks.

### What dies
- Testimonials (2 of 113) and manifestos (0 of 39, most expensive per sale).
- UPF or synthetic vs whole food as the main message in video (UPF 0 of 23, +65% CPA). Supporting proof only.
- Hormones as the lead (0 of 25), pregnancy as the lead (0 of 54). Test carefully.
- Hype hooks ("everyone's talking about it"): buyers cancel at about twice the rate.
- Thin, short scripts. Recent ads average 3.5 symptoms and 66s vs winners' 6.1 and 96s.

### Jobs (one ad set per job)
- Iron: 4 of 27 won, cheapest sales of the main jobs. Rising in survey (16%).
- Kids: TOBACCO is the biggest ad ever (£229k). Kids ads get the most clicks.
- Organs without eating liver: top reason to buy (32%). Customer words: "I know liver is good for me but I can't stomach it", "organ meats end up in the freezer but not eaten".
- Gut: biggest pain (30% of buyers with a health concern), but only 3 of 57 videos won. Needs better scripts, not more volume.

### Statics
- Support act, not growth lever. 1 to 2 good ones per job. 408 made, 15 got spend.
- Works: short bold line plus product ("You'll never go back", "This is not a supplement"), reviews or stars on image, "replace your supplement shelf", saving shown, real food vs synthetic.
- Dies: iron or kids story in a static (18% to 56% more expensive), price or free gift on image, stats, text heavy (0 of 85), no product in shot.
- No AI images with wrong packs (400g, "Grass fed New Zealand", "COLLUSTRUM"). UK £, US $.
- Judge statics on cost per sale, not clicks.

### Customer insights
- 1 in 3 buy for the whole family.
- Price nearly stopped half of buyers: show cost per day against the supplements it replaces.
- Taste is the top reason families cancel: show it cold, hidden in recipes.
- They research for weeks. Say "you'll feel it in a few weeks." No instant results.

### Plan, next few weeks
- 4 to 8 truly different ads per ad set (different speaker, format or angle). A new hook on the same video is the same ad. Max 6 new ads a week per ad set.
- 22 of 29 proven winners are switched off. Build new versions of winners BEFORE inventing new ideas.

## Proof and consistency rules
- Never say "cure", "treat", "fix" or "heals" (some old ads said customers "cured" IBS). Note: site founder line "reversed 15 years of IBS" needs a check before use.
- One guarantee everywhere. Old ads say 30, 90 and 100 days. Site says 100 day money back on the first pouch. CONFIRM.
- One customer count. Old ads say 20k to 200k. Site says 200,000+. CONFIRM.
- Every personal detail must be true.
- Which site stats are approved for ads (93% digestion, +40% memory, 4.9 Trustpilot): NOT CONFIRMED.
