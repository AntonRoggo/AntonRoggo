- "Was skeptic not gonna lie, but I LOVE IT!"

- And the one who never had the objection: "Rubbing this natural, silky, hydrating triple rendered beef fat on my face is definitely one of [my choices]."

What got them over it, ranked by how often it's the stated reason:
1. **Natural, no chemicals.** "I have extremely sensitive skin and decided to try the F-Balm because it's made from all natural ingredients." "I liked the idea of using beef tallow."
2. **Tried everything else.** "I was pretty skeptical after trying so many products in the past with little to no results." "Said what the heck, I've tried a lot of things, might as well try this."
3. **Someone they trust used it first.** Daughter, granddaughter, friend, mom, sister (see the table above).
4. **Researched it.** "I researched tallow and historical uses, and decided to give it a try, especially given that it's a Canadian company."
5. **The reviews.** "The reviews on the site enticed me to try this." "Then I had seen the REVIEWS that I was waiting for." [R27076]
6. **A cheap way in.** "I was on the fence, on a tight budget, but kept thinking 'maybe this is exactly what I need.'"

Nobody reports being laughed at or teased by a husband or friends: zero hits. The "crazy" is always self-directed, and usually about a grandmother who turned out to be right.
