# Dr. Eric Berg, "ultimate face remedy for wrinkly and dry skin": breakdown and a testable mechanism (2026-09-25)

## His argument, in his order
1. Whatever you put on your skin gets absorbed into the body, "eventually through the liver." Chemical lotions, scrubbing, sterilizing kill the friendly bacteria, skin goes red, dry, or too oily.
2. Environment: pollution, smoke, fluoride and chloride in the shower, UV.
3. Diet: alcohol, trans fats, junk food. Worst thing for skin is a low-fat diet, "skin is mainly protein and fat." Patients on low-fat keto with soy isolate "start looking older, not just skin, hair."
4. The villain: seed oils. Soy, corn, canola, cottonseed, safflower, sunflower. "Highly industrialized, highly refined, very unstable," extracted with hexane, deodorized, decolorized. They "get lodged into our membranes and our skin" and "stay in there for two and a half to three years." Too much omega-6 and too little omega-3 "compete with each other," so "you start developing flaky dry red skin." Seed oil use is up 1,600% in 100 years, doubled in 20.
5. The bridge to the jar: "a lot of these omega-6 oils, like sunflower oil, are in facial creams. It's going to dry out your skin, it's going to make your skin older." Also: no alcohol in creams.
6. The remedy: "are you sitting down for this? Grass-fed tallow. That's actually beef fat. Before you click off, hear me out." Chemistry "very similar to the chemistry of part of your skin." "The Latin word for tallow means sebum, that's the oil your body produces."
7. Why tallow: won't oxidize the skin, won't cause inflammation, has vitamins A, D, E, K, has CLA "which tends to retain moisture." Not greasy like coconut oil. Softness, healthy glow, helps dark spots and wrinkles. "Just try it for a week and see what happens."
8. The fatty acids as jobs: about 50% saturated; oleic "a really good moisturizer"; palmitic "softens the skin"; stearic "repairs the skin"; linoleic (omega-6) "only like 2%."
9. Proof: "I'm not recommending a brand, just look at the reviews and read what people are reporting."
10. The heritage close: "a forgotten secret, we used to use this long ago, but we replaced it because someone came up with this idea that saturated fat is bad for us."
11. The protocol: cut seed oils from the diet, tallow on the skin, omega-3 (fish or cod liver oil) inside. "Within a matter of weeks, a huge shift."

## How he describes the mechanism (the shape, not the facts)
Villain in the jar and on the plate (seed oil) → it lodges in the skin for years and oxidizes → dry, flaky, red, older-looking skin → the fix is the fat that matches your own skin's oil → each fatty acid gets a plain job (moisturize, soften, repair) → the reason nobody told you is a 100-year-old lie about saturated fat → try it for a week, read the reviews.
It is a villain-swap mechanism. Ours (locked) is a loss mechanism: after 40 the oil is gone, the cream is water, put the oil back. His says the cream is the wrong oil. They stack cleanly: water plus the wrong oil is what's in the jar.
