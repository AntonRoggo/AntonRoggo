# Post-purchase survey, Sep 1 2025 to Sep 1 2026

Source: Post Purchase Survey export, 2,081 responses, 92 columns. Contains names, emails, addresses; the raw file stays in Downloads and is not copied here. All numbers aggregate. The Exit Intent export for the same range is empty ("No Results Found," 29 bytes).

Response rates fall through the survey: 2,081 answer question 1, about 1,250 answer the demographic questions at the end. Percentages are of those who answered each question.

## Ranked for marketing

**1. They buy for lines. 65% of buyers say so.**
"What problem are you hoping this solves?" (multi-select, n=1,692): fine lines/aging skin 64.9% · cleaner, more natural ingredients 41.5% · dry or dehydrated skin 39.4% · healthier skin overall 20.9% · sensitive or reactive skin 18.0% · redness or inflammation 13.7% · eczema or psoriasis 12.6% · acne or breakouts 7.1%.
Same order in every age band (45-54, 55-64, 65+). Matches the review desire data (lines 112 vs glow 15) and confirms the dryness lane sits third as a stated problem even though it's 1.13x as an ad lead. People who want dryness fixed are the same people who want lines fixed; lead with lines.

**2. The #1 reason to buy is trust in the ingredients, not price.**
"What was the #1 reason you decided to buy today?" (n=1,593): Other (write-in, not exported) 25.6% · cleaner alternative to conventional skincare 22.7% · heard tallow was good for my specific issue 10.0% · saw an ad and it resonated 9.9% · curious, wanted to try something new 9.9% · researched tallow and this brand stood out 8.8% · friend, family, or creator recommended 7.8% · **the price/offer made it easy 5.4%, last.**
New buyers: cleaner alternative 20%, curious 15%, ad resonated 12%, recommended 12%. The sale/price/scarcity bucket at 6.30x is closing people who were already sold; almost nobody says the deal is why they bought.

**3. The buyer is older than the brief assumes.**
Age (n=1,278): 55-64 36.9% · 65+ 32.6% · 45-54 21.7% · 35-44 6.5% · under 35 2.4%. **69% are 55 or older; 33% are 65+.** Gender: 88.6% women. Work: "Retired" is the top answer (22%). Hobbies: outdoor activities 58%, reading 45%, cooking 41%. Ethnicity: 92% white. The brief's "45 to 75" center should move to "55 to 70."

**4. The consideration window is months, not minutes.**
"How long ago did you first hear about us?" (n=1,780): 3+ months ago 63.4% · 1-3 months 14.4% · 1-4 weeks 10.2% · within the last week 6.6% · today 5.4%. Two thirds watched for a quarter or longer before buying. Matches "I've been watching these ads for at least a year." Retargeting and email carry the sale; 44% of tracked conversions are Klaviyo, 44% Facebook, 7% Google.

**5. Trust in the website is the top named blocker.**
"What almost kept you from purchasing?" (n=1,284): Other (write-in, not exported) 40.6% · wasn't ready to buy immediately 28.1% · wasn't sure I could trust the website 15.8% (about 20% including combinations) · wasn't sure about quality 7.6% · checkout confusing 3.6%. The parking-lot skeptic and made-in-Canada winners answer exactly this. A trust beat belongs in every creative, and "official site" language in every CTA.

**6. They already use one or two products. The "twelve bottles" story is wrong for 73% of them.**
"How many skincare products do you currently use?" (n=1,343): 1-2 (just the basics) 73.0% · 3-5 23.7% · 6-10 1.5% · 10+ 1.9%. Only one in four has a routine to simplify. "Replaced my expensive cream" is true for them; "threw out a shelf" isn't. Reframe the skit's staging from a crowded shelf to one jar that didn't work.

**7. Half the respondents are repeat buyers, and a quarter of the new ones came from another tallow.**
Orders count: 1 order 51.1% · 2-3 orders 34.3% · 4+ 14.6%. "Have you used tallow before?" (n=1,356): used The Tallowed Truth before 48.7% · first time with tallow 38.0% · used another tallow brand 13.3%. Among new-to-TT buyers, roughly one in four is a tallow switcher. Supports the "switched from another tallow" creative; the 269 reviews say the same.

**8. Where they heard: Facebook, then Instagram, then a person.**
(n=2,081): Facebook 52.7% · Instagram 19.2% · friend or family member 13.9% · YouTube 7.0% · Google 1.7% · TikTok 1.3%. One in seven came from word of mouth. "A friend, family member, or creator recommended it" is also the #4 stated reason. Referral and the "my daughter/sister is using it now" review lines are underused.

**9. Canada is 72% of buyers. Ontario alone is a third.**
Country: CA 72.4% · US 27.1%. Region: Ontario 33.0% · Alberta 12.2% · BC 12.1% · Quebec 4.2% · Saskatchewan 2.9% · California 2.6% · Florida 2.3% · Texas 2.2%. The made-in-Canada lane is the core customer, not a segment.

**10. What they used before: Olay is the #1 named brand, and "nothing" is second.**
Free text (n=1,381, half of it long-tail brand names): tallow or TT 9.2% · nothing/none 8.7% · "various / everything" 6.9% · Olay (all spellings) 5.8% · drugstore generic 2.4% · Clinique 1.8% · CeraVe 1.7% · coconut oil 1.5% · Nivea 1.5% · castor oil 1.3% · Aveeno 1.3% · retinol 1.1% · Mary Kay 1.0% · Cetaphil 0.9% · Vichy, Neutrogena 0.7% · **Vaseline 0.4%**. Long tail: Arbonne, Riversol, Aloette, Sukin, Three Ships, prescribed steroid creams, homemade. Olay is the enemy the audience actually names (also 17 review mentions). Crepe Erase, Gold Bond, StriVectin: zero.

**11. The basket.**
Median order $82.64, mean $87.56. Orders containing: F-Balm original 1,949 · Orange & Vanilla 541 · Lavender 394 · Face Whip Summer 323 · Field Balm 314 · Lip Balm 255 · Body Butter 159 · Black Label 99. The new scents are in nearly half of orders; that's the 4.26x "new scents" bucket in the test data. Subscription: 12.4% of orders. Buying for: myself 93%, kids 11%, partner 10%.

**12. Checkout is fine.** 95% report no issues placing the order.

## Gaps in the export
- The "Other? Write it in!" free text was not exported for the #1 reason (408 answers, 41% of repeat buyers) or for what almost stopped them (521 answers, the single largest answer). Those two fields are the most valuable in the survey and they're blank in this file. Re-export with write-in text, or check the survey tool's export settings.
- Exit Intent survey: empty for the range. Either it had no responses or the export failed.
- No ROAS, spend, or cohort data; this is who bought, not what it cost.
