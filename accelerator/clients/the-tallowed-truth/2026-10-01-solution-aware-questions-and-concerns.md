Quality: smell split ("smells like honey" vs "stinks like dead cows, so greasy," "made me sick at first," "doesn't even fade after a few hours"; unscented "can't use it on my face because of the smell"). "Made in China" on the label, two commenters ("mine states made in China"; "the label caught my eye"): conflicts with the "Made here, not in China" top ad, ask Ben. Plastic leaching. Amazon: cheaper, real one?
Efficacy: breakouts reported by buyers ("made my face break out," "used it twice and it gave me acne," cosmetician 71 "too rich... blocking my pores," repeat buyer "recurring acne"). Professionals against: esthetician sister "will not penetrate the skin," "dermatologists state that it's comedogenic," "my facial gal says please do not use." Limits: "Does not replace botox or helps with deep wrinkles... 6+ months... makes my skin feel nice."
Trust and friction: "another scam? Why don't they give samples," subscription unclear, single-tin surcharge, overseas charge, political pushback (10).
