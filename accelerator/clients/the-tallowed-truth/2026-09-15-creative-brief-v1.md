# 6. UMP Cause

**Question:** What's really causing this, and who's responsible?

## Surprising Culprit

The surface problem is dryness, dullness, and lines. The problem behind it is that the skin's own oil supply shut down and everything on the shelf is built to sit on top of skin, not to replace what it stopped making.

- The oil decline is hormonal. The literature attributes it mainly to falling androgens after menopause; estrogen loss drives thinning and collagen loss. [Study 1] Do not write "estrogen makes sebum."
- Moisturizers are 60 to 80% water. [Study 5] The rest is emulsifiers, preservatives, fragrance, and lipids, and the lipid is often mineral oil or petrolatum, which "was introduced as a cosmetic oil in the late 1800s" and remains a primary moisturizer component. [Study 4, Rawlings 2012] Petrolatum appears in one of every 14 cosmetic products. [Study 4, EWG figure, lower confidence]
