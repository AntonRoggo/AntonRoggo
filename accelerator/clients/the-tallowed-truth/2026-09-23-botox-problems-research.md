# Botox: the problems, sourced (2026-09-23)

For the "easiest fix is NOT Botox" script. Each line is usable as written; the source is beside it.

## What it is
- Botulinum toxin A is a neurotoxin, the most potent toxin known. Cosmetic Botox is the same molecule at small doses. (FDA label; Kroumpouzos 2021 review, J Cosmet Dermatol.)
- FDA boxed warning, the most serious kind: the toxin's effects can spread from the injection site to distant parts of the body, hours to weeks after injection; swallowing and breathing difficulties can be life-threatening, with reported deaths. (FDA label, 2021 and 2024.)

## It doesn't treat the skin
- It paralyzes the muscle under the wrinkle. Nothing reaches the skin itself. Lines caused by dryness, sun, or oil loss (not expression) are untouched. (Mechanism of action, all sources.)
- Effect lasts 3 to 5 months; then it's gone. (2021 review, Aesthetic Surgery Journal.)

## Repeated use
- "Serial and repeated injections may also cause undesirable and unanticipated muscle atrophy that is impossible to reverse." Changes in muscle composition persist up to 4 years after injection. (Aesthetic Surgery Journal 2016; 2021 long-term review, PubMed 34611099.)
- Case report: subcutaneous fat under the forehead thinned to 0.8 mm from 2.0 mm after injections; dermatologists describe "visible waviness of the skin overlying the muscles treated." (PMC11938406; Yahoo Health derm interviews.)
- The long-term evidence base is thin and much of it industry-funded; few studies follow past 6 months. (PubMed 34611099.)

## Resistance
- Neutralizing antibodies: 0.5% in short trials, but in patients whose Botox stopped working, 32.5% had antibodies (56.7% for Dysport). Re-treating before 12 weeks and higher doses drive it. (PMC11508269; JAAD Int 15-patient series.)

## The frozen face
- Eyelid ptosis (droop) is the most common significant side effect; brow droop 1 to 5%; asymmetry and the frozen look are listed complications. (Nestor 2021; Kroumpouzos 2021.)
- Compensation: freeze one muscle and the neighbors take over, producing new lines, the "bunny lines" beside the nose. (PMC13428043; injector literature.)
- Neal and Chartrand 2011: women who had Botox were worse at reading emotion in other faces than women who had filler, because they could no longer mimic expressions. (Social Psychological and Personality Science; Harvard Health summary.)

## Around the mouth specifically (the lip flip)
- Drooling, inability to drink through a straw, trouble whistling, speaking, brushing teeth; numbness; lasts until it wears off. (Cleveland Clinic; NewBeauty; Forbes 2024 "proceed with caution.")
