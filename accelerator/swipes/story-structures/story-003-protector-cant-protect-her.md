## 1. Hook formula

**[A man puts hands on his woman] + [he cannot respond, and his body is the visible reason] + [she looks at him, or says the line] + V.O.: "She didn't have to say it. I'm too fat to protect the woman I love. He has no idea what that night cost me."**

## 4. Timeline, checked

Six weeks on the drops. Before that, "five days a week for three months" in the helper's scene, which the source never marks on screen (fixed in v2 with a card and a V.O. bridge). "This gut hasn't moved in three years." Ages: 34 and a helper who is "the same age" (cast says 36). Holds. Week six "Sharp. Lean. Present" from 260 is the one the comments would flag; v2 describes instead and moves the number to week twelve.

## 5. Logic catches on the source (all fixed in v2)

1. Three-on-one, and she goads him. The viewer sides with him and turns on her ([[story-ad-logic]] checklist 11, the cholesterol-ad rule, word for word). Fix: one man, and his body is why it fails; nobody swings, they pity him.
2. "What that night cost me" costs him nothing on screen. Fix: the guest room, six weeks, paid off at week five.
3. Three months disappear between the doctor and the locker room. Fix: a card.
4. The grocery hook cannot share this hold (the mirror line "carry her groceries," the gas station return). Fix: replaced.
5. "Lean" at week six from 260. Fix: describe, and carry the number to week twelve.
6. The man re-offends at the exact moment they return. Fix: he sees him, and does not even try.
7. No tipping point for how a 34-year-old got there. Fix: "three years since the knee, three years behind a desk."

## 6. Why it is in the library anyway

The structure is sound even where the execution was not: a public failure with the body as the visible cause, a partner who says the thing once, a mirror that names what he used to be, a wrong authority, a peer guide who was worse, a household skeptic who gives him six weeks, one witness per week, and a return to the same place where the same situation resolves without a word. That is a complete male spine, and it is the only one in the bank that is not a betrayal. When a male lead comes in on shame rather than cheating, this is the skeleton.

Candidate Spine B (male shame, return to the scene): 003 plus the "came home a day early" script share the mirror, the doctor, the gym months, a peer or coworker guide, weekly witnesses, and a public payoff where the wound's witness sees him again. Extract when a second story on it is pasted as a winner.
