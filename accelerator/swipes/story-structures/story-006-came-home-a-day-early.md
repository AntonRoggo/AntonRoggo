## 1. Hook formula

**[Came home early] + [saw it] + [the silence: didn't yell, didn't break anything, closed the door, walked back downstairs] + [what the silence cost or changed].** H1 is the silence itself. ## 4. Timeline, checked

**Caught:**
1. **H3 says "a few weeks later."** The hold runs three months plus the doctor plus three weeks before the truck. "A few months later." (Fixed in the Mexican-American adaptation.)
2. **"Sharp. Lean. Awake. Present."** Assertion, the same line 003 uses. At 22-plus down from 35 over it is within reach, so it stands, but it is the one line in the script that tells instead of shows.
3. **Keith's sister, "into natural health."** The checklist's own example of a credential that is not one. It is the banked line, so it stays, but this is the weakest link in an otherwise airtight script.

## 5. What to watch when reusing

- Every specific is a man's specific: the Camry behind her car, the door cracked four inches, 5:15, the clinic he built, the jacket from 32, number 12, the interception. Swap them for another trade or another ICP the way the Mexican-American cut did (the lonchera, the Silverado, the mayordomo, Apá), one for one. Do not generalize them.
- The ex never gets a line beyond his name. She freezes in the robe, she stands alone on the wrong side, she says "you look..." and doesn't finish. The story never gives her a chance to explain, and it is stronger for it.
- The woman on the bleachers is not a romance. She grabs his arm at the interception and says "that's your boy." That is all. She exists so the viewer sees a woman look at him.
