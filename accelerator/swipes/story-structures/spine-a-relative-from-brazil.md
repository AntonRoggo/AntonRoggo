## Hook formula

[The wound in one or two lines] + [the ages or the years] + "What my [relative] flew in from Brazil to show me" + [changed everything / is the only reason I didn't crumble / made sure I got the last word].

Three variants per story: the wound itself, seeing the other woman or the aftermath, the fear or the insult. 002's winner was the variant that withheld the most.

## Beats, in order

1. **The silent catch.** Came home early on a Tuesday. Heard noise. Walked up. There they were, in our bed. [Ages or years]. Didn't scream. Closed the door. Downstairs. Stared at the wall until it got dark.

Symptom list in her words: belly, puffy face every morning, jawline gone, skin dull or glow gone, [edges thin]. Then the concession, dialed by the operator: 001 concedes ("I got why he did it"), 002 almost does ("I almost believed them").

5. **The wrong authority.** Doctor didn't look up. "It's just [menopause / stress]. [Eat less, walk more / sleep more, drink water]." Cried in the car.

11. **Mechanism.** [Cause: estrogen drops / months of cortisol]. The list of what sat there. Locked in. Hardened. Stuck. [In your belly, in your face, under your jaw.] Not fat, waste. Can't burn waste. Have to drain it.
12. **Four herbs, each with a job tied to the cause.** Stillingia breaks down. Prickly ash restarts the circulation [the cause] shut down. Cleavers sweeps out. Red clover calms so it doesn't build back.
13. **Objection two and the promise.** "There's no way drops can fix [years / months] of this." / "Six weeks. By week [one] your [face]. By week three your [jawline / waist]. By week six you won't recognize yourself. Watch."
14. **Six weeks.** One body change, one number when there is one, one witness per week. Week 1: bathroom every hour, face, [ring / bracelet] loose, told no one. Week 2: [belt / jeans], bloat gone, first witness ([daughter on the phone / mom at breakfast]), "you [sound / look] different," shrugged. Week 3: jawline, skin, sleeping through the night for the first time since [the tipping point]. Week 4: down [12 to 14], eating normally, [brain fog / the event two weeks away and didn't think about it]. Week 5: down [16 to 19], the dress in the back of the closet, put it on and cried, [the app, a man with kind eyes]. Week 6: energy, [the date / the wedding day she skips].

The bathroom: a stranger her age at the sink, "I'm sorry, I have to ask," her husband just left, she tells, the stranger orders at the sink. Back out: the betrayer says her name like a question, "You look incredible. What did you do." She doesn't smile, doesn't speak, walks past. [The new man's hand on her back.] "Glowing. Not from [him]. From me."
16. **The circle.** [Daughter / roommate] sees the bottle on the counter, "[why do you look like that / we're the same age and you're glowing]," tries it, two weeks later a photo: "I look like you."
17. **The truth.** He was a coward, not right. [001: he couldn't handle a woman his own age. 002: that's not revenge, that's consequences; cowards do coward things.] "[Cause] just locked everything in. The second I drained it, I got me back. Not [30-year-old / the old] me. The me I was supposed to be at [age]. Sharp. Awake. Alive. The woman he should have been terrified to lose."
18. **The close.** "Look. I don't know where you're at right now. Maybe [the wound, generalized]. Maybe you've tried [the list]. Maybe your doctor [didn't look up]." "Don't let them sell you Ozempic and [hormone therapy / another self-care routine] when your body just needs to drain what [the cause] locked in." Origin line. Four herbs. "[Relative] said they sell out constantly. Small batches. I checked yesterday and they were almost out. If you still see the orange button below, you got lucky. 60 day money back guarantee. But honestly, after six weeks, you won't need it. That's all it took for me."

## The fork

**Relative flies in** (001, 002): the arrival is a beat ("didn't hug me first"), the bottle comes on day three, the guide is in the house for the six weeks and says "there she is" at the mirror.

**Relative on the phone** (004): the call is the beat. She is the first person to ask how the lead is, the wound is said aloud for the first time, and she sends the bottle. No arrival, no day three, no one in the house. The lead does the six weeks alone, which is why the seamstress has to be the witness.

Pick by wound. A wound with a house to come home to gets the flight. A wound the lead has been carrying alone gets the phone.

## Known slips carried by both (fix on reuse)

- "Every single week" against a daily dose. Use "every single day."
- "[Afro-]Brazilian women have used three of them for a hundred years." The herbs are not Brazilian. The clinic credential is the one that holds; keep it, drop the century.
- Dose. 001 does not state it. 002 says "two drops every morning." 004 says half a dropper morning and night. The rest of the bank says one full dropper, or one to two droppers. One answer, then every script.
- Guarantee. 60 days in 001 and 002, 30 in 004 and the male scripts. One answer.
- "Stillingia breaks down the years of hardened waste" when the story's stress is ten months (004). Match the herb line to the story's clock.
