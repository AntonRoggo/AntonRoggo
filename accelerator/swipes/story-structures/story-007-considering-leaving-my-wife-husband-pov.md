STRATEGIC LAYER

● Angle: The husband who loves his wife and is watching her weight kill her. "I can't watch the women I love and mother of my child slowly kill herself." Sold through the ultimatum he doesn't want to give, then the update where the honest conversation worked: "my wife has lost 35 fucking lbs."

● Market Awareness Stage: Problem-aware. He knows exactly what the problem is and that his attempts have failed, and has no solution: "I don't know what I can do, I feel like i've tried everything." The update resolves on a conversation, not a product, so the reader is never moved to solution-aware.

● Sophistication Stage: Stage 1 for the "product" (there is none; the mechanism is a direct claim, one honest talk).
