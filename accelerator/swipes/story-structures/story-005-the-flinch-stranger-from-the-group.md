## 1. Hook formula

**One moment, cut at three intensities.** Same bedroom, same act, same flinch. V1: he compliments her an inch from her mouth and she smells it on his breath. V2: she looks up at the wrong second and sees the pull at his nose. V3: she sees it, gets off the bed, locks the bathroom door, cries with her hand over her mouth. Then one V.O. for all three: "He didn't say anything. He didn't have to. This is the exact second I've been dreading for two years." Same construction as 003's "She didn't have to say it."

## 3. What is new to the bank

- **A symptom, not a weight.** The first story where the body is not measured in pounds or dress sizes. The proof is the absence of a flinch.
- **Diagnosis by guessing.** The guide earns trust by describing the lead's symptom before the lead does. It replaces the credential: she doesn't need a clinic, she needs to have had it.
- **The kitchen sink.** A mechanism metaphor the viewer can picture in one second, and it explains three things at once: why washing fails, why the tests are clean, why it comes back.
- **The pre-frame on side effects.** "First week you're gonna pee constantly. Don't freak out." Said twice, by Renee and then by Mia. It turns the product's most alarming effect into proof it's working.
- **The guide chain.** "Somebody in that same group explained it to me." Renee was converted the same way she converts Mia, and Mia converts Em the same way. Three links, same script.
- **The convert is the close.** No direct address. The offer is spoken by a character to a character. "70% off with a 30 day money back guarantee, so you've got nothing to lose. But it sells out constantly, so order it tonight."
- **The payoff mirrors the hook shot for shot.** Neck, collarbone, stomach, moving down, and the face. Same camera, opposite result.

## 5. Timeline, checked

Two years of the symptom, two years with Josh ("it's been like two years"), Renee four years, the box in two days, six weeks, the six-week call, the payoff, then Em. Holds.

**Caught:**
1. **Scene 2 only follows Hook 3.** "You locked the door. I could hear you crying in there" happens in V3 alone. V1 (she turns to the wall) and V2 (she pulls her panties back on) never lock a door. The three hooks are not interchangeable as written. Fix: "You shut me out. I could hear you in there" is still V3-only. Hook-neutral: "You won't even look at me after. What am I supposed to do with that? Is it me?"
2. **"Every partner since has made a face"** in the 2am post, against a two-year relationship with Josh that covers the whole two years. Either she has been with Josh less than two years, or the line is "He's made the face. Every time." Checklist 5.
3. **Scene 1 is out of order.** The doctor comes first, then "morning after the bedroom." Either the doctor visit is before the hook (it needs a card) or it belongs after Scene 2. As cut, the viewer has to work out that the parking lot and the morning after are different days.
4. **The offer in a character's mouth.** "70% off with a 30 day money back guarantee, so you've got nothing to lose. But it sells out constantly." This is the ad talking through Em's best friend. It is the format's choice, and it may be why the format works, but it is the line a viewer would call an ad. Noted, not fixed.
5. **Renee** is now used three times in the bank with three roles (narrator in 001, best friend in the cookout, stranger here). See the register.

## 6. What to watch when reusing

- The spine transfers to any shameful symptom the product can plausibly reach (odor, bloat, puffiness, skin). The kitchen sink is the mechanism block for all of them; only the "nearest exit" changes.
- The guide's credential here is having had it. For a symptom nobody admits to, a stranger who admits to it is more credible than a relative, and the anonymous group is where such strangers are. Do not move this guide to a kitchen counter.
- The timeline class is still LONG. The symptom is not weight, but the product is the same, and the proof beat (six weeks, "nothing there") sits where every other story's does.

## 7. Source text, verbatim

HOOKS
VERSION 1
Bedroom. Low light. Josh comes back up from under the covers. Close to her face. Smiling.
Josh: "You're so beautiful, you know that?"
He's talking an inch from her mouth.
She gets the whiff. Off his breath. Onion.
Her whole body goes still.
Josh: "What? What's wrong?"
Mia: "Nothing. I'm just tired."
She turns to the wall. Eyes open in the dark.
VERSION 2
Bedroom. She slides her panties off.
She looks up at exactly the wrong second.
His face. Half a second. The tiniest pull at his nose.
He catches it. Smiles too late.
Josh: "Come here."
Mia: "Actually... can we just watch something?"
She pulls her panties back on under the blanket.
VERSION 3
Bedroom. Josh kisses her neck. Her collarbone. Her stomach. Moving down.
He slows.
The face. A flinch he tries to swallow.
She sees it.
She's off the bed before he can say a word. Sheet wrapped around her. Bathroom door. Lock.
Josh (through the door): "Mia. Babe. What did I do?"
She sits on the edge of the tub with her hand over her mouth so he can't hear her cry.
VO
Mia (inner voice): "He didn't say anything. He didn't have to. This is the exact second I've been dreading for two years."
HOLD
SCENE 1
Doctor's office. Mia on the exam table, dressed. The doctor scrolls the chart.
Doctor: "So we've done the boric acid... probiotics, the washes... and you said you did a cleanse on your own."
Mia: "Nothing works. It goes away for like a day and comes right back."
Doctor: "Well, your panel's clean. No BV, no yeast, no infection. Everything looks normal."
Mia: "It's not normal. Trust me."
Doctor: (small shrug) "Some women just run a little different. Honestly? This might just be you."
Beat. Mia nods slowly.
Mia: "Right."
Parking lot. She sits in the car. Doesn't start it.
SCENE 2
Morning after the bedroom. Kitchen. Josh sets his coffee down. Careful, like the room might break.
Josh: "So are we gonna talk about last night?"
Mia: "I was tired."
Josh: "Mia."
Mia: "I was tired, Josh."
Josh: "You're tired every night. The lights are always off. And every time I try to... you know. You pull me back up. Every time. It's been like two years."
She stares into her coffee.

Mia: "It's not you."
Josh: "Then what?"
Her mouth opens. Closes.
Mia: "I'm handling it."
Josh: "Handling what?"
She's already walking out with her coffee.
SCENE 3
2am. Couch. Josh asleep down the hall. Mia's face lit by the phone. An anonymous women's group. She types, and we hear her voice, barely above a whisper.
Mia (Voiceover): "I'm scared of sex because of the smell. It started two years ago and I can't get rid of it. Every partner since has made a face. My tests are all negative. My doctor says it's just me. I just want to feel feminine again. Someone please help."
Her thumb hovers. She posts it.
Replies roll in. She scrolls past them, shaking her head.
Mia (Voiceover): "Boric acid. Tried it. Probiotics. Tried it. Tried it. Tried it..."
She stops scrolling. A DM. A stranger's voice reads it.
Renee (Voiceover): "I had this for four years. It's not what you think it is. Can I call you?"

Next evening. Mia in her parked car. Phone to her ear. First seconds of the call, a little awkward.
Mia: "Hi. Sorry, this is weird. I don't usually call strangers from the internet."
Renee: (a small laugh) "Nobody does. Okay, let me guess. Started a couple years ago, totally out of nowhere. And it never went away."
Mia: "...Yeah. Exactly."
Renee: "Kind of oniony? Sour? You shower and by lunch it's back."
Mia: "Okay, how do you know that."
Renee: "Because I had the exact same thing. Four years. I cried in more parking lots than I can count."
Mia: (quiet laugh, almost crying) "I'm in a parking lot right now."
Renee: "Of course you are. Okay. Do you want to know what it actually is? Because it took me four years and it's stupid how simple it is."
Mia: "Please."

Night. Mia on the couch, phone to her ear. She's half smiling before Renee even picks up.
