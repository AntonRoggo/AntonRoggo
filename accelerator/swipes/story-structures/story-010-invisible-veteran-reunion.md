# Story 010: "Invisible" (veteran at his unit reunion, drama)

Filed 2026-10-12. Source: the operator. It is a Lymphoria drama ad, marked as a performance winner. It is the reference script for [[drama-ad-guidelines]], and new dramas are written to match its length (within 10 percent).

Taxonomy:
- **Wound:** Invisible to his own people. His platoon and his wife look past him. His wife lights up for the squadmate who got in shape.
- **Lead:** M, 42, White, Army veteran (infantry, two tours), about 265, aggro, tells war stories.
- **Guide:** Jake Brennan, his old squadmate. He was 280 two years ago. He is also the man his wife was looking at, so the guide is tied to the wound.
- **Delivery:** Gym dialogue while they train: kettlebell, the thumb-dent test, the inset.
- **Timeline:** LONG. The clock is Veterans Day, six weeks out, with one witness per week.
- **Payoff:** The same VFW hall and the same people. Every hook is flipped.
- **Close:** A short VO naming the viewer's wound, then the offer (70% off, 30-day guarantee, button).

**CAST**

Still cocky. Never skinny.
- **Rachel:** White, 39, his wife. Pretty, social, lights up any room she walks into.
- **Jake Brennan:** White, 41, Cole's old squadmate. Same frame as Cole, in great shape now, fitted t-shirt, easy smile. The kind of guy wives notice. Still a normal guy, not a fitness model. He has to look like he used to be heavier.
- **Captain Sutter:** White, 58, their old platoon leader, retired. Lean, gray high-and-tight, loud, loves a toast.
- **Young vet:** White, 26, just got out, jacked, high-and-tight, Oakleys on his cap.

**VERSION 1**

`Unit reunion. A VFW hall. Platoon banners, cheap beer. Cole stands by the bar with Rachel. Gut over his belt. Sweating through a polo two sizes too small.`

`The door opens. Jake Brennan walks in. Fitted t-shirt. Built like a recruiting poster.`

`Rachel sees him and lights up.`

**Rachel:** "JAKE!"

`She runs across the room and jumps on him. Arms around his neck. He catches her like she weighs nothing.`

**Jake:** "Rach! Look at you!"

**Rachel:** "Look at YOU. Oh my God."

`She's still hanging on him. Her hand is on his chest.`

`Cole stands at the bar holding two beers.`

**Cole:** "I'm right here, Brennan."

`Nobody hears him.`

**VERSION 2**

`Unit reunion. Captain Sutter stands on a chair with a beer in the air.`

**Captain Sutter:** "To Second Platoon! Where's my Hollis? HOLLIS!"

`Cole raises his beer. Rachel is right next to him.`

`Sutter looks right at him. Then past him. Keeps searching the room.`

**Captain Sutter:** "Anybody seen Hollis?"

**Vet next to Cole:** "Sir. He's right here."

**Captain Sutter:** "Holy shit. Didn't even recognize you, big man! Rachel, what the hell are you feeding him?"

`SOUND: The whole room laughs.`

`Every head turns to Rachel. She laughs along, red in the face. She won't look at Cole.`

**VERSION 3**

`Unit reunion. Cole and Rachel by the bar. A young vet, fresh out and jacked, leans in to shake Cole's hand.`

**Young vet:** "Thank you for your service, sir. What'd you do, supply?"

**Cole:** "Infantry. Two tours. Kunar."

`The young vet looks him up and down.`

**Young vet:** "No shit? You don't look it."

`He turns his back on Cole and asks Rachel where the bathroom is.`

`Rachel stares down into her drink.`

**SCENE 1: THE PARKING LOT**

Length: ~25s. Pace: Quiet, then one explosion, then short and cold. Music: The reunion, muffled through the wall. Cast: Cole, Jake.

`Night. VFW parking lot. Cole walks out alone to his truck.`

**Cole:** "FUCK!"

`He slams his fist into the tailgate. Again. Again.`

`SOUND: Metal denting.`

`Footsteps on the gravel behind him. Jake.`

**Jake:** "Hollis."

**Cole:** "Get the fuck away from me, Brennan. And stay the fuck away from my wife."

**Jake:** "Two years ago I was two-eighty. My wife stopped looking at me too."

**Cole:** "Don't fucking compare us."

**Jake:** "When you cool off, come by my gym. Saturday. Six AM."

`He walks back inside.`

**Cole:** "Fuck you."

**SCENE 2: THE DRIVE HOME**

Length: ~15s. Pace: Cold. Just the road. Music: None. Cast: Cole, Rachel.

`Rachel in the passenger seat, looking out the window.`

**Rachel:** "Jake looked incredible."

**Cole:** "Yeah. I saw you looking."

**Rachel:** "Don't do that."

**Cole:** "Don't do what? I was standing right there, Rach. Nobody saw me. You didn't see me."

`She doesn't answer.`

**SCENE 3: JAKE'S GYM**

Length: ~75s. Pace: A long pause in the truck. Then keep them moving. Jake coaches him through sets while he talks. Music: Gym speaker, low. Cast: Cole, Jake.

**SCENE NOTE: MAKE INTERACTIVE. DON'T MAKE IT BORING OR PEOPLE STANDING IN ONE PLACE TALKING. WE NEED TO EXPLAIN IT WITHOUT LOSING VIEWERS.**

`EDITOR NOTE: Cutaways play under the dialogue and add no runtime. The visuals are suggestions. Improvise and generate your own mechanism shots if they sell it better, like the sludge in the body shots we used before. `Saturday. 5:58 AM. Cole sits in his truck outside a garage gym. Engine running. He almost pulls away. He turns it off.`

`Inside. Chalk, rubber mats, a flag on the wall. Jake is already warmed up.`

**Jake:** "Figured you'd show."

**Cole:** "I'm not here for a pep talk, Brennan."

**Jake:** "Good. Grab the kettlebell."

`Cole swings it. Ten reps and he's done. Bent over, sucking air.`

**Cole:** "Fuck."

`Jake grabs Cole's hand off the kettlebell and presses his thumb hard into the back of it. When he lets go, the dent stays.`

**Jake:** "See that? Fat doesn't hold a dent. That's fluid. That's waste."

**Cole:** "Waste?"

**Jake:** "Your body makes it every day. Dead cells. Old hormones. Toxins. Your lymphatic system is the drain that carries it out. It's got no pump. It only moves when you move."

`VISUAL: Inside the body. The drain backing up. Waste soaking into the fat like water into a sponge. The fat swelling and going hard.`

**Jake:** "We came home, sat on our asses, drank, stopped sleeping. The drain backs up and all that shit soaks into your fat like a sponge. That's the gut. That's the puffy face."

**Cole:** "I lift. The VA says my numbers are fine."

**Jake:** "The VA told me the same shit. That's why the gym made you stronger and never smaller. That's why you're gassed after ten swings."

**Cole:** "So what'd you do?"

**Jake:** "Opened the drain."

`Jake pulls a small glass bottle out of his gym bag. Golden inside.`

**Jake:** "Lymphoria. Four herbs. Half a dropper, morning and night. Tastes like honey. It's liquid, so it's in your blood in fifteen minutes."

**Cole:** "Drops. You're shitting me."

**Jake:** "I still train. I still eat right. Nothing came off till I started these. First week you'll be pissing every hour. That's the waste leaving."

`He tosses Cole the bottle.`

**Jake:** "Veterans Day's in six weeks. Whole platoon's gonna be there."

`That night. Bathroom. Half a dropper on his tongue. Golden. Sweet like honey, then a low warmth.`

`He hides the bottle behind the shaving cream.`

**SCENE 5: VETERANS DAY**

Length: ~25s. Pace: Let it build. Every head turns. Hold on faces. Music: The hall, loud. Cast: Cole, Rachel, Jake, Captain Sutter, the young vet, the platoon and partners.

`The same VFW hall. Flags everywhere. The platoon.`

`Cole walks in with Rachel on his arm. The whole room turns. This time nobody's watching the door for Brennan.`

`Jake raises his beer at Cole. Cole nods back.`

`Rachel slides in under his arm.`

**Rachel (quiet):** "Everybody's looking at you."

**Cole:** "Let 'em look."

**WHY IT WORKS**

- The wound is being invisible. In a room full of his own people, his platoon and his wife look right past him.
- All three hooks hit him in the same room on the same night, and each one ends on Rachel's reaction.
- The helper is the guy she was looking at. Cole tells him to fuck off first, so the help lands harder.
- The clock is a real event with the same people: Veterans Day, six weeks out.
- The payoff flips every hook in the same hall. Sutter spots him first, the young vet says "I can tell," and Rachel looks at Cole.
- The dialogue is short and blunt. Nobody explains how they feel: "Yeah. I saw you looking." "Let 'em look."
