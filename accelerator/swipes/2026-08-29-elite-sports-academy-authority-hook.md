# Swipe: institutional authority + anger hook

Related: [[Promotion]] [[Workbench vs Memory]]

## The hook
"An elite sports academy just told me something that explains why some boys develop faster, even when the training is exactly the same. If you're a mom with a teenage son, this is going to piss you off. Because it pissed me off."

## Mechanism
Five elements in sequence:
1. Institutional authority (not a person, an institution)
2. Explains a mystery the audience already lives with
3. Audience targeting by identity
4. Emotional stake (anger)
5. Proof of emotion (I already felt it, so you trust me to deliver)

The authority is an institution turning insider, not a credential. The anger is on behalf of someone you love, not yourself. The "because it pissed me off" line does double duty: proves the speaker felt it first, and gives permission to feel it too.

## Where it fits
Adaptable to any angle where an insider source reveals something the audience should have been told. Strongest when the audience is already frustrated and doesn't know why.
