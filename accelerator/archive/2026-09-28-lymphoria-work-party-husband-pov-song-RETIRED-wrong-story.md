## 4. Story logic pass (workflows/story-ad-logic.md)

Chain: Janet asks, Lisa leaves, the coworkers laugh where Kevin can hear → "You good?" / "It was the dress" → that night, the text he deletes → the kitchen: he asks for the ordinary fixes, she has already tried them and stopped, he says the stake (heart disease, the boy at sixteen), "...alright, I hear you" → the hall mirror → Malik: Dana was worse, asked at her sister's wedding, the gym won't fix it, it's not fat → the drain, no pump, after a baby and after forty, stuck, hardens → four herbs, nineteen in six weeks → the relay in six lines, "drain it out how," the offer, "for me," "...alright, I'll try it" → Day 1 to Week 6, sneakers at the door in week five → the boy: "you look like you" → same party, same dress, "still not pregnant," no laughing, "let them ask" → Kevin's CTA.

What it caught and what I did:
1. **The witness hears, the lead doesn't (tailgate rule).** In the Lisa song she overheard the bathroom. Here the coworkers talk behind Kevin, and Lisa is across the room. Her line at H3, "I'm fine. It was the dress," is the tailgate's "best day I've had in months": the denial that makes the talk necessary.
2. **The contradiction between the sources.** The Lisa song has her eating clean and walking every day; the Reddit has her turning down every idea. Resolved in four lines: she said no to every one, "not because she's lazy, because she'd tried... nothing moved, so she stopped." Effort on record before the talk, refusal explained, no accountability on her.
3. **No ultimatum given.** Operator ruling: he doesn't want to leave her. The Reddit's "thinking about separating" becomes the text he types and deletes, and the kitchen opens on "I'm not going anywhere. I'm scared." The CTA names the ultimatum he almost gave and says she didn't need one.
4. **Checklist 4, the guide.** Malik's credential is the tailgate's: his wife had it worse, he learned the herbs from her. "How do you even know all this?" / "Because Dana found these drops." Dana's "asked when she was due at her own sister's wedding" is the tailgate's "looked 10 months pregnant," pointed at the hook.
5. **The clog cause absolves.** The tailgate keeps the beer and blames the waste. Here the birth is the cause ("the weight came with him and never left"), after forty stacks on it, and "it's not fat, it's waste" lands twice. Nothing she ate is named as the cause.
6. **The mirror beat is hers.** Tailgate: he lifts the shirt, it barely moves. Here she pulls the dress flat and lets it go. Same beat, her body, no line.
7. **S6, who sees her.** Kevin is the spouse, so the tailgate's wife slot goes to the boy: "Mom?" / "You look like you," the tailgate's "you look like yourself again" in a ten-year-old's words. It also pays off "I need you to see him at sixteen."
8. **Arithmetic.** Lisa 46, the boy 10, "ten years ago," "she looks at herself like she did ten years ago." Nineteen in six weeks is under the 4-lb-a-week ceiling. Fourteen at week four (the tailgate's own). Five months in the Reddit compressed to six weeks, the bank's clock.
9. **Same event, recognized.** Same party, same dress, same two guys, no laughing. Janet knows her at once. Walmart ruling holds.
10. **"Just try it. For me."** The relay closes on it, as the tailgate does. Her answers are the tailgate's three words both times: "...alright. I hear you." / "...alright. I'll try it."

Watch:
- "Before you call me an asshole, let me explain" is the Reddit's first line moved to line nine, after the phone. It is the strongest line in the source and the reader's suspicion of Kevin is the whole hook, so it earns its place. If the profanity is a problem for the platform, "Before you judge me, let me explain."
- "I thought it was her head. She got that fixed." is the Reddit's PPD beat in two lines. It is his read, not a knock on her, but it is the one place a viewer could hear him blaming her. Cut both lines if it reads wrong; nothing downstream depends on them.
- The mechanism says "after a baby it slows down." If you want the c-section cause from the farmers market completion ("they cut through the drains"), it is one line in Malik's mouth, but only if Lisa had a c-section on record.
- Dose is the tailgate's "twice a day, under the tongue," not "half a dropper morning and night." Both are in the bank. Still the account's open ruling.
- Guarantee: 30 days, the tailgate's own.
