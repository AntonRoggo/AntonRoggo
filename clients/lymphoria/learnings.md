# Lymphoria — learnings

- [2026-10-12, Granola Oct 12 call] Spend fell 60% in week 41 and NCPA rose. The main cause was ready ads not getting launched, not creative quality. Check launch volume first when CAC spikes.
- [2026-10-12, Granola Oct 12 call] In protection stories, a direct human villain (abusive boyfriend, disrespectful stranger) beats an abstract threat (robbery, disaster), and an ongoing villain beats a one-off. The dismissal should happen in front of wife or family. Peak converting age is 45-54.
- [2026-10-12, Granola Oct 12 call] Haris's pregnancy-betrayal movie ad ran at $54 CPA with 18% hold on a 7-minute video. Heavy dialogue beats voiceover for the "real movie" feel. The Black-ICP sister/betrayal version keeps beating the white version.
- [2026-10-12, Granola Oct 12 call] The constipation market is about 2x the sticky-poop market, and a customer call said the drops resolved her constipation. Use authority-figure formats, not songs, for this angle. Winter timing helps.
- [2026-10-12, Granola Oct 12 call] In long movie ads the first 30-60 seconds decide everything. Once viewers are in the story they finish 5-7 minute ads, so the movie feel must not drop when the mechanism starts.
- [2026-10-07, Granola Oct 7 call] In current winners the mechanism and product intro barely change. The hook/story lead is the main variable to iterate.
- [2026-10-07, Granola Oct 7 call] Pick songs by demographic: modern country works for women in their 30s, and 50+ women respond to breakup songs from their 20s. Never use recognisable copyrighted characters (DMCA); mimic the vibe, palette and behaviour.
- [2026-10-02, CS Learning Call Gemini notes] Blended CAC was ~$70 at $400-500K/day across Google and Meta. New concepts took 50% of spend at $73 CAC. The week-2 batch hit rate was 16%, then fell to 5.8%.
- [2026-10-02, CS Learning Call Gemini notes] Concepts now burn out in days (one went from $15K/day to $200/day by day 4) because song and weight-loss ads are saturated. Keep finding new ICP pockets and high-intensity emotions.
- [2026-10-02, Granola Oct 2 call] Ego/respect is the current arbitrage for men: a strong former self (athlete, soldier, popular guy) vs. today's weight, as in the veteran reunion and the former athlete at a wedding. Men beat women on CAC that week. Protection ads ran at $65 CPA (son) and $70-80 (daughter) on ~$15K spend each.

- [2026-10-02, CS Learning Call Gemini notes] Naming conditions like arthritis or autoimmune disease is a compliance gray zone, like kidney and liver. A general "holistic health journey" angle with no disease named is approved for MOF.
- [2026-10-02, CS Learning Call Gemini notes] Slap-opening hooks got over 60% hook rate. Re-hooking older violent or controversial winners is the agreed way to revive them, and violence has not triggered Meta bans so far.
