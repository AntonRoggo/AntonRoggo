# Lymphoria

ClientsOS lists the start date as 2026-07-22. Anton was working inside the account before that. The sample creator brief is dated Jul 2, and he attended the Jul 10 and Jul 13 calls. Anton works as one of Lymphoria's in-house creative strategists. He uses a lymphoria.co account and sits in their learnings calls. He is not running a separate agency relationship. On Jul 22 he said he was "only doing AppLovin". By August he was briefing Meta video, UGC and the Spanish ads, and he managed the Spanish editors. The team owns the account: Milad Saleh (leadership), Roko/"Lymphoria Marketing" (head of creative), Haris, Kuba, Niklas/Nicholas and Tysin (strategists), Serena (UGC/creator sourcing) and Nathan (media buyer).

## What they sell

- **Product:** Nature's Sunrise Lymphatic Drainage Drops, sold as "Lymphoria". It is a liquid herbal concentrate (tincture) taken under the tongue. The pitch is that it "absorbs in 15 minutes instead of dying in your stomach like capsules". PDP: https://lymphoria.co/products/natures-sunrise-lymphatic-drainage-supplement
- **Formula (four herbs):** Stillingia Root ("breaks down" hardened waste), Cleavers ("sweeps/flushes it out"), Prickly Ash Bark ("restarts circulation/the pump") and Red Clover Blossom ("calms the inflammation so it doesn't build back"). Every script uses this order and these roles.
- **Claims used in briefs (unconfirmed against the PDP):** "third-party lab tested in the USA", "full-spectrum extracts", "clinical strength doses", "no fillers", "made in the USA", "tastes like honey", "thick, dark, honey-coloured liquid in a glass bottle".
- **Dose:** the briefs disagree. They say "two drops a day", "one dropper a day/under the tongue every morning" and "half a dropper morning, half at night". The newest briefs (Sep, U942-944, U963-965, U978-980) use half a dropper twice a day (unconfirmed).
- **Offer ladder** (Notion Products page, prices shown in RSD, probably a geo-localised view of the store):
  - Buy One: 4,200 RSD
  - **Buy 2, Get 1 Free: 8,400 RSD** ("Most Popular", free shipping). This is the default offer beat in Anton's July scripts.
  - Buy 3, Get 2 Free: 12,500 RSD
  - Subscribe & Save 25%: delivery every 4 weeks, cancel anytime. Used for retention closes (e.g. V79 end card).
- **Offer language in the Sep/Oct UGC briefs:** "up to 70% off", "huge sale", "free shipping", "50% off deal" (in the "I lied" script). The AppLovin SOP warns that a "70% off this week only" running for months is a fake-urgency violation.
- **Guarantee:** inconsistent. The Notion product page says "Guarantee/review-count: not provided yet — confirm". Briefs use both a **60-day** money-back guarantee (Master Idea Sheet Briefs 1-3, U813-815, U936-938, U999-1001) and a **30-day** one (U939-941, U942-944, U963-965, U978-980). Confirm before every brief.
- **Social proof stats in briefs (all unconfirmed and mutually inconsistent):** "over 40,000 five-star reviews", "over 100,000 customers", "over 350,000 women have used it", "tens of thousands of women". Treat none as verified.
- **Where sold:** lymphoria.co (main), and Amazon. Per the Jul 10 call, the Amazon store hit best-seller in its category within two weeks of launch. Ads also run on Google/YouTube (CPA ~$35 on Aug 21) and AppLovin. Leadership talks about future retail (Target/Walmart) and a **second SKU** that is coming (Jul 10, unconfirmed what it is). Copycat listings exist. Lymphoria built an in-house IP/brand-protection team, and briefs say to buy "only from our official site or the official Lymphoria listing on Amazon".
- **Scale context:** "first-purchase profitable" at ~$50 CAC with strong retention (Jul 13). Spend was ~$280K/day in late July and later reported at $400-500K/day blended across Google and Meta (Oct 2).

## Who buys

The detailed avatars live in `avatars/`. In the Notion Avatars DB every avatar is still marked "To Test", although the calls show several are proven in practice.

- **Menopausal / 40-60 women.** This is the core of the "weight loss" story ads. Their wound is betrayal: a husband cheating with a younger woman, "menopause weight/belly", puffy face, jawline gone. Roko on Jul 13: menopause is "the biggest TAM ever in the US for scaling". Sub-pains that proved out or were tested: mood swings ($41 NCPA on launch, Jul 24), brain fog, low libido, energy.
- **Men 40-58, beer drinkers, "middle-aged crisis".** They were cheated on, overweight, losing respect, with kids or friends mocking them. "We're no longer framing it as alcoholics". Since late September the strongest male lane is ego/respect/identity (veteran reunion, former athlete at a wedding) and protection (daughter or son threatened). On Oct 2, men beat women on CAC.
- **Black men and Black women.** These versions repeatedly get lower CAC and a higher hit rate than the white versions (Jul 13, Sep 25, Oct 12). For Black men, a "carrying the family / not respected / stress" mechanism beats the alcohol mechanism. Black women respond to "husband hasn't touched me" and curvy/toning framing.
- **Women 55-65+.** On Jul 31, ages 65+ ran at ~$49 NCPA and 55-64 at ~$52. The "saw my ex-husband" hook was a top performer for 60+ women (Jul 27). Pains: swollen/heavy legs, keeping up with grandkids, widowhood. On Oct 2 there was a caveat that grandmothers have low online buying power.
- **Postpartum mothers.** Strong hit rate but high NCPA because the messaging is too shallow. Their language is "mom brain", not "brain fog" (Jul 31).
- **Weaker or deprioritized segments:** wine-drinking women (48 ads, ~2% hit rate, Jul 31). Chinese men, gay men, Christian women and Latino as primary avatars were not validated (Jul 31). Even so, LGBTQ ads later showed very low CAC on low spend (Sep 25, Oct 2). Spanish-language ads dominated spend in August but had higher CAC by late September.

## What the ads say

Don't copy the angle/mechanism/avatar libraries here; they're exported separately. Status per the Notion DBs (Oct 2026):
- **Angles:** **Cellulite = Proven**. Lymphatic Draining (MOF "proper way to drain"), Skin and Bloating = Testing. Everything else is "To Test", including Weight Loss. The DB is stale: the calls show weight loss is the account's main performer.
- **Problem mechanisms (UMP):** **Proven: Alcohol Waste (V733-735), Protein Waste (Top 7 LFS), Microplastics (V1059-1061).** Testing: Lymphatic Sludge, Toxic Waste, Stack1, Gut Swelling, Pregnancy Fluids, Chronic Inflammation, Capillary Leaking, Liver congestion, Constipation Blockage, FungalColony. Estrogen Decline is "To Test" even though Anton used it in V79/V80. Conflict: on the Jul 22 call the team said microplastics "doesn't resonate with our branding".
- **Solution mechanisms (UMS):** all are "Testing". The default is the four-herb **Breakdown** chain. **Inner Draining** is listed with Burdock root, which is not in the current formula. Check before using it.

**The central belief shift (Jul 22 call):** "it's not fat, it's waste your body stopped draining. You can't burn waste. You have to drain it." Anything (alcohol, weed, stress, menopause, pregnancy, aging) can be framed as clogging the lymph, which positions drainage "as the only solution". Every concept must connect back to **weight loss** (Notion Creative Strategy rule 7).

**What converts:** emotional, high-intensity stories with a specific "wound". The product enters as the fix about a third to halfway through.
- **Cheating/betrayal story → mirror moment → friend/sister/brother reframes it ("you don't have menopause weight, you have sludge") → four herbs → week-by-week timeline → payoff ("You look incredible. What did you do?" / walk past the ex) → offer.** This has been the dominant engine since July. The source was a rip of the brand Quasi/Kwasi's female cheating story.
- **Formats in order of proof:** AI **song ads** (~$8M spend, ~20% CTR, ~70% hook rate, ~15% hold rate per Jul 13), then AI **drama/movie** ads (CPA fell 120→50 in July; 60%+ hold on 6-8 min ads), then realistic AI dialogue ads (ego/protection, written with Opus 5.5, Oct 2), then UGC sale ads, then the "Barbara" deepfake authority. Barbara was ~15-20% of spend in July and was later cut back for compliance.
- **Why it works (team's read):** "the song is a catalyst, not the core: storytelling and the wound are what convert" (Jul 20). Every line has a congruent visual. "Fun"/"unhinged" ads beat standard ones (Jul 10). The ICP is "in an emotional high-intensity zone".
- **Big ideas in Anton's July set:** ex/divorce revenge glow-up (daycare, graduation, department store, church), Ozempic villain ("no needles, no prescription"), talking jeans plus Estrogen Decline, couple libido comeback, "Backed Up" bloating reframe with F-grades for detox teas and water pills, morning lymph flush, aging mom, the Brazil origin story ("Brazilian women have known this forever"), and the Nordic/Oslo story.
- **Proven UGC scripts (inferred from reuse):** "I need to publicly apologize… because I lied" (the "lie" is that the discount is bigger). It was localised to Spanish and reused at least four times; a "come clean" founder-style ad hit $41 NCPA and 1.75 new-customer ROAS (Sep 7-8, unconfirmed whether it's the same ad). Also: "Do NOT buy Lymphoria" reverse psychology, yes/no glasses Q&A, "don't buy on Amazon — fakes", "what happens if you take it for 4 weeks", doctor-patient skit, founder story/FAQ, 15-20s "last call" sale ads.

## Voice and rules

**Tone:** dramatic, emotional, entertaining, culturally specific (slang and names matched to the avatar), conversational UGC ("like tipping off a friend"). Leadership prefers "fun" or "unhinged" over scary. Fear-mongering, "purple legs" or horror tonality "just doesn't work for us" (Jul 13, Jul 22). AI creative (songs, characters, voices) is explicitly welcome (Notion Creative Strategy).

**Compliance rules (from the Jul 22 call, Oct 2 call, Jul 24 call and AppLovin SOP):**
- No disease or organ cure claims: avoid liver, kidney, dialysis, heart, thyroid medication, "stop taking your medicine". Lymphoria removed itself from "health related problems like kidney liver fat liver" after Rosabella was sued (Jul 22). Weight loss and appearance (de-puff, jawline, bloat, "feel lighter") is treated as the lower-risk lane.
- Naming conditions such as arthritis or autoimmune disease is a "compliance gray zone". Holistic-journey messaging is OK for MOF if no disease is named (Oct 2). Avoid PCOS fertility claims (Jul 24). Stay away from lipedema, edema and lymphedema (Sep 25).
- **Deepfakes:** reduced volume (max 2 per person, Jul 20), no deepfakes of famous people, always label THDF, and launch only in the correct ad account. A Google account was banned after an unchecked deepfake.
- Pre-landers are product pages only: "no advertorials anymore, no listicles" (Jul 22).
- Don't name real artists in song briefs; describe the vocal style (Jul 24). Don't use recognisable copyrighted characters; mimic the vibe, palette and behaviour (Oct 7).
- Sensitive protection/threat-to-daughter concepts need Milad's approval before launch (Sep 25). Violence has not triggered Meta bans so far (Oct 2).
- Ozempic angle: aim it at women who can't or won't take GLP-1s. Never say "quit your medication" (Master Idea Sheet Brief 8).
- Elderly swelling: keep it mild and end-of-day. Add the on-screen line "If swelling is sudden, one-sided, or painful, see a doctor" (Brief 11).
- Medication question script: "we always recommend checking with your doctor or pharmacist first" (U978-980).
- **AppLovin only (much stricter):** no bodily fluids, waste or organ visuals (sludge, foamy pee, "protein waste"); no second-person "you/your" health statements; no before/after or zoomed "problem area" shots; say "botanical/plant-based", never "herb"; no profanity; no cure/treat/prevent/safe; no unnamed studies or "#1" claims; ad and landing page must match on price and promo; FDA disclaimer required.
- **Anton's own July scripts** carried [CHECK] tags where source claims ("clinically studied", "2.8 million women") had no Lymphoria proof and were swapped out. Keep doing this.

**Brand do's/don'ts in briefs:** confirm the live offer and guarantee window against the landing page before export (Master Idea Sheet). Use real UGC b-roll, not AI product b-roll (Jul 31). Show the under-tongue take; it "is our absorption claim, must-have".
