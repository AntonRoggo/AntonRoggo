# The Tallowed Truth

Canadian, family-owned beef-tallow skincare brand (Shopify site thetallowedtruth.com, plus Amazon and TikTok Shop). Founder and on-camera face: **Ben Corkery** ("Ben"). Production is in-house at their own facility in Ontario (Peterborough shoot planned; warehouse in Toronto), with a team of about 20 hired locally per the founder-story drafts. Media buying, CRO and the creator programme are run with **Digital Ride** (Andre, Brian, Chris/Cris, Alex); Anton writes and briefs the Meta (and YouTube) ads, coaches creators and runs the editors (Michal, Miki, Mubashir). Hero SKU is **F-Balm** (the calls' transcripts spell it "F-Bomb"). The business's north star is 250,000 orders a year (Sep 9 monthly call: "at 52%... slightly behind pace").

## What they sell

- **F-Balm (hero, nearly all ad spend).** A whipped face balm with "five ingredients you could eat": grass-fed, grass-finished Canadian beef tallow ("triple-purified", "never bleached or deodorized"), raw Ontario honey, Canadian beeswax, organic / cold-pressed olive oil, and frankincense. Positioned as "one jar that replaces everything else": face, hands, eyes, neck, dry patches.
  - Variants: frankincense ("helps with finer lines") and unscented; **orange vanilla** and **lavender** launched ~Aug 20–22, 2026 as permanent SKUs with "select your scent" on the PDP (sales "weaker than expected", Sep 9 call). Sandalwood was dropped (fragrance cost 25x).
  - Use: "a pea-sized amount on damp skin"; "a tin lasts the average customer 3 months" (Tin Looks Too Small brief). "Feel effects within 10 minutes" (Content Grid).
  - Price: **$44** in the eczema brief CTA; the multi-scent page was proposed at **$44.99, buy 2 save 20%, buy 3 save 30%** (Jul 30 call). Treat the live price as unconfirmed.
- **Offer ladder (changes often; check before quoting).**
  - Buy 2 Get 1 Free (Content Grid "Selling Points", V55 brief; also described as protection against Amazon resellers).
  - Multi-unit discount "up to 40% off" (Sep 8) / "up to 40–55% off" (Aug 31); briefs told to end on "bundle and save 30%+".
  - BOGO / "we made too much" US-only clearance ran early Aug (12,000 US units expiring Feb 2027).
  - Subscribe & save 15% (20% considered), 30/60/90-day cadence, free Freedom Field Balm on the front-end subscription offer (Aug 27). 1,000 subscribers reached by Aug 5.
  - Rule from the Aug 18 call: mention the bundle deal but avoid specific offer numbers while the offer page is being A/B tested.
- **Guarantee:** "30-day money-back guarantee... If you don't love it, you don't pay." A "60-day guarantee" was said once on the Oct 8 brainstorm and an "empty-jar guarantee" appears in one brief's overlays; both unconfirmed (the Moisturizer Dog brief says confirm before using).
- **Proof numbers in use:** "Over 10,000 five-star reviews" (most briefs), "over 12,000" (V300-302 and the 69-and-looks-50 winner), 27,565 Loox reviews scraped Jun 2023–Jul 2026, and Judge.me reviews on site. Sold "hundreds of thousands of tins" (founder story draft). Pick one approved figure before launch.
- **Other products:** Freedom Field Balm (tallow tick and bug balm with essential oils; price test at +$5 steps; "Field Balm sunset messaging" planned Aug/Sep), F-Balm Whipped Body Butter 100 ml / 3.3 oz (the Notion product list calls it "Vanilla - Body Butter"), soap (lavender, citrus, unscented; drops moving to quarterly), S-Balm (returning next summer), winter-scent face whip (December). Pipeline: lip balm in a tube (before year-end), hair repair product, liver gummies (Jan 2027), tinted sunscreen with real SPF, lotion, deodorant, shampoo.
- **Where sold / shipping:** thetallowedtruth.com (Shopify; listicle, quiz and offer pages A/B tested in Convert), Amazon (Transparency program lifted buy-box from ~60% to 92%), TikTok Shop US (3,000 units in Fulfilled by TikTok; account banned in Aug and restarting). Markets: Canada, US, a small UK segment. Carrier cost ~$8.44/order (target ~$7). Free-shipping threshold: unknown.

## Who buys

- **Women 50+, mostly 55–70+.** V1-4's hypothesis: "97% women age 50+ bought". Zigpoll survey (Sep 16 call): 90% women, 69% aged 55+ (probably over-represented), 45% returning customers, and 64% knew the brand 3+ months before buying. The smoker's-lines winner spent mostly on women 65+, then 54–64.
- **Main pains in their own words:** dry, "papery" / "crepey" / "tissue-paper" skin after menopause; fine lines, "smoker's lines", "puppet mouth", under-eyes, crepey neck; "reactive, blotchy, and dry"; feeling invisible; not wanting "that weird look" from Botox. Objections: smell ("smells like hamburger grease"), greasiness ("greased pig"), "is it worth it", price ("Love it but I wish it was... less expensive").
- **Values-aligned "Freedom Shopper".** Reviews like "I like supporting non-woke companies" and "an incredible freedom-fighting company". The brand runs a Freedom Sale and a Freedom Convoy Sale.
- **Smaller pockets:** men (about $12K of $100K spend; "meat and potatoes skincare" tests), eczema / psoriasis families (strong reviews but inefficient ads so far), outdoors people and hunters (Field Balm), cracked working hands.
- Detailed avatars are exported separately to `avatars/`. Tracker avatars used most: Woman 40+, Women 60+, Women ALL, Menopause Women, Woman35+, Freedom Shopper.

## What the ads say

Angles and mechanisms are exported separately; these are the ones that come up most in briefs and calls:

- **Problem mechanism: "Age-Related Sebum Decline (Hormone Loss)".** After menopause "your skin loses the ability to produce enough of its own sebum". This is the most-used UMP in the tracker.
- **Solution mechanism: "Tallow is almost bio-identical to skin"** ("Your skin can barely tell the difference between this and itself"), plus the honey/tallow **"draw-and-seal"** pair (raw honey = "nature's hyaluronic acid" pulls moisture in, tallow and beeswax seal it).
- **Water-based moisturizers are 70–80% water.** These need preservatives and emulsifiers, "sit on top", "evaporate in twenty minutes". The 1%-ingredient-rule and "too diluted to work" lines support this.
- **"Not all tallow is equal" / industry exposé.** Cheap tallow is "sourced from China", "bleached and deodorized", made from scrap fat with meat bits (that's why it smells), or comes out of "the exact same factory in LA". F-Balm is "triple-purified", "grass-fed, grass-finished", from "Canadian farmers ten minutes from our facility", "made in-house, small batch". On Oct 13 the team said triple-purified must be turned into a benefit: "no smell, full nutrients".
- **Anti-Botox / age naturally:** "not plastic, not frozen", "never going near a needle".
- **One balm replaces the shelf:** "meat and potatoes skincare", "more products ≠ better skin".
- **Trust / factory / founder.** No other tallow brand can show its own factory. Founder BTS ("Come Clean", packing orders) and "made in Canada, not China" have the highest hit rate.
- **Strongest so far (per calls):** emotional, story-led hooks over pure mechanism. Examples: smoker's lines (Melissa), carded at the Mandarin at 73 (JP), esthetician shocked by her skin (Pat), friend's mom who hasn't aged, neighbour / "69 and looks 50", the plastic surgeon's wife. Then a short "why this tallow is different" mechanism and a guarantee close. The Aug 11 call: "the market is now aware of beef tallow basics; differentiation matters more than explanation".

## Voice and rules

- **Tone:** founder-led, casual, raw, lightly funny ("All jokes aside, people are loving this stuff"). Talk "like you're chatting with a friend". Defiant and anti-big-industry ("The F-Balm is for people who don't trust big corporations with their health"). Sincere at the close.
- **Words they use:** "five ingredients you could eat", "triple-purified", "grass-fed, grass-finished", "never bleached, never deodorized", "made in-house, small batch", "Canadian farmers ten minutes from our facility", "nature's hyaluronic acid", "one jar", "If you don't love it, you don't pay", "If you still see the link below, it's still in stock".
- **Values:** draft founder scripts say "We have Christian values and we don't hide them", and a hate-comments ad ("believing there's 2 genders...") reportedly drove hundreds of sales a day. A Granola search found no note approving "Christian"/"non-woke" as standing paid-ad language, so confirm with Ben before using it (unconfirmed).
- **Claims, not allowed or to soften:**
  - No fake before/afters. Don't say F-Balm "fixes" smoker's lines; real customer screenshots and explanatory visuals are fine (Oct 8).
  - Eczema/psoriasis results are **customer voice only + "Results vary"**; the brand promise stays cosmetic ("soothes / calms / supports barrier"). Eczema landers needed a Health Canada compliance cleanup (Jul 23). NHP registration is a long-term goal.
  - No SPF / sun-protection claims (current products).
  - "Eliminates" wrinkles was softened to "smooths"; scripts carry `[CHECK]` on fine-line claims.
  - Say "you're not getting the results you could be getting", not "it's destroying your barrier" (Sep 29).
  - Bill Gates / "engineered ticks" Field Balm conspiracy content = "do NOT run on paid", organic only.
  - Don't copy misleading celebrity claims (the "70-year-old olive oil" one).
  - Don't name or show competitor brands/logos as proof. Blur competitor labels. Label generic stock "Illustrative footage". Never use AI-generated "evidence". Day counts in testimonials must be the creator's real number. Dr. Eric Berg can be quoted as a reference, not shown as an endorsement.
  - "Made in Canada" is the claim. One October brief ("Smelly or Greasy") says "made right here in the USA" and "essential oils" instead of frankincense; treat that as an error until confirmed.
- **Creator brief conventions (house template, see skill `tt-brief-builder`):** deadline "5 days after receiving the brief"; product link; "1 Script, 3 Hooks"; shoot 9:16, HDR off; film script and B-roll as separate files; watch the reference video; "Make sure your skin is glowing beautifully"; no need to read word-for-word. Table: Scene | Script (verbatim) | What to film. 3 hooks + one shared body; export names like `TTT - <Concept> - F-Balm - V1`.
- **Editor conventions:** Proxima Nova Semi Bold. Always upload with captions (older audience watches muted). Keep captions short and don't let B-roll cover them. Put the strongest proof (e.g. grandma photo) in the first seconds. Use review pop-ups above frame. Edit review in Frame.io. Notion naming: `[TTT] - V# / Concept / W# / Avatar / Angle / UMP / UMS / Product / Awareness / Type / Level / Ad Type / Author / Editor / VID / TTT`.
- **Ben's style:** riffs best off a prompt (a comment or review) rather than a script. Scripted founder content can come across "unenthusiastic". For briefs he prefers a scripted read-through (laptop out of frame) that the editor cuts.
