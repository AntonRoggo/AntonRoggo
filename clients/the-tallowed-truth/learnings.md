# The Tallowed Truth — learnings

No Creatives Tracker row has Results or Learnings filled in yet (checked 2026-10-13), so everything below comes from call notes and research docs.

- [2026-10-13, F-Bomb ad performance review] Hook rates are strong across the board (some 70–78%+), so hooks aren't the problem. The weak point is the bridge from story to product. Mechanisms are explained too vaguely ("for the polyphenols and stuff"), features are named without benefits ("triple purified" should become "no smell, full nutrients"), and smell, greasiness and skepticism go unanswered.
- [2026-10-13, F-Bomb ad performance review] The same story can win for several creators: esthetician-shocked-by-skin worked for both Pat and JP. "Carded at the Mandarin at 73" did 4–6 sales/day for JP over three days. Don't avoid a story because someone else already ran it.
- [2026-10-13, F-Bomb ad performance review] Stories with no skincare lead-in pull a high new-visitor % (top-of-funnel potential) if the body/sell is improved. Pat found that scripting the body, not the story, makes videos shorter and tighter.
- [2026-10-08, Creative Brainstorm] Melissa's smoker's-lines ad hit ~$377/day and $1.4K CAD in 7 days, and its budget was doubled. Iterate the hook/concept but keep her product explanation and mechanism intact.
- [2026-10-08, Creative Brainstorm] The ads that scaled biggest had a strong emotional component, not just utility. The "angry customer email" ad drove hundreds of sales/day with almost no product info. Suggested split: ~90% effort on proven iterations, ~10% on bigger emotional swings.
- [2026-10-08, Creative Brainstorm] No fake before/afters, and don't claim F-Balm "fixes" smoker's lines. Real customer comment screenshots and diagrams of what smoker's lines are are fine.
- [2026-10-07, Morning Brief citing Oct 6 Granola notes] The broad "I owe you an apology" structure is "outperforming the negatives". Next iterations planned off Melissa's winner: straw-drinking and menopause-collagen. (Second-hand; unconfirmed.)
- [2026-09-29, F-Bomb ad creative review] Engagement without conversion means the story is good but the bridge to action is missing. John's fragrance ad (56% hook, 16% hold, 2.6% CTR) and Andrew's bar story (56% hook, 5% CTR, 3 add-to-carts, 0 purchases) both needed a natural product intro and a CTA with the money-back guarantee.
- [2026-09-29, F-Bomb ad creative review] Two competing mechanisms in one script (Chantelle: "you're paying for water" plus lipid-barrier damage) weakened the close. Also, people who moisturize feel moisturized, so "it's destroying your barrier" lands weakly; reframe as "you're not getting the results you could be getting".
- [2026-09-29, F-Bomb ad creative review] Andrew's young-male creator skewed delivery to young men while buyers are older women. Young creators need a bridge such as "my mom / mother-in-law uses this".
- [2026-09-29, F-Bomb ad creative review] Speed of iteration is the unlock. Pat's Mandarin story held ~$400–500/day through rapid variations; sitting on a promising concept for two weeks resets momentum.
- [2026-09-22, Ad creative review] One big idea per ad: single-story ads beat multi-theme ones. Put the strongest proof (the 97-year-old grandma photo) in the first seconds, not ~10s in. Always upload with captions; the older audience watches muted.
- [2026-09-22, Ad creative review] Nikki's mom ad (57% hook, 20% hold, 2.5% CTR) opened at ~$40 NCPA for two days, finished at $90 NCPA over 5 new-customer purchases, and was duplicated into scale. Its natural story-to-product bridge is the model for new creators.
- [2026-09-16, UGC strategy review] Menopause targeting wasn't gaining traction, so the team is leaning to 50–60+ targeting across all ads. Brooklyn's ad had a high new-visitor % but ~$90 NCPA, likely a quiz-funnel mismatch with the ad.
- [2026-09-16, UGC strategy review] Zigpoll: 64% of buyers knew the brand 3+ months before purchasing, which supports running top, middle and bottom-funnel ads. 90% women, 69% aged 55+ (probably over-represented).
- [2026-09-15, Weekly Ad Creative Call] The smoker's-lines hook came from a customer comment: 54% hook, 25% hold, 4% CTR, 17s average watch, spend mostly on women 65+, then 54–64. Mine comments for specific customer terms (crow's feet, laugh lines, turkey neck) and build hooks from them.
- [2026-09-15, Weekly Ad Creative Call] The scent-objection ad (orange vanilla / lavender) got ~50% hook, 30% hold, 9% CTR. It's a valuable middle-funnel converter, not a top-of-funnel scaler.
- [2026-09-15, Weekly Ad Creative Call] The educational eczema explainer (32% hook, 12% hold, 3% CTR, ~$80–90 NCPA) missed targets. Next try is a real first-person customer story plus a dedicated eczema landing page; the audience may be small.
- [2026-09-15, Weekly Ad Creative Call] Don't hard-cut from a funny story to the product (Evan's mom ad: 60% hook, 5% CTR, abrupt pivot). Let a third person introduce the product inside the story. A scaled winner means 10–30 sales/day, not 1–2 testing sales.
- [2026-09-15, TTT Yapper Research summary] Only 1 of 15 organic winners on the "someone noticed you look better" theme was a studio talking head; skits and POV vlogs carry it organically. On paid, every relationship / social-proof ad found was a yapper or VSL-style testimonial (29–125s). Use skit/POV for organic, yapper testimonial for paid.
- [2026-09-10, The Tallowed Truth DR Call] "Weird mouth wrinkles" showed early signal: $78 spend, 5x first-click ROAS, 6 conversions in one day. Make hook and green-screen variants. Skepticism is the top concern in post-purchase surveys.
- [2026-09-08, Brian 1:1] Factory content has a high hit rate. Both factory ads performed, and "Come clean about the formula" (off-the-cuff with Ben) moved into scale. No other tallow brand can show its factory. "Made in Canada, not China" was "crushing" mid-funnel. Men are only ~$12K of $100K spend, a possible unlock.
- [2026-08-31, Brian Catch Up] Pat's side-by-side "ours vs China" sourcing ad tested well but got no spend in the main scale campaign. Keep the unique mechanism to one or two sentences: triple-purified, not bleached, made in Canada.
- [2026-08-31, Brian Catch Up] For the apology format, don't lead with bleaching; let the trust elements (purity, Canadian, in-house) do the work. A listicle previously lifted revenue per user ~25–30%, and an offer page added ~20%.
- [2026-08-27, DR Weekly Call] The scent-launch sale drove $20–25K/day, almost all from returning customers. Triple Pixel ROAS was 2.3–5x but new-customer ROAS only 0.3–0.68, and raising spend to $1.5K/day cut profit to ~$700/day. The creative was too bottom-funnel; new-customer acquisition needs stronger top-funnel ads.
- [2026-08-20, Growth Call] The best email-only launch had sold ~2K units in 24h. Launch creative should be short and scarcity-led ("limited number") in the F-Balm tone.
- [2026-08-18, Weekly Coaching Creator Call] Smell is the biggest recurring complaint, even among happy customers, so it works as the primary hook for scent launches. Mention bundles but not specific offer numbers while the offer page is A/B tested; real first-batch scarcity is fine.
- [2026-08-13, Growth Call] The scripted MHI founder videos made Ben come across as "unenthusiastic". Founder content works best when Ben riffs on a prompt he cares about.
- [2026-08-11, Aug 11 creator call] Emotional hooks are beating mechanism-only ads at reaching new audiences. The market knows tallow basics, so answer "why is this tallow different?" (e.g. yellow vs bleached white). Brooklyn's eczema ad didn't take off; Pat's product-aware comparison got $0.03 spend on day one.
- [2026-08-06, Growth Call] The BOGO offer did 28 purchases at 10x ROAS and 1.85 new-customer ROAS. July–August seasonality suppresses new-customer ROAS every year, so forecast off a lower base.
- [2026-07-30, July 30 Growth Call] No real F-Balm unlocks since early June; ~25 concepts tested with few winners. Main performing creators were Ben, Pat and JP. Target front-end CPA is ~$55 (maybe ~$70 if post-purchase LTV improves). The Field Balm listicle lander got CPA down to $35.
- [2026-07-24, Growth Call] The Field Balm lander test should have been seeded only with angle-matched ads, not all winners; the algorithm didn't self-select well. The US campaign's early CPAs ran $50–80+ before bid-cap changes.
- [2026-07-08, 1:1 with Ben] Ben's founder ads are prompt-driven, not script-driven: he riffs off a customer comment or review into differentiators and results. "Papery skin" is a high-frequency word in reviews worth using in scripts.
