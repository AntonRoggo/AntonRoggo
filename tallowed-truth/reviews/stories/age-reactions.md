# Third-party age-reaction stories

43 stories (reviews and Facebook comments) where someone else reacted to a customer's skin or age, or a professional noticed. Built for F-Balm ads; Google Doc copy: TTT BRAIN > 02 Story Ideas > "Third-party age-reaction stories (F-Balm)". Screenshots: TTT BRAIN > 02 Story Ideas > Age-reaction screenshots (rebuild them with the commands at the bottom).

**Why this doc:** Third-party age-reaction stories carried the week. 15 of 16 winners open on the payoff: who noticed, what they said, or her age. So every story below opens on the payoff in the first line, never on the setup.
**How to use it:** 1) Pick a story. 2) Open with Hook 1 (who noticed / what they said) or Hook 2 (her age), word for word or close to it. 3) Tell the rest of her story in the creator's own words, then the product. 4) The editor pops the screenshot on screen the moment the payoff is said (tt-ad-edit pop-up: centred on the caption zone, 0.3 s fade in, captions off while it's up, 2-8 s). Long screenshots also have a short card that starts at the payoff; use the short one on screen.
**Rules:** Quote the review exactly; never change her words or her age. The screenshot must be the same person and the same words being talked about. No medical claims (heals, cures, treats). Say what someone said about her skin, not what the balm 'does'.
**Screenshots:** Every file is numbered to match the story number (e.g. '01 - Judy-B - r-d50f79e6.png'). Real Judge.me screenshots are used where we have them; the rest are review cards drawn in the same Judge.me style from the review word for word. Real Judge.me screenshots come from the Dropbox `Testimonial Text` folder (see `testimonials/screenshots.csv`). Every other card is drawn with:

```
python3 tallowed-truth/tools/review_card.py <review id> --name --out output/age-reactions/screenshots
python3 tallowed-truth/tools/review_card.py "<review id>@<first words of the payoff sentence>" --name --max-chars 300 --out ...   # short card that starts at the payoff
python3 tallowed-truth/tools/review_card.py --comment "<comment text>" --out <file>.png                                          # Facebook comment
```
