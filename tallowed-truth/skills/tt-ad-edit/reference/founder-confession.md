## Structure

| Part | Time | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0-3 s | "Come clean" + the banner "OFFICIAL STATEMENT from The Tallowed Truth Founder". Feels like news, not an ad. |
| Proof flash | 3-5 s | Two real customers on screen while he says "gives people glowing and plump feeling skin". |
| Open loop | 5-9 s | "Give me 60 seconds, I'll show you what we actually put inside this." |
| Ingredient tour | 9-40 s | One ingredient per stop, each held or shown while named: tallow, honey, beeswax, olive oil, essential oils. Each comes with a benefit and a "not" (not bleached, not deodorized, not greasy, not synthetic). |
| Quality claim | 40-46 s | "It's all natural ingredients, but it's the quality." |
| Social proof | 46-54 s | Scrolling real reviews while he reads what they say. |
| Offer | 54-61 s | Who it's for (tried cheap tallow / a serum), "Premium ingredients, premium results." |
| Risk reversal + CTA | 61-64 s | Money back, "click the link below", points down. |

## Measurements

- **Speech:** 248 words in 63.8 s (3.9 words a second). First word on frame 1, last word ends 0.2 s before the end.
  Pauses between words mostly under 0.25 s; up to 0.5 s only while the review pop-up is on (he's listing).
- **Captions:** Proxima Nova Semibold, x-height 23 px at 720 wide, which is about 70 px font at 1080. White,
  lowercase, apostrophes kept ("i'll", "you've", "doesn't"), no other punctuation. Black outline about 4 px at
  1080 plus a soft black drop shadow down-right. Usually 2 words (1 for a long word like "antimicrobial", rarely 3).
  Centred, baseline at 68% of the height (y 1305 of 1920). Never moves.
- **Captions disappear while a pop-up is on screen.** The pop-up sits exactly where the captions are.
- **Banner** (0-3.07 s): flat white box, square corners, x 123-954 (77% wide), y 1107-1232, tight padding (~12 px).
  Line 1 "OFFICIAL STATEMENT": black uppercase, extra-bold, cap height about 46 px (about 70 px font).
  Line 2 "from The Tallowed Truth Founder": black bold, about 50 px font. Captions run just under the box.
- **Pop-up 1** (3.07-5.17 s): two webcam-style customer clips side by side (each about 35% wide, thin dark border),
  centred at the caption height. Fades in over about 0.3 s with a slight blur, leaves on the next hard cut.
- **Pop-up 2** (45.93-53.73 s): screen recording of the Judge.me reviews page scrolling slowly, white panel about
  80% wide, centred at the caption height. Fades in over about 0.4 s starting on the cut, leaves on the next cut.
  It includes a 3-star review ("Feels great but I'm having a hard time getting used to the smell"), which makes
  it believable.
- **Audio:** -14.4 LUFS integrated, peaks -0.7 dBFS. Voice and room tone only; no music bed found under the pauses
  (to confirm).
- **Product:** the F-Balm tin is in his hand in nearly every shot; held up to the lens for the offer.
