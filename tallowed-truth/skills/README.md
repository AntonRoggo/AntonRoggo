# Skills

Skills for the company Claude project. Each folder is one skill: zip the folder and upload it in the
company Claude settings (Capabilities, Skills), or keep it in the repo for Claude Code.

| Skill | What it does | Version |
|---|---|---|
| `tt-ad-edit/` | Edit a talking-head ad in the house style of the founder confession (cuts, captions, banner, pop-ups, audio), with a checklist. `reference/` has the shot-by-shot breakdown of that ad. | v1, 2026-10-29 |

## To make tt-ad-edit better (questions for Anton)

1. **The raw footage of the founder confession.** Then the skill can learn what gets cut (how many takes, how
   long the pauses were, what got dropped), not just what's left.
2. **The editor's project file or presets** (Premiere / CapCut / Mubashir's setup): the exact caption shadow,
   banner fonts and sizes, pop-up animation.
3. **Is there music under it?** The analysis found voice and room tone only. Confirm, or name the track.
4. **3-5 more winners to compare**, ideally creator ads: "Esthetician shocked again", "Carded at 73",
   "Stop buying tallow", "$900 French creams", a static before/after. The rules that hold across all of them
   go into v2; the founder-only ones get marked as such.
5. **The source of the customer clips at 3 s** (the two women on webcam). Are those from customer calls, video
   reviews, or something else? Where are more of them?
6. **What you'd change.** Anything in this ad you'd do differently next time, so it doesn't become a rule.
7. **Brand assets:** Proxima Nova font files (Semibold, Bold, Extrabold), the TTT logo and a cut-out PNG of the
   F-Balm tin.
