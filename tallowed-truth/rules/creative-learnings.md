# Creative learnings

What we've learned from testing TTT ads. Each point says where it came from. Add to this after every
weekly "what's working" call: one short, general rule per learning, not a note about one ad.

## How to test

- **One change per ad.** Keep the words of the script the same and change only one thing: delivery,
  order, setting, prop or who says it. Then the result can be read. (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23)
- **Delivery, not message.** Don't rewrite a review to make it spicier; make the delivery better.
  (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23)

## What works

- **Props win.** Any ad with a real prop beats the same ad without one, e.g. holding a real photo of
  the grandmother being talked about. A real photo is proof, so use it. (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23)
- **Show the product early.** In a review-based ad there's no point hiding the tin; lead with it.
  Test: grandma's photo on the left, the tin on the right, in the first frame. (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23)
- **Ordinary stories are fine.** These stories aren't dramatic. Win on delivery. (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23)
- **Lead with the strangest true detail.** E.g. "My grandma put beef fat on her face every night."
  as the hook. (Ads Arcadia call, 2026-10-23, test 4)
- **Pop the proof in the first 2 seconds.** The person or photo the hook is about pops up on screen,
  with burned-in captions. (F-Balm briefs, editor note)

## Filming rules every brief repeats

- Screen-free filming: creators never film phones, tablets or computer screens. Editors add reviews,
  screenshots, messages, photos and websites as pop-ups in the edit.
- Film the script and the B-roll as separate files. 9:16, HDR off, clean lens, room around you.
- Super casual and conversational, like talking to a friend. Not word for word; same message.
