# Text-on-screen (TOS) hook rules

From Anton, 2026-10-30: the pattern analysis of 41 top TTT video ad hooks ($1,891,400 CAD tracked spend, CPA
$38-89). Every edit's opening text box follows these rules. It also references copy-rules.md, story-ad-logic.md
and israel-copy-pass.md, which aren't in this brain yet.

## Short reads fast

TOS hooks work at scanning speed: read in one glance, before the thumb swipes.

- 88.6% of spend came from hooks of 8 words or fewer. Every hook over $100K spend is 3-7 words.
- The top 3 ("Attractive 50 year old neighbor part 2:", "Weird Botox hack:", "Got approached by a skeptic") are
  57% of all spend and average 5 words, 28 characters.
- A 5-word hook reads in about 1 s; viewers decide to swipe within 1.5 s.

## The open loop

Hook = **grounding anchor** (1-3 words of concrete context: a person, an age, a place, Botox) + **friction**
(1-4 words: an anomaly, an unexpected reaction, an open question) + **a cliffhanger** (punctuation that leaves
the thought unfinished).

| Punctuation | Ads | Spend | Share |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colon `:` | 12 | $1,144,300 | 60.5% |
| Quotation marks | 7 | $148,100 | 7.8% |
| Question mark `?` / `??` | 4 | $130,000 | 6.9% |
| Comment bubble | 2 | $72,100 | 3.8% |

**Text and voice don't say the same thing.** The voice is casual and slow ("So I was walking out of the
grocery store and this woman..."); the text is the headline ("Got approached by a 60 year old woman at the
grocery store:").

## The rules

The hook text never covers his mouth: above his head, or below his chin.


1. **3-7 words, under 35 characters.** Never over 8 unless it's a comment bubble. If it wraps to 3 lines, cut it.
2. **One piece of context, zero payoff.** The viewer knows what it's about and has no idea how it turned out.
3. ~~End a story, encounter or hack hook with a colon.~~ **Anton, Oct 30 2026: no colon or other punctuation
   on the on-screen hook.** Keep the open loop in the words instead ("Esthetician shocked by 64 year old's skin").
4. **A third party, never self-praise:** a neighbor, a skeptic, a stranger, an esthetician, a plastic surgeon,
   a customer review.
5. **Exact numbers:** "64 years old", "19 year difference", "Carded in her 70's". Never "look younger" or "look
   decades younger".
6. **Pit the balm against something expensive or clinical:** Botox, plastic surgery, smoker's lines, the drugstore
   cream.
7. **No mechanism in the hook:** never "triple purified", "grass-fed", "fatty acids" in the text.
8. **Look native, not polished:** comment-reply stickers, raw text in quotes, handwritten signs.

## Templates

- Encounter: `Got approached by a [age] year old at [place]:` · `[Authority] shocked by [age] year old's skin:` ·
  `Attractive [age] year old [neighbor/relative] part [n]:`
- Weird hack: `Weird [procedure] hack:` · `Weird "[wrinkle]" hack:` (crepey neck, crows feet)
- Milestone: `Carded at [age] years old?` · `[n] year difference?` · `Getting "noticed" at [age]:`
- Quoted skeptic: `"Stop telling women to [behaviour]"` · `I told a [skeptic] what nobody else will:`
