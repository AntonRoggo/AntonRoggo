# What to post: the whole account, no restrictions

This is based on every reel on @antonroggo: 583, from 2022 to October 2026. I downloaded and transcribed all of them and tagged each one with a topic. On top of that:
- **Sandcastles:** views and outlier score (views ÷ your average at the time).
- **Metricool:** shares, saves and 3-second hold for the 302 reels since June 2025, plus your TikTok and YouTube.
- **The full table**, one row per reel with the topic I gave it: [`data/all_reels.csv`](data/all_reels.csv)

The question was: with no limit on money, travel, time or format, what should you post?

## The answer

Post about one reel a day, built on four engines:

1. **A month in an underrated country, told through its food and everyday habits.** This is your best topic by a distance.
2. **Phone and attention.** Your most consistent topic and your most shared.
3. **One season-long experiment at a time.** "As close to homeless as possible this summer" hit twice in a row.
4. **The "do this" go-outside line, every week.** It's where most of your viral reels come from.

Fill the gaps with text-on-clip posts. Keep oysters as your signature inside all of it, not as the topic.

**Stop:**
- Indoor philosophy talks
- Fitness
- Nutrition explainers
- Travel vlogs that aren't about the local food and habits
- Trial Reel batches

## 1. The country month (your flagship)

**Serbia, Feb–Mar 2025:** 25 posts, median **82,259 views**, median **6.5×**, 14 of 25 over 5×, including your 12.6M reel. That's the best run on the account.

**Brazil, Aug 2025 – Mar 2026:** 17 posts, median 25,941, 0.9×, **none** over 5×.

The difference is the angle:
- **Serbia was about the country's food and everyday norms, seen through your values** (raw dairy, markets, prices, outdoor gyms, long lunches), and you praised them. People from Serbia and the diaspora share that.

## 2. Phone and attention, made bigger

It's your steadiest topic (22% over 5×) and the most shared and saved (8.6 per 1K each, nearly 3× the travel posts). In 2026 it has the highest median views of any topic with 10+ posts (52.7K). It also has the 18.6M "Let's all ruin our next day…".

**What works in it:** not anti-phone lectures. It's "do this instead", where the instead is cheap and nice: a sweet treat, a walk, pho with friends.

**Examples:**
- [868K](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DPaVsNIDRVe/): "Can we please just start grabbing a sweet treat…"
- [249K](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DWVTlDTDdWF/): "Why aren't you going for an evening walk…"
- [207K](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DLtU4GVsCNY/): "…doom-scrolling outside"

**Bigger ideas:** these are mine, untested, built on the topic's numbers.
- **30 days on a flip phone.** A 10-second daily check-in with the day count on screen. It uses the experiment mechanic from section 3.
- **A monthly phone-free oyster dinner.** Phones go in a box at the door and you film the night. Your "host an oyster dinner party" giveaway got 29.8×.
- **"Nobody's watching."** Do the slightly odd thing in public while you say it. @thedavidsun did this for 24.8M (see `series/do-this/ANALYSIS.md`).

## 3. One season-long experiment at a time

"The goal this summer is to be as close to homeless as possible" ([1.07M](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DIFO1u_P4_4/)), then a check-in three weeks later ([1.07M](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DIz3yiHClGf/)). It's the only non-travel idea that hit twice in a row. It worked because of the identity ("homeless"), the season and the updates.

**Run one per season:** announce it on day one, then check in every week or two.
- **Winter:** "As close to a retired Mediterranean grandpa as possible" (this is script 3 in `series/do-this/SCRIPTS.md`).
- **Spring:** "No phone before noon for 90 days."
- **Summer:** "Homeless-maxing, Europe edition." A sequel to your proven one.
- **Any season:** "Oyster farmer for a season." Your oyster-farmer day-in-the-life got [81K, 3.5×](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DYTdwAcNf_i/) on Instagram and 50K on TikTok.

## 4. "Do this" lines, every week

A third of what you post, and 6 of your 14 reels over 500K. The rules and 25 scripts are in [`series/do-this/`](../series/do-this/).

**Keep the ask cheap even with no budget.** All 6 ask for something cheap and close: a watermelon in the park, a swim then a café, boiling water on frozen ground, leaving the house all day. Kitesurfing only shows up as the last item in one of them. Big activities can be B-roll, but the line has to be something the viewer can do this weekend.

## 5. Text on a clip, as filler

Two of your three biggest reels ever have no talking: 12.6M and 3.2M. Post one whenever you can't film.

## Oysters: your signature, not your topic
