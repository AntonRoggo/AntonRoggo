# Footage behind the cut plans

The plans in `series/do-this/plans/` and `series/lifestyle-legends/cuts/` use short names for the camera files. Footage isn't kept in the repo. To re-cut a plan, download these DJI originals from Dropbox and link or copy them into the plan's `footage` folder under the short name.

```bash
mkdir -p input/dothis
ln -s ~/Downloads/DJI_20261029130947_0011_D.MP4 input/dothis/t0011.MP4
```
