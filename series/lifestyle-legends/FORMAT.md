# Lifestyle Legends: series format

Lifestyle Legends tells the stories of people who lived first (sea, mountains, road, adventure) and figured out the work later.

This format is built from Sandcastles' global data: the top-outlier reels across all creators where someone tells the story of a famous person, from the last six months. None of it is based on your own videos.

**Patterns across them**
- **The hero's-journey arc wins.** Every top biography runs bold risk → rejection or struggle → turning point → payoff → one human detail people don't know.
- **Ages and numbers carry the story:** "at 15", "$300", "$1.8 billion". The more specific, the more believable.
- **The series is explicit.** There's a named series line, an episode number, "follow for the next one", and a tease of the next subject.
- **Comment bait is built in.** Guessing games and "when did you figure it out?" give people a reason to comment.
- **Long is fine when the story is good.** The top reels run 26 s to 3 min.

## The series

- **Name:** Lifestyle Legends
- **Series line** (said in every episode, about 5 s in): *"This is Lifestyle Legends: the people who lived first and figured out the work later."*
- **Who qualifies:** someone who spent their early years at sea, in the mountains, on the road or in the water, and built the work out of that life later.
- **You:** the storyteller, talking to camera outdoors, with the story's photos above you. You're telling a friend a story you can't stop thinking about.

## The three formats

The shot-by-shot editing breakdown of every reference is in [BREAKDOWNS.md](BREAKDOWNS.md). Every script in [SCRIPTS.md](SCRIPTS.md) copies one winning transcript line for line. There's also a 30-second short cut of the Origin Story, built on the Favreau winner.

### A. The Origin Story (60–90 s)
The full arc above, told from the start of their life to the payoff. Uses the split screen throughout, with photos on top and you on the bottom. Go full screen for the series line and the moral.
References: Let's Talk About It, Favreau, Lens on Legends, Alan Bond.

### B. Guess the Legend (45–60 s)
The same arc told as clues, one beat per age ("Age 15: buys a boat…"), with the name withheld and faces pixelated. The last clue gives it away, then the reveal ("It's Jack London."), then "Comment when you got it."
Reference: the guess-the-footballer reel. It's the cheapest format to film (one take) and the best one for comments.

### C. In Their Footsteps (60–90 s)
You go and do what they did: the morning swim, the oyster dock, the surf-first workday. You tell their story while you live it, with archive photos cut in. End with what you learned from doing it.
References: Stevenson Trail (11% engagement), Hever Castle, Harry DuBois. This is where your outdoor life is an advantage nobody else in the niche has.

## Hook bank

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| At [age], [legend] [did bold thing]. | "At fifteen, Jack London borrowed three hundred dollars and became a pirate." |
| [Legend]. [What they're known for]. By [age], [surprising failure]. | "Ian Fleming. The man who invented James Bond. By forty, he'd failed at almost everything." |
| In [year], [unknown person] [small act]. [N] years later, [huge result]. | "In 1957, a broke eighteen-year-old bought a used forge from a junkyard. Sixty-five years later, he gave away a three-billion-dollar company." |
| In [year], this was the [superlative] [thing] in [place]. | "In 1903, this was the book everyone in America was reading. Twelve years earlier, its author was stealing oysters." |
| Can you guess this legend from the story of his life? | (Format B, every time) |
| I'm [doing X] because of [legend]. | "I'm shucking oysters on a dock at midnight because of a writer who used to steal them." |

## Schedule

- **Weekly rhythm:** Origin Story on Monday, Guess the Legend on Thursday (same person, so you research once and post twice), and In Their Footsteps whenever the location works.
- **Episode numbers** go in the header strip (`Ep 01`, `Ep 02`…), as in the "Part 187" series.
- **The newsletter** gets the long version of each Origin Story, plus the Community Legend of the week.
- **The line-up:** Jacques Cousteau (Ep 01), Yvon Chouinard (Ep 02), Ernest Hemingway (Ep 03). See [SCRIPTS.md](SCRIPTS.md).
- **The bench:** Robert Louis Stevenson, Jack London, Ian Fleming, Anthony Bourdain, then Duke Kahanamoku, John Muir, Patrick Leigh Fermor, Peter Mayle and Jimmy Buffett.
