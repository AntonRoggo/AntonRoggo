# "Do This" reels: what wins, what doesn't

This covers your quick format: you say one line to camera ("you need to…", "nothing is stopping you from…"), then the activity starts. It's the fastest thing you can make, and it's behind most of your biggest reels.

**Where the data comes from**
- **Sandcastles:** all 583 reels on @antonroggo, with views and outlier score (views ÷ your average at the time).
- **Metricool:** saves, shares, 3-second hold and watch time for the 302 reels since June 2025.
- **Transcripts and frames:** I downloaded every reel from 2025–26 (443 of them), transcribed each one and pulled four frames from each.
- **The full table**, one row per reel with link, views, outlier, length, format and first line: [`data/reels_2025_2026.csv`](data/reels_2025_2026.csv)

## The short version

1. **This format is 80% of what you post, and your hits come from it.** Only 1 in 14 of these reels breaks 10×, though, so the format alone isn't doing it. The opening line and the shot are.
2. **Winners show you doing the thing. Losers are you talking indoors.** Your bottom quarter is mostly a lecture filmed in your room or at night with no activity at all. The irony: "If you don't love life, it's because you don't realize how much time you spend inside", filmed inside, got 0.6×.
3. **Winners open with a permission slip or a reframe that has a concrete picture in it** (a watermelon, a job, "as close to homeless as possible"). Losers open with a philosophy line ("There is no emotion without motion").
4. **Short.** Half the winners are 15 s or under. A third of the losers run past 20 s.
5. **A winning frame only works once.** Every time you reused a hit's opening frame, the copies did a fraction of the original and kept shrinking.
6. **Two of your three biggest reels ever (12.6M and 3.2M) have no talking at all.** Text on top of a clip, with a song. Even quicker to make.
7. **You stopped posting daily.** Every month of 2025 you posted almost every day, and 9 of those 12 months produced a reel over 500K. Since August 2026 you've posted 9 times.

### What's different, line by line

| | Winners | Losers |
|---|---|---|
| **First line** | A permission slip or a reframe with a picture in it: watermelon, job, homeless, Polish dream, fall | A philosophy line with no picture: emotion, complaining, curating, cringe |
| **The enemy** | Named and ordinary: your job, staying home, scrolling tonight, the season | Missing, or it's the viewer ("you're pissing me off") |
| **What you ask for** | 2–3 cheap things you could do this weekend, each one you can see: sweet treat, park, e-bike, pho | Abstract behaviour: "curate your life", "don't complain", "notice the little things" |
| **Time anchor** | "this weekend", "tonight", "this summer", "in the fall" | Timeless |
| **Tone** | Warm and in on it: "Can we please just…", "Life's more fun that way", "Who's with me?" | Scolding: "You guys are pussies", "you deserve to be depressed" |
| **Picture** | You doing it, outside, by second 3, with a cut on each noun | You in your room talking |
| **Length** | 8–15 s and stops on the button line | 18–46 s, with a story or a second point |

## A frame only works once

Every time you reused a winning frame, the copies did a fraction of the original, and each one did less than the last. Change the frame, keep the rules.

| Frame | First time | Copies after |
|---|---|---|
| "Let's all [bad habit]… Who's with me?" | **18.6M** (Sep 2025) | 51K, 62K, 58K, 43K, 42K, 36K, 19K |
| "Nothing is stopping you from…" | **1.95M** (Jun 2025) | 45K, 39K, 34K |
| "Forget the American dream. I want the ___ dream" | **625K** Polish (Jul 2025) | 52K Brazilian |
| "Why don't you have any friends that'll…" | **285K** (Jun 2025) | 122K, 116K, 73K, 34K, 29K, 26K |
| "As close to homeless as possible" | **1.07M** (Apr 2025), plus a 1.07M follow-up 3 weeks later | 75K, 55K |

**Why the "Let's all" copies flopped:** the 18.6M one described exactly what the viewer was doing when they saw it, which was watching reels late at night. The copies described someone else's weekend. If you try the frame again, make it about the moment they're watching in.

## Momentum

| | Posts a month | Months with a 500K+ reel |
|---|---|---|
| 2025 | 27–34 (almost daily) | **9 of 12** |
| 2026 Jan–Jun | 13–19, some of them same-day batches | 2 of 6 |
| 2026 Jul–Oct | Mostly Trial Reels, then 2–4 a month | 0 of 4 |

**Your hits come from volume.** At about one a day you get a 500K+ reel most months. The scripts in [SCRIPTS.md](SCRIPTS.md) are built so you can film 15 of them in one morning and post one a day.

- **Post them as normal reels, one a day.** The 74 same-day batches and Trial Reels sat at about 2.6K views each and didn't build anything.
- **Use the CSV to check yourself.** If a reel holds under 60% at second 3, the first line or the first picture is the problem.

## The rules

1. **First line:** say who or what is in the way, and give permission, in one sentence with a picture in it. Add a time ("this weekend", "tonight", "this winter").
2. **Be doing it by second 3, outside.** Never film an advice reel in your room.
3. **Ask for 2–3 cheap things they could do this week,** one shot per noun.
4. **Keep it 8–15 s and under 45 words.** Stop on the button line.
5. **Be warm and in on it.** "Can we", "let's", "you deserve". Don't scold.
6. **One frame, one use.** Keep the rules, change the frame.
7. **When you can't film, post text on B-roll:** a full, specific sentence and a clip odd enough to stop on.
