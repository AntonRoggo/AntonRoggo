# "Do This" scripts: film 15 in one morning

Every script follows the rules in [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md):
- **The first line** names who or what is in the way and gives permission, with a picture in it and a time.
- **You're doing it, outside, by second 3,** with one shot for each thing you name.
- **Length:** 8–15 s, under 45 words.
- **The last line** is warm.

"Built on" says which winner each one copies the mechanics of, and why. None of them reuse a frame you've already used, except two marked **(2nd use)**.

## How to film the batch

Four stops, about 3 hours. The script numbers say what to shoot at each stop.

| Stop | Scripts | Bring |
|---|---|---|
| **Sea, 8–9am** | 2, 5, 9, 10, T3, T5 | Coat, towel, phone on a mini tripod |
| **Market and café, 9–11am** | 3, 6, 12, 13, A1–A3, T1 | Oysters from the market, a friend for the clink and laugh shots |
| **Walk and bench, 11am–1pm** | 1, 7, 8, 11, 14, 15 | Book, sandwich |
| **Golden hour / evening** | 4, plus the sunset shots for 1 and 12 | Croissant or ice cream for 4 |

For each script:
1. **Say the line to camera three times** at that stop.
2. **Shoot 3–5 B-roll clips of about 3 s each,** one for each thing the line names: hands, food, water, feet, the place.
3. **That's the whole reel.** No intro, no outro.

### Editing

- **First word at 0.0 s.** No breath, no "hey guys".
- **Cut to B-roll on the first thing you name** (around 2–3 s), then one shot for each noun, about 1–1.5 s each.
- **Keep your voice running** over the B-roll, and come back to your face for the last line if you have it.
- **Captions** in your current style: small, white, lowercase, middle of the frame.
- **End on the last line.** Leave a half-second of the final shot after it, then cut.

## Talk while doing it (15)

### 1. They stole an hour
**Line:** "The clocks went back, so we're taking the hour back. Lunch is outside now. Calls are walks. Coffee stays on the terrace until the sun goes down. Be cozy at night. The daylight is ours."
- **Length:** about 13 s, 35 words. Post this week, while the clock change is fresh.
- **Shots:** selfie on a sunny terrace at noon → sandwich on a bench → walking with the phone to your ear → espresso in the sun → sunset at 5.
- **Built on:** "Don't let your job stop you from acting unemployed" (899K). Name the thief, then three cheap ways to win it back.
- **Alt first line:** "It's dark at five now, which means lunch is the new golden hour."

### 2. Act like it's August
**Line:** "Don't let November stop you from acting like it's August. Swim before breakfast. Eat lunch in the sun. Walk home the long way, along the water. Wear the coat, keep the summer."
- **Length:** about 12 s, 32 words.
- **Shots:** selfie on the beach in a coat → coat drops, you run into the sea → terrace lunch → seafront walk.
- **Built on:** "Don't let your job stop you from acting unemployed" (899K): the "don't let X stop you from acting Y" flip, with a season for the time anchor.
- **Alt first line:** "Summer people quit in October. Be a winter swimmer."

### 3. Retired Italian grandpa
**Line:** "The goal this winter is to be as close to a retired Italian grandpa as possible. Espresso standing up. Two-hour lunch. Argue about tomatoes at the market. Evening walk, hands behind your back. Bed by ten."
- **Length:** about 13 s, 36 words.
- **Shots:** espresso at the bar → tomatoes at the market (talk to the seller) → long lunch table → walking with your hands behind your back (as in your 547K reel) → lamp off.
- **Built on:** "The goal this summer is to be as close to homeless as possible" (1.07M). An absurd identity, a season, then a list.
- **Alt first line:** "I'm retiring for the winter. Not from work. From rushing."

### 4. Let's all (one test of the reverse frame)
**Line:** "Let's all keep watching reels in bed with the brightness on max. Let's hit snooze four times, skip the sunrise because it's cold, and eat lunch at our desk again. Let's be exhausted for no reason. Who's with me?"
- **Length:** about 14 s, 39 words. **Post at 10–11pm** your audience's time.
- **Shots:** one take, walking outside at sunrise or golden hour, eating a croissant. The 18.6M one was also one take, walking, eating.
- **Built on:** "Let's all ruin our next day by staying up unreasonably late and scrolling reels tonight" (18.6M). The original described what the viewer was doing at that moment. Your seven copies described a weekend and averaged 44K. This one goes back to "right now, in bed, on reels".
- **Use this frame once,** then retire it.

### 5. Holidays are a habit
**Line:** "You don't need a holiday to feel like you're on holiday. You need to stop living like you're at work. Sit down for breakfast. Swim before emails. Take the scenic way. Holidays are a habit, not a place."
- **Length:** about 14 s, 38 words.
- **Shots:** selfie by the sea → breakfast at a table → jump in → coast road.
- **Built on:** "You don't need to feel better to go outside. You need to go outside to feel better" (584K). Flip the sentence, then give three moves.

### 6. My favorite cortisol supplement
**Line:** "My favorite cortisol supplement: a dozen oysters, a cold glass of white and two friends who don't check their phones. Take daily. Side effects include laughing too loud."
- **Length:** about 10 s, 28 words.
- **Shots:** selfie at the oyster bar holding one up → shucking → clink → friends laughing, phones face down.
- **Built on:** "My favorite biohack… grab a couple of your buddies, go to the café" (346K). Health jargon, social answer.
- **Ad version:** see A1.

### 7. Nobody's watching
**Line:** "I'm eating a dozen oysters on a bench at ten in the morning and not one person cares. Nobody is watching you. Swim in November. Dance badly. Talk to the old man at the bar. Do the thing."
- **Length:** about 14 s, 38 words. One take, in a busy spot. People walking past is the proof.
- **Shots:** the bench take → optional cuts to the swim, the bar.
- **Built on:** @thedavidsun "Nobody cares about what you do" (24.8M, 4,675×). He does the slightly odd thing in public while he says it.

### 8. Old people were right, part 2 (2nd use)
**Line:** "Old people were right again. Soup fixes most things. Walk after dinner. Call your mum on Sunday. Go to bed when it gets dark, not when your phone dies."
- **Length:** about 11 s, 29 words.
- **Shots:** soup → walking at dusk → phone call (a real one to your mum) → lights off.
- **Built on:** your "Old people were right" (190K on TikTok, your best there; 77K on Instagram). Label it part 2. It's a sequel, not a copy.

### 9. Winter Riviera-maxing
**Line:** "Winter Riviera-maxing. Swim at eight. Coffee in the sun. Do absolutely nothing until lunch."
- **Length:** about 7 s, 14 words.
- **Shots:** rocks → jump → café → lying on a bench, eyes closed.
- **Built on:** "Day-in-the-life-maxing. Wake up and go for a swim. Sit at the café and do absolutely nothing" (1.18M, 9 s). Three beats and no explanation. This is the winter version, from the same rocks.

### 10. Be a contradiction
**Line:** "Be a contradiction. Up at six, but never in a rush. Read old books, swim in cold water. Eat like a peasant, dress like a count."
- **Length:** about 10 s, 26 words.
- **Shots:** sunrise, slow coffee → book → sea → bread and sardines → you in a good coat.
- **Built on:** "You need to be paradoxical" (362K, 11 s). One pair per shot.

### 11. Five ways to waste a winter
**Line:** "Five ways to waste a winter. Hibernate. Scroll in bed till one. Skip the sea because it's cold. Eat lunch at your desk. Wait for spring to start living."
- **Length:** about 11 s, 29 words.
- **Shots:** walking selfie, with a text pop-up for each item. Or cut to you doing the opposite of each one (in the sea on "skip the sea").
- **Built on:** @kymalicore "10 ways to waste your 20s" (9.2M, 3,523×). A rapid list with a visual on each item.

### 12. Beat the Sunday scaries
**Line:** "The best way to beat the Sunday scaries is to have a Sunday so good Monday can't touch it. Swim. Market. Long lunch. Phone in the drawer. Asleep by ten."
- **Length:** about 11 s, 30 words. Post on a Sunday afternoon.
- **Shots:** sea → market → lunch table → phone going into a drawer → dark window.
- **Built on:** "The best way to live your life is to leave your house first thing in the morning…" (1.25M). That frame has only been used once.

### 13. A Frenchman who doesn't have to look cool
**Line:** "This is how a Frenchman would spend a Tuesday if he didn't have to look cool. Oysters at eleven. Nap at three. Pétanque until it's dark."
- **Length:** about 10 s, 26 words.
- **Shots:** oysters → bench nap → pétanque with the old guys.
- **Built on:** "This is how a European would eat if he wasn't a prissy" (397K). An identity tease, then three beats.

### 14. The trade (stitch)
**Line (after 2 s of a flex video, like a supercar or a Rolex unboxing):** "That watch costs more than my whole year. My year: swimming every morning, oysters on Fridays, friends who actually show up. I'd make that trade every time."
- **Length:** about 12 s with the stitch, 27 words.
- **Shots:** stitch → sea → oysters → table full of friends.
- **Built on:** @tatewright_'s stitch (29.3M, 2,090×), and your own "I want to get rich so I could buy a Rolex…" (108K). The contrast does the work.

### 15. This weekend's only plan
**Line:** "This weekend's only plan: find the warmest bench in town and don't leave it until the sun does. Bring a book, a sandwich and someone who talks less than you."
- **Length:** about 11 s, 30 words. Post on a Friday.
- **Shots:** selfie on the bench → book → sandwich → friend beside you, sun going down.
- **Built on:** "Nothing is stopping you from buying a watermelon and sitting in the park for six hours straight this weekend" (1.95M, 8 s). An absurdly simple plan, a long stretch of time, "this weekend". Same mechanics, new frame.

## Posting

- **One a day,** posted as a normal reel. Not a same-day batch, not a Trial Reel.
- **This batch** covers about three weeks: 15 talking, 7 text, 3 ads.
- **After a week, check the 3-second hold in Metricool.** Over 65% means the first line works; under 55% means swap in the alt first line next time.
- **Add each new reel to `data/reels_2025_2026.csv`** so the next round of this analysis includes it.
